## Overview

The Badlands is a country in [[Eldoria]] that was once filled with vibrant colors and teeming with life. Today it is a desolate wasteland — a desert-like expanse of dead forests, ruined cities, and harsh terrain. Average life expectancy is approximately 30–35 years. Nobody knows for certain what caused this.

## Description

The landscape resembles a land that was magically devastated — dead forests, ruined settlements that look like a once-thriving civilization was destroyed overnight, scattered tiny villages eking out survival. Magically infused beasts roam the wilds — literal abominations born from whatever destroyed the country.

Traders from [[Brittania]] visit every six months. Most Badlands residents don't believe Brittania exists despite this.

## History

[[The Great Decimation]] occurred approximately 100 years ago and reduced the country to its current state. The exact cause is unknown. Life in the Badlands is seen by much of the world as a lost cause.

## Culture

- Villages are primitive and deeply superstitious
- Folklore and oral storytelling are the primary means of passing knowledge; each village has its own account of what happened, and they contradict each other
- Most population is humans, tieflings, orcs, and goblins
- An illness known as **the Red Death** kills many
