## Overview

Brittania is a "utopian" country in [[Eldoria]] where political corruption runs rampant beneath the surface. It is currently ruled by [[Lucerin Atreides]], a child king who inherited the throne after a plot killed his entire family. Very diverse — humans are the largest population at approximately 10%.

## Description

A country of contrasts — presenting itself as a utopia while hiding deep corruption. The capital is the floating city [[Caer Brittanis]], where aristocrats, government officials, celebrities, and the wealthy live elevated above everyone else. The country is home to [[Strixhaven]], one of the most prestigious magical institutions in the world.

## History

A prosperous country with a long royal lineage. The Atreides family ruled for generations. Recently, a plot killed the entire royal family except for Lucerin, who had snuck out to [[Strixhaven]]. [[Oldric Ameris]] (Lucerin's uncle, the former Warmaster) gave up his claim to the throne. [[King's Crown]], the ruling council, now grooms the young king toward their own agenda.
