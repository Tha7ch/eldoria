## Overview

Frostgard is the snowy country of [[Eldoria]]. Its capital city is [[Winterhold]].

## Description

A land of snow, ice, and severe cold. The far northern reaches — the **[[Ycehelm-Thermguard]]** region — consist of snow peaks and hills, reaching temperatures of -40°C at the highest elevations. The region is too harsh for most human habitation and is home to creatures ranging from fluffy hares to white dragons. Ruins of old settlements lost to the cold dot the landscape, claimed back by ice over the past century as conditions have worsened. The only permanent human inhabitants of those heights are members of white and silver dragon tribes, who are nomadic and follow their dragon's movements.

The more habitable lowlands make up the rest of Frostgard. The country's dragon tribe population is a mix of Chromatic (white dragon, solitary, old ways) and Metallic (silver dragon, more diplomatic and integrated).

For detailed lore on dragon tribes, naming conventions, and inter-tribe relations, see [[Dragon Tribes]].
