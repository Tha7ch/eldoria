## Overview

Camelot, known as **the Kingdom of All Kingdoms**, was an ancient nation — by all accounts nearly a utopia. It no longer exists. Its last king, [[Arthur Atreides]], gave his life trying to protect Camelot from demons. After his death, the kingdom was torn apart by those who came to seize what remained of it. [[House Atreides]] — and through them, the royal line of [[Brittania]] — descends from Arthur Atreides and Camelot.

## Description

Described as beautiful, nearly a utopia — the standard against which "the Kingdom of All Kingdoms" name was earned. Nothing else is currently known of its geography, culture, or location relative to [[Eldoria]].

## Notable Residents

- [[Arthur Atreides]] — the last king of Camelot

## History

Camelot fell when [[Arthur Atreides]], its last king, died defending it from a demon assault — giving his life for the kingdom. In the aftermath, with no one left to hold it together, power-hungry opportunists descended on what remained and picked it apart.

*Selflessness killed the King. Selfishness killed the Kingdom.*

The surviving Atreides line went on to found [[House Atreides]] and, eventually, the royal dynasty of [[Brittania]].
