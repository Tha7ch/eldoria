## Overview

Elsweyr is the third country in [[Eldoria]]. Miles and miles of enchanted forest, home to animal-based races living in settlements similar to Ewok villages. It is the most magically dense area in all of Eldoria.

## Description

Vast enchanted forests, teeming with natural magic. Home to several druid groves — making it a significant center of druidic tradition in the world.
