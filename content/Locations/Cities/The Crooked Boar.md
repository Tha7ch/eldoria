## Overview

The Crooked Boar is a rough tavern in the lower criminal districts of [[Caer Brittanis]], in the streets beneath and adjacent to [[Black Lantern Row]]. It serves as the base of operations for trafficker [[Kane Orso]], who keeps a private room on the upper floor.

## Description

Accessed via a narrow alley leading to stairs that descend into a lower street where the lanterns burn red instead of yellow. A large boar skull, cracked down the middle, is nailed above the door.

Inside: packed with rough clientele, open weapons on tables, hoods worn indoors despite the warmth, cheap ale and music that sounds like three songs fighting each other. The noise fades in pieces when someone notable walks in — first the table nearest the door, then the card game beside it, then the bar. By the third step, the whole room is watching while pretending not to.

The upper floor has private rooms where operators conduct business, protected by guards posted at the landing.

## Notable Residents

- [[Kane Orso]] — people trafficker; operates from the upper room
