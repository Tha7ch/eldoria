## Overview

Lowtown is the slum district of [[Caer Brittanis]], located roughly 20–30 miles from the city proper. It is a place of poverty and neglect, largely ignored by the nobility and the crown.

## History

[[Lanius]] was born and raised in Lowtown before being adopted by [[House Howelle]] as a child. Years later, returning as an adult with the eyes of someone who had lived on both sides of the divide, the reality of Lowtown's conditions broke something in him. Standing in the rain, he tore the sigil of House Howelle from his tunic and silently vowed to change things. That moment became the foundation of [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]].
