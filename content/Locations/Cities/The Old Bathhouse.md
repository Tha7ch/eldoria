## Overview

The Old Bathhouse is [[Veyr]]'s base of operations at the far end of [[Black Lantern Row]] in [[Caer Brittanis]]. It presents on its surface as an exclusive private venue, but its true function is as the hub of Veyr's information-brokering network. The real business happens in the lower floor — a vast steam-filled bathing chamber accessible only via a hidden staircase.

## Description

Built from dark stone that looks older than the surrounding street. Steam curls from narrow vents along the roof. Red lanterns hang on either side of the carved entrance, which is painted black and gold. Two guards are stationed outside at all times.

**First floor:** Polished wooden floors, warm lantern light, private booths with thin curtains, low voices, servants carrying food, wine, and folded towels. Too quiet for its location. Nobody looks like they're relaxing. Every hand rests near something — a drink, a knife, a coin purse, a shoulder. Visitors who are recognized have their reputation arrive ahead of them; those who aren't recognized quickly figure it out from watching everyone else's reactions.

**Lower floor:** Accessed via a hidden panel in the back wall that opens to a stone staircase. The air grows warmer and wetter with each step down. At the bottom, two guards stand beside double doors — weapons must be surrendered before entering.

Beyond the doors: a wide, stone-pillared chamber filled with steam. Shallow pools and lanterns hanging from chains above. Water dripping steadily from a carved spout. This is where Veyr conducts all meetings of consequence — it is her territory completely, and she conducts it accordingly.
