## Overview

Black Lantern Row is a criminal district in the lower reaches of [[Caer Brittanis]] — the ground-level city that sits beneath the floating aristocratic tier. It is the organized heart of Caer Brittanis's underworld: private gambling clubs, underground markets, information brokers, and criminal organizations operate here openly. The kind of place where people don't hide what they are.

## Description

Narrow, wet streets packed with people who all seem to carry at least one weapon. Red lanterns hang from iron hooks above the road, their light dark and smoky. The deeper you walk, the richer the buildings become — not cleaner, not safer, just richer. Broken gambling halls and cheap taverns give way to private clubs with guarded doors, stone balconies, black curtains, and symbols carved above entrances that mean something to people who make money doing illegal things.

Nobody shouts here unless they want attention. Nobody pulls a weapon unless they're ready to use it. People watch from windows, rooftops, doorways, and corners, all pretending they aren't watching. Whispers travel faster than footsteps — a notable name moves ahead of its owner before they make ten steps into the Row.

## Notable Residents

- [[Veyr]] — information broker; runs her operation from [[The Old Bathhouse]] at the far end of the Row
