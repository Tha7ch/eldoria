## Overview

Caer Brittanis is the capital city of [[Brittania]] — a floating city elevated above the ground where aristocrats, government officials, celebrities, the wealthy, and nobles live, separated from the common population below. It is also [[Lanius]]'s childhood home; the lower city contains **Lowtown**, the slum district where he grew up before being adopted by [[House Howelle]].

## Description

The city exists on two tiers. The upper floating city is the face the kingdom presents to the world — noble estates, government buildings, academies, grand markets, and the gleaming architecture of wealth and power. Below it, tethered to or built on the ground beneath the floating platform, the lower city sprawls in the permanent shadow of the floating tier. Less glamorous, less regulated, and considerably more dangerous. The lower city is where common people live, where the working underclass keeps the upper city running, and where the city's criminal infrastructure operates openly.

**Lowtown**, part of the lower city, is defined by narrow streets, tattered clothes, broken rooftops, and the specific atmosphere of a place that has been abandoned by anyone with the power to change it. Rain doesn't drain properly. Shelter is improvised. Residents eye outsiders with the practiced wariness of people who have learned not to trust anyone who looks too comfortable. The conditions there are brutal — hunger is routine, safety is not guaranteed, and survival is the primary occupation of most residents.

Even in childhood, Lanius felt the way strangers here watched him after his adoption — waiting for a sign of weakness, because weakness cost you something real in Lowtown. It wasn't hostility. It was survival.

## Points of Interest

- [[Strixhaven]] — the only advanced magical institution in [[Brittania]]; has on-campus dormitories
- **Lowtown** — the slum district in the lower city; where Lanius was born and raised
- [[Black Lantern Row]] — criminal district in the lower city; heart of the city's organized underworld; private gambling clubs, underground markets, information brokers; characterized by red lanterns and a perpetual sense of being watched
- [[The Old Bathhouse]] — far end of Black Lantern Row; Veyr's base of operations; lower chambers with steam baths accessible via hidden staircase
- [[The Crooked Boar]] — rough tavern in the lower criminal districts adjacent to Black Lantern Row; Kane Orso's base of operations
