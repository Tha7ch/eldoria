## Overview

Strixhaven is the most prestigious magical institution in [[Brittania]] — the only advanced school for magic. It is very large and split into five individual colleges, each with a distinct philosophy and magical focus.

## The Five Colleges

### Lorehold
*School of History, Archaeology, Spirits, and the Past*

Ancient knowledge, forgotten civilizations, sacred relics, ruins, and ancestral magic. Students are historians, archaeologists, battlefield scholars, relic-hunters, and spirit-mediums. Magic involves memory, restoration, protection, radiant force, ancient oaths, and communion with spirits.
**Core belief:** The past is alive, and those who understand it can shape the future.

### Prismari
*School of Elemental Art, Performance, Creativity, and Advanced Magic*

The most artistically expressive and magically advanced college. Students treat magic as performance, emotion, and spectacle — fire, ice, lightning, wind, water, light, sound, and raw arcane force. Magic is bold, beautiful, and experimental.
**Core belief:** Magic is at its strongest when it is expressive, emotional, and unforgettable.

### Quandrix
*School of Mathematics, Research, Patterns, Nature, and Reality*

Logic, theory, natural patterns, and mathematical magic — symmetry, geometry, probability, ecosystems, time, space, and scale. Magic can alter size, manipulate movement, bend probability, reshape terrain, or reveal the structure behind magical phenomena.
**Core belief:** The world is built from patterns, and magic is the art of understanding and rewriting them.

### Silverquill
*School of Politics, Rhetoric, Ambition, Nobility, and Social Power*

Words, influence, debate, reputation, and aristocratic ambition. Orators, diplomats, poets, nobles, and political strategists. Magic tied to speech, writing, command, contracts, inspiration, intimidation, charm, and social dominance.
**Core belief:** Words are weapons, reputation is currency, and power belongs to those who can command a room.

### Witherbloom
*School of Nature, Life, Death, Decay, Growth, and Survival*

The full cycle of the natural world — birth, growth, disease, healing, death, decay, and renewal. Magic involves healing, poisons, herbalism, blood magic, decay, fertility, survival, and the transformation of death into new life.
**Core belief:** Nature is not gentle. It is beautiful, brutal, and endlessly renewing.

[[Cyris Voss]] runs a time magic research lab here and developed a spell capable of rewinding a small object ten seconds — a noted breakthrough.
