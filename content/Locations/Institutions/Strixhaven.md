## Overview

Strixhaven is the most prestigious magical institution in [[Brittania]] — the only advanced school for magic. Located in [[Caer Brittanis]], the capital. It is very large and split into five individual colleges, each with a distinct philosophy and magical focus. It has dormitories on campus — [[Cyris Voss]] currently lives in them.

## The Five Colleges

### Lorehold
*School of History, Adventuring, Finance, Offensive Magic, and Melee Combat*

History, archaeology, and the practical application of knowledge in the field. Students tend toward adventurers, mercenaries, battle-scholars, and those interested in the financial and logistical side of expeditions. Focuses on offensive spellcasting alongside melee combat — magic as a weapon, not a performance.
**Core belief:** Knowledge without the ability to act on it is worthless.

### Prismari
*School of Magic — Explosive, Dramatic, Powerful, and Ancient*

The school of magic in its most raw and overwhelming forms. Elemental force, ancient spells, dramatic large-scale casting, and the pursuit of pure magical power. Students are drawn to magic that is spectacular, difficult, and world-shaking. Ancient magic falls squarely within Prismari's domain.
**Core belief:** Magic should be felt. If no one noticed, you weren't trying hard enough.

### Quandrix
*School of Mathematics, Science, and Mechanical Magic*

Mathematics, engineering, theoretical magic, and mechanical systems. Students gravitate toward precise, calculated applications — artificers, researchers, enchanters of devices, and those who treat magic as a system to be understood and optimized rather than felt.
**Core belief:** Everything can be measured. Everything can be improved.

### Silverquill
*School of Politics, Debate, Etiquette, and Practical Magic*

Politics, social power, rhetoric, and the magic of everyday life. Practical spellcasting — utility cantrips, mage hand, prestidigitation, minor enchantments — taught alongside debate, etiquette, noble custom, and the art of wielding social influence. The school for those who want to rule rooms, not burn them down.
**Core belief:** The right word at the right moment is worth more than any spell.

### Vigorbloom
*School of Nature, Healing, Transformation, Modification, and Alchemy*

*(Formerly Witherbloom.)* Nature magic, healing, alchemical brewing, and transformation. Covers growth, restoration, and modification — making things larger, stronger, or fundamentally different. Potion brewing and alchemy are core disciplines. Students range from healers and herbalists to body-modifiers and experimental alchemists.
**Core belief:** Life is not fixed. Everything can be grown, changed, or made into something better.

## History

[[Lucerin Atreides]] was secretly visiting Strixhaven to watch a magic duel when the royal family was assassinated — his unauthorized absence is what saved his life.

[[Cyris Voss]] runs a time magic research lab here and developed a spell capable of rewinding a small object ten seconds — a noted breakthrough.

Strixhaven also has a **forbidden section** *(restricted archives — exact contents unknown)*. [[Merlin Morningstar]] showed it to [[Cyris Voss]]; [[Karsus Morningstar]] is aware and overlooks it.
