## Overview

Eldoria is the continent where the campaign takes place. It is divided into three countries, each vastly different in character. The world is medieval in era but advanced through the application of magic — innovation and technology exist, but are largely driven by arcane means rather than industry.

## The Three Countries

- [[Brittania]] — A self-proclaimed utopian country where political corruption runs rampant beneath the surface
- [[The Badlands]] — A country once filled with vibrant life, now a desolate wasteland following [[The Great Decimation]]
- [[Elsweyr]] — A vast enchanted forest country, the most magically dense region in Eldoria; more mystery than known fact

## World Details

- **Era:** Medieval, but with magical innovation and advancement
- **Adventurers** exist and are a recognized part of society
- **Magic** is widely known but proficiency varies enormously — most people know basics; advanced magic requires formal schooling
- [[Strixhaven]] is the only institution for advanced magical education
- **Magic tools and items** exist but must be crafted by artificers, making them largely limited to the upper classes
