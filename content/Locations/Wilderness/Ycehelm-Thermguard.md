## Overview

Ycehelm-Thermguard *(Y-ice-helm, Therm-guard)* is the far northern snow peaks region of [[Frostgard]]. Crested with hills and peaks of snow, it reaches temperatures of -40°C at its highest elevations — far too cold for ordinary human habitation. The region has grown harsher over the past ~100 years, and ruins of old settlements have been swallowed by the ice.

The only permanent human inhabitants are members of white and silver [[Dragon Tribes]] — nomadic people who follow their dragon's movements and build their camps wherever the dragon rests.

## Description

Snow peaks, harsh winds, and extreme cold. Home to creatures ranging from fluffy hares to white dragons. Littered with the ruins of old establishments lost to the worsening cold.

## Notable Residents

- [[Solstice Shuriv]] — grew up here as a member of the [[Unvy'Ezil]] white dragon tribe

## History

Conditions have worsened over the past ~100 years, destroying prior settlements and leaving the region to dragon tribes and wildlife. The ice cave at the base of the peaks was the resting place of **Ezil** after his great migration down from the heights — the beginning of the end for the Unvy'Ezil tribe.
