***It is the duty of the strong to protect the weak.***

Lanius had heard these words since the day [[House Howelle]] had adopted him, since he saw the girl his age with unforgettable white hair outside of his home in the slums of [[Caer Brittanis]]. It wasn't until he had returned to his childhood home and lost the lens of nostalgia that he realized just how out of place he was. The predatory stares of lowborn commoners staring at him, waiting for him to drop his guard so they could rob him for everything of value. It wasn't until that moment that Lanius realized that the people he had been taught were weak…weren't weak at all. They sat at the upper echelons of society tossing scraps and pennies down at the dregs of society acting like they were doing them favors. Bile and acid rose up his throat as he realized the real joke was on him, thinking that [[House Howelle]] had liberated him when instead they had imprisoned him in a cage of superficiality and shallow personalities. Staring up into the sky, Lanius could feel the humidity thicken as small droplets of rain began to fall on his face, but they brought no comfort with them. "Hurry inside Malaci, it's about to start raining and we haven't gotten the cover on the roof yet." Lanius looked over to see a boy and his mother running down the street in nothing but tattered clothes and rags, and again that same emotion he couldn't quite place began to flare again. Still staring into the sky, the rain's volume had increased leaving only him and the people unfortunate to not have any shelter. The dark crimson sky lit up just then with purple lightning and the crackle of thunder that followed was monstrous, and in that moment Lanius understood what he felt…***Anger.*** Walking through the slums that were Lowtown, his anger only grew as he realized that while he was lucky enough to "escape" so many had been left to die or suffer. "Why was I picked while so many others were left to just survive?" Survival…Lanius knew no better word than that, because that's all there was to do. Hopefully eat once a day and keep your life while trying. The rain did little to improve his mood or clear his thoughts as he wandered through the streets of Lowtown as his thoughts turned to [[House Dawnmere]]. The black sheep of the noble houses and yet so thoroughly loved by the commoners. Was trying to help those in need such a bad thing? Surely not, but Lanius had heard the whispers from other houses and had heard [[House Howelle]]'s own opinions of them. "It is the duty of the strong to protect the weak."
