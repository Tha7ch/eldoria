# Out of the Crystal

Darkness.

Not the darkness of a cave or a moonless night.

Something different than that.

A darkness so complete that Cal could not remember where it began.

Time did not exist there. There was no hunger. No pain. No dreams.

Only silence.

Then something changed.

A sound, faint at first. Like distant thunder beneath an ocean.

The darkness trembled, cracks spreading through it.

Light pierced the void.

Cal's eyes opened.

The first thing he saw was blue.

Shards of crystal surrounded him, glowing with cold light. They stretched above him like frozen lightning trapped in glass. The air felt strange against his skin.

Heavy, sharp, alive. It had been so long he forgot what that was like.

He took a breath and nearly choked.

His lungs burned.

His heart slammed once against his chest.

Then again, then faster.

The world rushed into him all at once.

Cold, Sound, Smell, Feeling, Life.

His legs nearly gave out as he stumbled forward. Crystal splintered around him, crashing against the stone floor.

For several moments he could only stand there, breathing. Trying to understand what had happened. Trying to remember.

Anything.

Nothing came.

Only fragments.

A castle in the distance, Golden banners, swords and shields, sunlight. All foggy in his mind.

Then nothing again.

A headache stabbed through his skull.

"Easy."

The voice froze him.

Cal spun.

A man stood several paces away.

Tall and Lean. Dressed in dark traveling clothes beneath a long coat. A wide-brimmed hat shadowed most of his face. One hand rested casually near his weapon. Not as a threat, but just as precaution. This new figure took him in, studying him.

The stranger's eyes were calm. Far too calm, like this was normal. Something not to be bothered by.

"Who are you?" Cal says

His voice sounded rough and unused.

The stranger smiled slightly.

"I was going to ask you the same thing."

Cal began to form a reply, but frowned.

The answer should have been simple.

His name. His family. His home.

Anything.

Instead there was only emptiness.

"I…"

Nothing.

The stranger's smile disappeared, not just amused but concerned.

"You don't remember."

It wasn't a question.

Cal hated that immediately. Hated how easily the man had seen through him.

"No."

The stranger nodded slowly.

"Interesting."

Cal's eyes narrowed.

"What is?"

"You."

That answer irritated him even more.

The man stepped closer. Not enough to threaten, just enough to get a better look. His gaze moved across Cal's clothing. The pendant hanging around his neck. The strange crystal prison behind him. Every detail. Nothing escaped his attention.

The stranger was quiet for a moment.

Not confused.

Thinking.

Evaluating.

Cal disliked that immediately.

It felt as though the man was weighing him against something only he could see.

Finally, the stranger spoke.

"How much do you remember?"

Cal frowned.

The question should have been easy.

Instead, his mind felt like a library after a fire, searching through scorched pages for just a sentence of information.

"My name."

The memory pierced him like an arrow.

"Cal."

The stranger nodded.

"And?"

Cal opened his mouth.

Nothing came out. A flash of sunlight. Steel. The feeling of wind against his face.

Then emptiness.

His jaw tightened.

"I don't know."

The stranger's expression didn't change. He just let Cal think.

"What year is it?" He finally asked.

The stranger answered.

The number meant nothing, cal didn't recognize it.

Didn't recognize the kingdom he mentioned afterward either.

Or the names of its rulers.

Nothing.

Every answer only proved how little he knew, how much he had lost, and with it a strange hollowness settled in his chest.

"You don't have to figure it out right now, give yourself some slack."

Cal laughed once, a short, humorless sound.

"I don't even know who I am." The reality of that sinking in as the words fell off his lips.

"No." The stranger agreed.

"But I think I know what kind of person you are."

That earned a look.

The man shrugged.

"You woke up confused, disoriented, and completely alone. First thing after that, I shattered your whole world. Flipped you on your head."

Cal narrows his eyes slightly at that, trying to understand his point.

"And despite all of that, you haven't looked scared to me once. And I consider myself pretty good at picking up on those sorts of things"

Hearing Cal's silence, he continued.

He pointed casually toward the tunnel behind him.

"Any other person would have run straight for the exit, given into hysteria immediately. Let that feeling take them over completely."

"I'm not any other person." Cal finally responds

"I know."

The man smiles.

"That's why you're interesting."

Something about him was infuriating, but Cal couldn't explain why. Maybe because he seemed to see things Cal himself couldn't. Maybe because he was so right about them.

"What do you want?"

The stranger was silent for a moment.

Then he extended a hand.

"My name is Roland."

Cal looked at it.

Roland's expression remained calm, patient.

Like he already knew what answer was coming.

Silence settled over the cave again.

Cal studied him in return.

The man looked ordinary enough. A traveler, a mercenary, maybe something else.

But there was an intelligence behind his eyes that made him difficult to read, every word felt carefully chosen. Every movement or expression was measured.

It was like he was already in cal's head, 3 steps ahead of the conversation.

"What do you want from me?" Cal asked.

He paused, considering this question.

"The world outside this cave is broken," Roland said finally.

"Corrupt nobles. Starving districts. People crushed beneath systems that were built to keep them powerless, and the ones on top fat with their labor."

Immediately there came a sense of revulsion. A furious, burning rage at that concept.

True though it was he had just woken up, a world like what he described was a foreign one to Cal. More than that, it was unacceptable.

Roland noticed the reaction. Of course he did.

"And judging by your face, I don't think you like that very much."

"No."

The answer came instantly, and Roland smiled.

"Neither do we."

"Who's we?"

Roland's smile widened, as if he had been waiting for that question.

"Come with me and find out."

Roland didn't rush him, didn't pressure him.

Just waited.

"You're not going to tell me where I am?"

"I will."

"Who you are?"

"I'll tell you that too."

"And if I refuse?"

Roland shrugged.

"Then you walk out of this cave and start telling strangers about your amnesia, see how long it takes them to call you insane."

A faint grin appeared beneath the shadow of his hat.

"I don't recommend that plan."

Despite himself, Cal felt the corner of his mouth twitch.

Roland saw it, unsurprisingly.

"The world outside is complicated," Roland said.

"But I think in your search for those memories of yours, you'll come to see the world just the same as we do. And whatever put you in that crystal, well maybe that's another step in making the world a better place. So let's find out together."

Cal looked back at the shattered remains behind him.

Cold blue light reflected across the stone.

Questions.

Nothing but questions.

No home, no memories, no direction, just a name.

Cal.

But when he looked back to consider Roland again, this didn't seem like such a bad place to start.

Roland was still waiting, not demanding or ordering. Just offering, person to person.

A path. For now, that would have to do.

"Alright," Cal said.

Roland smiled.

"Good."

Then he turned and started toward the tunnel.

Cal followed, emerging for the first time into a new world. A world of corruption, a world of suffering. A world of forgotten people. A world, that Cal would change.
