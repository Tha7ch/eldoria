## Overview

Three years before Campaign 2, [[House Dawnmere]] was massacred. Every member of the house was murdered in an organized attack. [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — then approximately 20 years old — was the sole survivor. The assassins who carried out the killings are dead. The people who ordered the murders are alive and unknown.

## Key Participants

- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — sole survivor (age 20)
- [[Auris Dawnmere]] — grandmother; very old, a bit senile, but loving
- [[Lirien Dawnmere]] — mother; former commoner, beloved by common people
- [[Sorin Dawnmere]] — father; head of house; gruff but soft-hearted
- [[Finn Dawnmere]] — younger brother, 16 at time of death; idolized Tirian
- [[Lyra Dawnmere]] — younger sister, 12 at time of death; adored by Tirian, treated like a princess
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — Finn's close friend (age 17); was away from [[Caer Brittanis]] when it happened; arrived approximately three days after the slaughter

## Causes

Unknown. The motive behind the slaughter has not been established. Possible angles: political rivals, a conspiracy involving [[King's Crown]], or something connected to House Dawnmere's specific legacy.

## What Happened

The house was attacked and all members killed. Tirian survived. The assassins responsible were subsequently killed — whether by Tirian himself or another party is unrecorded.

**The scene Tirian found:** The estate had been slaughtered. Every body bore stab wounds — indiscriminate, brutal, no hesitation and no mercy. The violence was not surgical. It was thorough.

The estate had been staged to look like a robbery gone wrong. Drawers pulled open, items displaced, surfaces disturbed. But Tirian's intelligence told him otherwise immediately: nothing of real value was taken. The things that would actually matter to thieves — certain jewelry, particular documents, items of genuine worth — were untouched or still present. Someone wanted it to look like opportunistic violence. It wasn't. It was targeted, planned, and executed by people who knew exactly what they were doing and needed cover for why they did it.

**Kaisel's arrival:** [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — Finn's close friend — was away from [[Caer Brittanis]] when the slaughter occurred, dealing with an unrelated matter. He returned approximately three days later. [[Roland Irvenest]] told him. He went to the Dawnmere estate and found [[Tirian Dawnmere]] present but visibly broken.

## Consequences

- [[House Dawnmere]] reduced to a single surviving heir
- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] now operates a dual life — public noble and secret vigilante Noctis — partly to hunt those responsible
- [[Gideon Atreides]] — close in age to Tirian and a good friend — helped Tirian through his grief in the aftermath; his murder in the royal family assassination three years later made that event deeply personal to Tirian
- Potential connection to the [[Assassination of the Royal Family]] ~3 years later *(unconfirmed — Tirian intends to investigate)*
