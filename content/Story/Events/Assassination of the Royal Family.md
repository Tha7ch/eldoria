## Overview

Approximately one month before Campaign 1 begins, the entire Brittanian royal family was murdered in an organized plot. [[Lucerin Atreides]] survived only because he had secretly slipped away to [[Strixhaven]] to watch a magic duel — nobody knew he had left. [[Myrcella Atreides|his mother]], his father [[Leo Atreides]], and all seven of his younger siblings were killed. [[Oldric Ameris]] (Lucerin's uncle, and Leo's brother) subsequently gave up his claim to the throne, passing it to Lucerin.

## Key Participants

- [[Leo Atreides]] — murdered
- [[Myrcella Atreides]] — murdered
- Lucerin's siblings (7) — all murdered
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — sole survivor, now king
- [[Oldric Ameris]] — Leo's brother; stepped aside from his claim to the throne, passing it to Lucerin
- [[King's Crown]] — primary suspects *(unconfirmed)*

## Causes

Unknown. [[Lucerin Atreides]] directly suspects [[King's Crown]], stating they may be "responsible for the death of my siblings and father." The timing and scale suggest an organized political coup.

## What Happened

The royal family was systematically killed while Lucerin was unknowingly away. [[Oldric Ameris]] stepped aside rather than take the throne. Lucerin was installed as king at age 10, with King's Crown positioned to guide him.

## Consequences

- Lucerin installed as king at age 10
- [[King's Crown]] gains significant influence over the crown
- Political instability across [[Brittania]]
- [[Lumiere Ameris]] is now heir to the throne if Lucerin dies

## Potential Connection

[[Tirian Dawnmere]] intends to investigate whether this assassination is connected to [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] three years prior. If both attacks were ordered by the same party, it would suggest a longer-running conspiracy targeting specific noble houses and the crown itself.
