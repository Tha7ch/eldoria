## Overview

The Doomed Timeline is the destroyed future that serves as the prologue to Campaign 1 (Session 1). In this future, [[Brittania]] has fallen, most of its people are dead, and the party — older versions of themselves — fights a last battle against [[Michael the Evangelist]] and ultimately [[Luciferus]] and his [[The Twelve Demon Generals|Demon Generals]]. The world ends with the arrival of [[Null]]. The present-day campaign is presumably an attempt to prevent this future.

## Causes

*(Unconfirmed)* The rise of [[Luciferus]] and the Demon Generals. Possibly related to [[King's Crown]]'s political machinations. The heroes tried for approximately 20 years to prevent this outcome.

## What Happened

The older party fought [[Michael the Evangelist]] at the ruins of Brittania's former capital. [[Kronos]] froze time and displaced party members. [[X]] nullified magic and transformed Kip into a tree. [[Luciferus]] arrived, erased Michael, and systematically destroyed the remaining party. [[Null]] appeared as the final force. The world went dark and everyone died.

## Consequences

The entire world ended. *(Unconfirmed: how the timeline was rewound to the present, and whether this is the only possible future.)*
