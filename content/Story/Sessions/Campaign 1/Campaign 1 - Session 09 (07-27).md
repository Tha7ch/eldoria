## Lowtown — The Wanderers

The opening scene is the slums of [[Caer Brittanis]] — Lowtown. Two unknown figures move through the streets, wary of how empty it looks.

**[[The Lone Wanderer]]** *(hooded man)*: "Where is everyone? It is unusual to see no one here."

**[[Hero With No Name]]** *(blonde)*: "I don't know. They say this is where he was last seen."

**The Lone Wanderer**: "Here of all places?"

**Hero With No Name**: "I guess."

---

## The King's Crown Chamber

[[Lucius Raymor]], [[Jeane Howelle]], and [[Kylo Wisemoon]] approach the party the next day and bring them to a chamber. Inside is the [[King's Crown]] — minus one member currently away.

**Present:** [[Manuel]] *(recently inducted, in place of someone unknown)*, [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]], [[Veritas Aletheia|The Grand Arbiter]], [[Oldric Ameris|The Warmaster]], [[Elric Fletcher|The Archmaester]].

[[Solstice Shuriv]] can tell they had just been arguing. The High Septon is breathing heavily.

[[Cyris Voss]] notices that [[Kylo Wisemoon]] is purposely staying out of the way and [[Lucius Raymor]] is sweating. [[Jeane Howelle]] almost looks like a kid in front of this group. He notices a slight resemblance between her and the High Septon.

[[Kip]] observes that they look important.

Solstice instinctively hisses. [[Aella Galefri]] scowls.

**Manuel**: "This was the group that aided in protecting the king."

**High Septon**: "Interesting, interesting. Say, elven girl — you look like someone I used to know. What's your name?"

**Aella**: "I don't feel the need to identify myself here."

**High Septon**: "Just a matter of formality is all. It's nice to meet you."

**Grand Arbiter**: "I believe that is the Galefri girl."

Aella rolls her eyes and sighs.

**Warmaster**: "Yes, that is Miss Galefri. And that is Cyris Voss — a promising student at Strixhaven. 

**Grand Arbiter**: What is your main magic, son?"

**Cyris**: "There are a few, but I guess water?"

**Grand Arbiter** *(squinting)*: "Checks out."

**Grand Arbiter**: "And you, goblin — who are you?"

**Aella**: "Hey, he has a name."

**Grand Arbiter**: "Shush, Aella."

**High Septon**: "Easy now, easy. These are renowned heroes — important to the realm. Need to treat them with a little respect."

The Grand Arbiter rolls his eyes. Kip stares at the ceiling and ignores the men talking at him.

A scale manifests in front of the Grand Arbiter, but it doesn't move in either direction.

**Warmaster**: "And you — who are you?" *(looking at Solstice)*

Solstice clicks her teeth at them and gives him a death glare, eyes slit.

**Warmaster**: "Very well. You don't have to answer to me, or anybody."

**Kip** *(to Solstice)*: "Hey, are these geezers talking to us?"

Solstice is still glaring at the Warmaster. The Grand Arbiter notices Solstice's grip on her bag.

**Grand Arbiter**: "What's in the bag?"

**High Septon**: "Well, before you start any of your scale shenanigans, I believe you should give the lady a chance to respond."

Solstice hisses at the Grand Arbiter.

**High Septon**: "We just wanted to get to know the heroes who saved the king. You are the future of Brittania. Such a shame what happened to his family."

It's hard to tell if the Septon is being sincere or sarcastic.

Kip, Solstice, and Cyris can see on Manuel's face that he does not trust the High Septon — he seems a bit angry. The Warmaster also looks a bit disgusted.

**Warmaster**: "Do not be so quick to speak about my family."

**High Septon**: "Simply expressing my sorrows at losing such great people. After all — was it not your son we offered the position of king to instead of the prince?"

**Warmaster**: "Not another word."

**Manuel** *(standing, looking between them)*: "Enough. We will not argue in front of our esteemed guests."

Solstice notices Jean looks meek right now.

**Kylo** *(stepping forward)*: "If I may —"

**High Septon**: "You may not. Go back to where you were, you glorified bodyguard."

**Manuel**: "Easy — he's one of mine."

**High Septon** *(sarcastically)*: "Then by all means, go ahead, *Ser* Kylo."

**Kylo**: "These four were instrumental to protecting the king. Solstice had the idea to put the king in the bag of holding."

**Warmaster** *(laughing)*: "You put my nephew in a bag of holding — ha!"

**Archmaester**: "Do any of you have any lingering wounds? Anything you need help with? I know it was a deeply traumatizing event."

Aella and Cyris quietly shake their heads.

**Kip**: "No thanks. Not anymore."

**Archmaester**: "Let me be the first to say it — I'm glad the four of you are okay. And I'm sorry about your companion."

**High Septon**: "Onto more important things. We have a replacement in mind. Aeron — would you please walk in?"

## Aeron Velmont

[[Aeron Velmont]] enters. Half-elf, approximately 20 years old, Brittanian. Long brown hair that covers his eyes a bit. A few scars on his face. Olive green eyes. High-quality tailored leather armor. Two sheathed swords on his back, pouches on his belt. 5'9. He looks pampered and noble — from [[House Velmont]].

Cyris has a look of recognition; he has heard the name Velmont.

**High Septon**: "This is Aeron from House Velmont. He has been assigned to your group by a personal recommendation from me. I spoke to Ludwig and he had no issue with it."

Aeron gives a noble bow.

**Aeron**: "It is a pleasure to be in the presence of such high-ranking people."

**Cyris**: "I mean no disrespect to you or Ludwig, but I think it is up to *the King* to decide."

Aella nods in agreement.

**High Septon**: "Primarily that would be the case. But as the king is occupied with other matters, we decided it would be best to make the replacement and inform the king when he's not so busy with all his affairs. After all, who would want to burden him during such tough times?"

Cyris narrows his eyes at him.

**High Septon**: "Is there a problem?"

**Cyris**: "You'll have to ask him later on."

**High Septon**: "Well, we *are* the king's ruling council."

Cyris looks surprised. Manuel gives him a look of understanding. The Warmaster tells Aeron to give his parents his regards. Manuel says they have places to be. The High Septon looks upset.

**Grand Arbiter** *(giggling)*: "I have no qualms with it. This meeting of the King's Crown is adjourned."

Cyris nods at the Archmaester as he leaves.

**Archmaester**: "Come visit me when you get the chance."

Cyris nods meekly.

As the Grand Arbiter walks by, Solstice growls at him. He looks down at her.

After the Arbiter, Septon, and Archmaester are gone, Aella lets out a big sigh.

**Warmaster** *(to Manuel)*: "I'd say you picked yourself an interesting group, Manuel. I think you picked good people to take care of my nephew."

The Warmaster explains: when he married into [[House Ameris]], he gave up his claim to the throne. When the royal family was killed, his son was offered the throne first — but when they heard Lucerin was alive, his son quickly declined, wishing not to take the throne while an Atreides still lived. He says House Ameris will be in their service. Oldric leaves.

**Manuel** *(sighing)*: "I hate that they made me a part of this group."

**Aella**: "It doesn't fit you."

**Manuel**: "I should be by the king, serving him."

**Cyris**: "This is serving the king in a way. Better you than someone else."

Manuel goes to Jean and gives her a small hug. He then dismisses Lucius and Kylo to return to the king.

**Manuel**: "Aeron — it is good to see you. I know we haven't talked much before. How are your parents doing?"

**Aeron**: "Father is chipper as ever."

**Manuel**: "How is your mother?"

**Aeron**: "Still deep in her research."

Cyris's ears perk up.

**Manuel**: "What does your mother research?"

**Aeron**: "Old ancient nature magic, magic artifacts — things like that."

**Manuel**: "Cyris, Kip — that may interest the two of you."

**Aella**: "Hey, I'm a spellcaster too."

**Manuel**: "I did not think those would be in your interest. I apologize."

**Aella**: "Where do you think I was for seven years?"

**Manuel**: "No one knew."

**Aella**: "I was in Elsweyr. That might explain it."

**Manuel**: "I went there once in my youth."

**Aella**: "Where to?"

**Manuel**: "Just the edge of it. We ventured in a little bit — this was when Leo was in his early twenties."

**Aella**: "Didn't get far, huh."

**Manuel**: "No. Leo wanted to, to be fair. When Leo was younger he was just looking for tales of glory and women to bed."

Aella chuckles.

**Manuel**: "Those were the days — him and Oldric."

**Aella**: "Well, Elsweyr is a beautiful place. It's a shame you couldn't see more of it."

**Manuel**: "I believe Oldric ventured further in his later years. You want to talk about someone carefree — that was Oldric at one point. His younger self wouldn't recognize the person he is now."

**Aella**: "How far in did he get?"

**Manuel**: "I think he reached the Cervan Kingdom."

Manuel introduces the full group to Aeron.

**Aeron**: "It's a pleasure to meet you all. I hope I'm not stepping on any toes — I know my joining was a bit forced."

**Aella**: "No need. It will be fine."

Cyris, Kip, and Aella all notice primal magic radiating from one of the fresh scars on Aeron's face — and not primal magic in a good way.

**Aella**: "The hell is that scar?"

**Aeron**: "Oh, this? I was out in the woods one night and I was attacked by a strange creature."

**Aella**: "Describe it if you can."

**Aeron**: "It was tall and lank — like a primal beast."

**Aella**: "How long ago did you get that scar?"

**Aeron**: "About three days ago."

**Aella**: "You should get that checked out."

**Aeron**: "I'm sure it's fine. It'll probably just heal over in a few days."

**Aella**: "Good luck with that."

**Aella**: "Are we getting out of here? Can we go?"

**Manuel**: "We were trying to find Damian."

**Aella**: "Is he not in the infirmary?"

**Manuel**: "No — he was refusing to stay there."

**Aella**: "How long was he supposed to be there?"

**Manuel**: "Quite a while."

**Aella**: "And it's been what — two weeks? He's going to kill himself."

**Manuel**: "I hope not."

**Aella**: "I'm not saying he's going to do it — but he's actively not helping himself."

**Manuel**: "I don't know what to say about him at the moment."

They all leave.

## The Rose Garden — Lucas Lyons

The party arrives in the rose garden. Jean and Lucius walk up. From the other side of the garden comes the sound of footsteps — Lucius turns around and lets out a sigh.

A blonde knight walks up between Jean and Lucius.

**[[Lucas Lyons]]**: "There you are, you old geezer. And here is the straight arrow and the lovely Lady Jean."

Jean rolls her eyes.

**Manuel**: "Everyone, this is Ser Lucas Lyons. The Lionheart — as he calls himself."

**Aella**: "Sounds pretentious."

**Lucas**: "You better remember that, geezer." *(to Manuel)*

Cyris giggles.

**Manuel**: "This is one of the ten strongest people in Eldoria."

**Solstice**: "Great."

Lucas throws his arm around Aeron. "Aeron! Still spending all your time in the woods."

Aeron is starstruck.

**Lucas**: "Geezer — who are these nobodies behind Aeron?"

**Manuel**: "They're not nobodies, Lucas. These people saved the king."

Solstice grumbles. Aella rolls her eyes.

Lucas kneels. "If you all saved the king, then it is an honor to meet the five of you."

**Manuel**: "He can be a bit overbearing, but he means well."

**Lucas**: "House Lyons is at your service if you ever need anything."

**Kip** *(confused)*: "The honor is all ours?"

**Solstice**: "I'm glad you bowed after being *corrected*."

**Lucas**: "A part of being a knight is admitting when you're wrong."

**Solstice**: "Part of being a knight is being chivalrous."

**Lucas**: "I am chivalrous! And arrogant."

**Lucas**: "Has the brute got here yet, or is he still on his way?" *(asking Manuel about [[Lumiere Ameris]])*

**Manuel**: "No, he has not. We were about to look for Damian."

**Lucas**: "The bastard?"

Manuel glares at him.

**Lucas**: "Oops. Sorry — I forgot the two of you are close."

**Lucas**: "Well, let's go." *(runs off ahead)*

## The Throne Room

Entering the throne room, the group hears little footsteps.

**Kip**: "Hey, Lucerin."

**[[Lucerin Atreides]]**: "Hi, Kip."

**Aella** *(ruffling his head)*: "Sup, little guy."

Solstice does the same.

Lucas sees Lucerin and immediately kneels, offering his sword.

**Lucerin**: "What is he doing?"

**Kip**: "I don't know — he looks stupid."

**Lucerin**: "Don't be mean to him."

**Kip**: "Just being honest."

**Lucerin** *(warmly)*: "Hi, Lucas!"

**Lucas**: "My sword is yours if you ever need it."

**Lucerin**: "Uh — don't you need that to fight?"

**Lucas**: "You're right, my king."

**Manuel**: "Lucerin, have you seen Damian anywhere?"

**Lucerin**: "No. I heard the maids say he goes into the crypts a lot."

Jean and Manuel exchange a look.

**Aella**: "What are the crypts?"

**Manuel**: "Where they buried the royal family."

**Aella**: "I see."

**Manuel**: "We need to go there and tell him to resume his duties."

**Aella**: "I can leave that to you."

**Manuel**: "No — we need everyone here for this."

**Cyris** *(sighing)*: "Why?"

They head toward the crypts. Lucas does not follow — he has left.

---

## The Crypts — Damian

Walking through the crypts as a group, everything goes dead quiet.

**Manuel**: "The reason we wanted to speak to you is because there is a gala to be held soon."

**Aella**: "Is it optional?"

**Manuel**: "No."

**Cyris**: "Why did you take us to a crypt to tell us about a gala?"

**Manuel**: "I don't need any other prying ears."

**Cyris**: "About a gala?"

**Manuel**: "The first thing about these gala announcements is that they are typically widespread. Before you entered the King's Crown chamber today, we were arguing about whether we should even have one. When maidens and servants and others hear about a gala it spreads like wildfire. With everything happening, we don't want additional talk."

**Aella**: "Then don't hold it."

**Manuel**: "This one is necessary."

**Aella**: "For what?"

**Manuel**: "To affirm Lucerin's place as king in front of the nobility."

**Aella**: "First a parade and now a gala. I'm sure nothing will go wrong."

**Manuel**: "I most certainly agree. But if we have it somewhere closed off, it is less likely."

**Aella**: "I still think it's a recipe for disaster."

**Manuel**: "It might be."

**Aella**: "And I have personal reasons for not wanting to attend."

**Manuel**: "I know you do."

**Aella**: "Please — if you know what's good for this country, don't hold this gala."

**Manuel**: "We were outvoted."

**Aella**: "Anyway — what do we need to know about this gala?"

**Manuel**: "Have any of you ever encountered a group called the People's Liberation Front of Brittania?"

He explains: they are a group that wants to topple the current state of nobility.

**Aella**: "Topple how?"

**Manuel**: "That much is not known yet. We've heard rumors from the slums."

**Kip**: "Why would they want to do that?"

**Manuel**: "Have you seen the state of Brittania?"

**Kip**: "Not much."

**Manuel**: "You've seen the nice parts. But a large part of Brittania is poverty-stricken smaller towns."

**Aella**: "Basically, Kip — it's a shithole."

**Manuel**: "Some houses have tried to help them. [[House Dawnmere]] was the best of that. Now they are survived by only their remaining son."

Aella looks shocked.

**Aella**: "I'm sorry? House Dawnmere — what?"

**Manuel**: "They were assassinated one night."

**Aella**: "Oh wow."

**Manuel**: "An engagement party for two people. Tirian was there, and while he was there, the rest of his family was slaughtered."

**Aella**: "That's terrible."

Lucius grimaces. Jean goes completely still.

**Aella**: "I always liked House Dawnmere."

**Manuel**: "They were good people. Their loss has been heavily apparent. I don't know if their son ever recovered from it."

**Aella**: "I can imagine not. Nobody knows who did it?"

**Manuel**: "No. There are theories, but nobody knows."

**Aella**: "Anything else we should know about this gala?"

**Manuel**: "A couple of things. All ten of the strongest people in Eldoria will be there."

**Aella**: "I'll need a list."

**Solstice**: "More pretentious people."

**Manuel**: "For the most part they are all vastly different."

**Aella**: "What's a name I would know?"

**Manuel**: "Probably Lanius Howell, Lumiere, Lucas, Princess Orelia..."

He continues: all of House Ameris, Raymor, St. James, Galefri — nearly all of the noble families.

**Aella**: "All of them?"

**Kip**: "Doesn't seem like my kind of place."

**Cyris**: "Likewise."

**Manuel**: "I'm sure you'll find some trouble to get up to."

---

The group walks deeper and comes to a halt. [[Damian Grimm]] is asleep on the floor between two crypts — [[Elio Atreides]] and [[Iris Atreides]], both age 4. Two of the royal children buried after the [[Assassination of the Royal Family]].

Suffocating silence between Manuel, Jean, and Lucius.

Manuel walks over to Damian and picks him up as you would a sleeping child.

**Manuel**: "Come on, son. It's time to go."
