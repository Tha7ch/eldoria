# Campaign 1 - Session 04

*The party shops for equipment, learns more about Ludwig and Damian, and encounters assassins on the rooftop.*

---

[[Lucius Raymor]] took the party to an inn. They wake up the next morning.

[[Ludwig St. James]] walks in.

**Ludwig**: "Pleased to announce your rooms at the guild hall are now ready for you."

[[Kieran Arden]] is sleeping in.

Ludwig takes the party to a store.

---

# The Shop

The shopkeeper studies Cyris for a moment.

**Shopkeeper**: "You're a time wizard, aren't you?"

**Cyris**: "How did you—"

**Shopkeeper**: "I'm a good judge of character."

## Items Purchased

- **[[Cyris Voss]]** — Chronolometer (1250 gold), Clockwork Amulet (150 gold), Failed Experiment Wand (150 gold)
- **[[Solstice Shuriv]]** — Snake Staff
- **[[Seros Ashwalker]]** — Claws
- **[[Aella Galefri]]** — Spear, Crystal

---

# Deck of Many Things

A Deck of Many Things is present in the shop. [[Seros Ashwalker]] grabs a card — then grabs two more.

- **Card 1:** Blinked Seros out of existence entirely.
- **Card 2:** Undid the effect of the first card, saving his life.
- **Card 3:** A minor temporary buff. *(Exact effect unrecorded.)*

[[Cyris Voss]], with his time magic, was the only one who saw Seros briefly vanish from existence. It spooked him — he did not draw a card himself.

---

# Conversation — Ludwig and Damian

[[Lucius Raymor]] is from the [[Church of Light]] and has a brief conversation with [[Aella Galefri]]. They clearly know each other from childhood — both are chosen figures of gods deeply ingrained in their respective churches, though Aella was banished from hers years ago.

Ludwig mentions that Lucius has been charmed by some Elven students at [[Strixhaven]].

**Ludwig**: *(on himself)* "I took a knight's oath at 14 years old."

**Ludwig**: *(on Damian)* "Damian is a sensitive kid. He used to love a maiden of the queen, but that girl was married off."

---

# Rooftop Assassins

The party eventually leaves the store. While traveling:

Aella feels a warm sensation dripping down her face — blood. A dagger grazed her cheek. A crossbow bolt whizzes between Kip and Seros's heads.

Cyris shoots two water bullets that knock an assassin over — he falls into crates below.

Aella swings down on the assassin with a lightning spear.

Kip stabs the assassin and knocks him out.

Seros and Solstice attack the other assassin. The assassin hits Seros three times and throws him off the roof. Seros is knocked unconscious.

Kip uses a slingshot to hit the assassin in the head with a rock.

Cyris's Steel Defender jumps from the roof and lands on the assassin, nearly knocking him out.

Cyris holds a gun to the assassin's head.

Ludwig kills one of the assassins and drags the other one off.
