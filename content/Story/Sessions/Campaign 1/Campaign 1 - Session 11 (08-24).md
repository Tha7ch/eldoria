## The King's Crown Gala — Dinner

The party heads to the gala. It starts with people seated at tables of people they are familiar with, having a nice dinner.

- [[Cyris Voss]] is with the Morningstar family
- [[Ludwig St. James]] and [[Lucas Lyons]] at a table
- [[Seros Ashwalker|Seros]], [[Aella Galefri]], [[Solstice Shuriv]], [[Kip]], and [[Aeron Velmont]] at a table
- [[Veritas Aletheia|Grand Arbiter]], [[Karsus Morningstar|Archmaester]], and [[Tirian Dawnmere]] at a table
- Amara and Rhys Velmont at a table
- [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]], Elizabeth Howelle (grandmother), and Lady [[Jeane Howelle|Jean]] at a table
- [[Oldric Ameris]], [[Lumiere Ameris]], [[Raven Whitlock]], and Samara (Raven's mother) at a table

[[Lucerin Atreides]] walks in with the Kingsguard. He pulls out a paper.

**Lucerin**: "First and foremost, thank you all for coming."

He crumples the paper.

**Lucerin**: "I don't feel like reading words like this. We are at a turning point in history. I am 10 years old and I am the king, but I can tell you all this: I want nothing more than to serve this country with every ounce of my body like my father and his father before me.

There are many that would challenge my claim to the throne because of my age. I'll admit a 10-year-old at the throne seems stupid, but I am taking the steps necessary to lead this country into a prosperous age. I want to create a society where everyone is free, equal, and I want to honor my family in doing so. Sorry, I'm like really nervous.

We are at the golden age of Brittania. We have many, many bright and amazing people. I have seen the distance people are willing to go to not only help me, but help Brittania and to help those who cannot help themselves. They too have inspired me to not just sit here but to overcome it, the way the Atreides have always done, the way my brother would have done. I have to carry their wills for a better Brittania and add that to my own will to become a king worthy of this crown. I know for some it is very hard to place your faith in a ten-year-old, while I do understand, I will not agree with those who cannot see I am trying to lead Brittania into a better tomorrow. I thank every one of you for being here, and let's go dance, I guess!"

Roars of applause.

## Seros & Lady Jean

[[Jeane Howelle|Lady Jean]] walks out to the dance floor, but no one is dancing with her.

[[Seros Ashwalker|Seros]] pounds his drink and walks up to Lady Jean. She looks him up and down, studying him.

[[Cyris Voss]] and [[Merlin Morningstar]] giggle to each other.

Seros puts an arm on her shoulder.

**Seros**: "Hey."
Jean looks over at his hand.
**Jean**: "I see the alcohol has made you bolder tonight, Seros."
**Seros**: "You could say something like that."
**Jean**: "I take it you're going to ask me for a dance."
**Seros**: "Only if you wanna," he says with an inquisitive look.

Jean looks around the room, looking for someone.

**Seros**: "Is everything alright?"
**Jean**: "Everything's fine. Yes, I'll indulge you, Seros."

Seros looks back at the party and gives a thumbs up with a big smile. The party is shocked. Seros steps on her feet a little, but it isn't a complete disaster.
