# Campaign 1 - Session 03

*The party arrives at the guild hall and meets Seros. The Kingsguard arrives with their first assignment.*

---

Half hour journey to [[Honest Hearts]] hall. Big courtyard, big building. Doesn't seem too busy — usually 50–100 people, but Ludwig says a lot are probably on quests because it's early in the month.

We walk into the guild hall. A few people scattered throughout at tables. We recognize [[Jeane Howelle]] and [[Kylo Wisemoon]].

[[Seros Ashwalker]] is alone at a table, nodding off.

**Kip**: "He looks pretty cool."

**Aella** *(blows a gust of wind at him)*

Seros hits his head as he wakes up.

**Seros**: "What the hell!"

**Ludwig**: "Seros, I'd like you to meet your new companions."

**Seros**: "Nice to meet y'all, I'm Seros."

**Kip**: "I'm Kip."

**Seros**: "You seem annoying, but nice to meet you."

**Kip**: "Annoying!? You're probably right."

Aella shocks his hand as they shake it in greeting.

**Seros**: "You six were specifically chosen by the king."

**Seros**: "To do what?"

**Solstice**: "Wish we knew."

**Seros**: "They seem promising."

---

**Ludwig**: "Do you remember High Priestess [[Vera Justicar]], of the [[Church of Love]]?"

**Aella**: "Not really."

**Ludwig**: "You should pay her a visit."

**Aella**: "For what?"

**Ludwig**: "You'd be interested in hearing what she said. She could tell you about your family. Nothing bad."

**Aella**: "Nothing bad?"

**Ludwig**: "Nothing bad for the foreseeable future."

**Aella**: "...Please."

---

# Introductions

Ludwig is heir to [[House St. James]], heir to the guild.

Cyris nervously introduces himself the way a student would — name, major.

Aella asks a bit about the guild.

**Kip**: "It sounds boring."

Cyris launches ink up at the ceiling.

**Cyris**: "Is that interesting?"

Solstice doesn't want to share. She is in a dark black bodysuit that covers all skin except her neck.

Kieran is from [[Brittania]], his family ran an apothecary, his dad was an investigator, he has a brother in the military.

---

Cyris locks eyes with [[Kylo Wisemoon]] across the room.

**Ludwig**: "You know him?"

**Cyris**: "No, but he seems to know a lot about me."

**Ludwig**: "You must be interesting — what magic do you use?"

**Cyris**: "I'm studying a few right now."

**Ludwig**: "Good way to dodge my question. If you need a leg up at Strixhaven, ask him — he's still close with the professors."

---

Ludwig tries to tease Kieran about Damian — asking if Damian is his lover.

**Kip**: "This is boring, where'd you get your scar from?"

**Kip**: "What's wrong, it looks cool, I want to know where it came from."

**Kieran**: "I can't answer that."

**Kip**: "You don't know?"

**Ludwig**: "Let's change subject."

---

Aella reluctantly shares that she grew up in the [[Church of the Storm]] and has lived in [[Elsweyr]] for the last several years.

Kip mentions he's from [[Petalton]] — a big village in [[Elsweyr]], actually it's really small.

**Aella**: "What part?"

**Kip**: "I don't know, I'm bad with places. Maybe south?"

**Aella**: "I mostly stayed north."

**Kip**: "You're from Elsweyr too?"

**Aella**: "Not from, but spent time near the coast."

---

Seros grew up in [[The Badlands]] and was a criminal.

**Kip**: "What'd you do?"

**Seros**: "We ran a gang. Robbed, stole, killed. We broke up because one of my friends got locked up."

Cyris gives him a glare before looking away.

**Kip**: "So you killed innocent people."

**Seros**: "I used to. Not anymore."

**Kip**: "Do you still steal?"

**Seros**: "Not anymore — were you listening at all?"

**Kip**: "A little bit."

**Solstice**: "He's a child, they never listen."

---

**Seros**: "So wasn't it the troll's turn?"

**Kip**: "There's not a troll here."

**Seros**: "You."

**Kip**: "You were calling me a troll? I look nothing like a troll. That's dumb. Have you ever seen a troll before?"

**Seros**: "You're green."

**Kip**: "A lot of people are green — goblins, orcs, elves, ogres."

**Seros**: "Oh my god, introduce yourself."

**Kip**: "Kip. I'm 14, from Petalton. I like animals, nature, stealing — especially from the rich."

---

# Assignment

The Kingsguard arrives. Ludwig walks off.

**Kip**: "Hey big gramps, what's up?" *(to Manuel)*

**Kip**: "Kind of boring here."

Manuel gives the party bags of 1000 gold each.

**Aella**: "The hell is this for?"

**Manuel**: "It is a rather serious assignment. In two days there will be a royal parade through the streets of [[Brittania]]. The Kingsguard will be protecting the king — we want additional people watching from above, for any rooftop assassins."

**Cyris**: "A parade seems like a stupid idea. A bad way to introduce 'the entire royal family is dead.'"

**Kip**: "But we get to see a parade!"

**Cyris**: "The [[King's Crown]] is either stupid or trying to sabotage you."

Aella and Cyris notice the guards all look at each other after Cyris says this.

**Lucerin**: "They won't say it, so I will. There is reason to believe the Crown is trying to sabotage the king. I also think they may be responsible for the death of my siblings and father."

**Lucerin**: "[[House St. James]], Ameris, [[House Howelle]] — somewhat trustworthy."

Cyris and Aella recognize the houses. [[House Ameris]] is known as a military house, known for lunar magic.

*The Battle of the Blackwood — [[Lumiere Ameris]], 40,000 vs 100,000, against a country of orcs. Active in the last 3 years, 13–0 in military campaigns.* — Cyris and Aella both recalling, as people from Brittania familiar with the battle.

**Lucius**: "You go to Strixhaven, right?"
