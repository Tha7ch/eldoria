# Campaign 1 - Session 07 (05-31)

*The royal parade is attacked. Hannibal reveals himself. Kieran is nearly killed. Kylo drops the biggest lore of the campaign.*

---

# The Parade

Rooftop team: [[Seros Ashwalker]], [[Cyris Voss]], [[Aella Galefri]], and [[Kylo Wisemoon]].

They overlook thousands of commoners. [[Lucerin Atreides]] is on a float, surrounded by the Kingsguard: [[Lucius Raymor]], [[Jeane Howelle]], [[Damian Grimm]], [[Manuel]].

Lucerin stands up and is about to speak.

**Explosions.** An attack begins.

Seros gets blown off the building. Aella uses her spear and casts Feather Fall on him — he doesn't take the full fall damage.

**Seros**: "Thanks, Aella."

Three grappling hooks latch onto the roof as assassins climb up.

**Aella**: "You got my back, I got yours."

**Cyris**: "Course."

**Aella** *(pointing at an assassin)*: "You look like the perfect lightning rod right now." *(booming voice)*

Manuel slices and dices an assassin. Manuel slams another into the ground — sinking him six feet under.

Falling debris hits [[Kip]], blasting him into an alleyway. He dodged most of the big pieces — insane instincts — but was still hurt.

[[Kieran Arden]] gets hit by rubble and knocked toward an assassin. [[Solstice Shuriv]] gets hit by a bunch of rubble.

**Jeane**: "All okay?"

They say they're alright but there are assassins. Lucius and Jean fight them.

Cyris uses his wand to ready a water orb, shoots it at an assassin — drenches him, deals significant damage — then launches a massive **blue** (not yellow) lightning bolt that electrocutes and kills the assassin.

**Cyris** *(turning to Aella)*: "Am I doing this right?"

**Aella** *(laughing)*: "Amazing!"

Cyris and Aella suddenly feel sweaty — a giant fireball is hovering over the building Kylo is on. All assassins nearby are obliterated, reduced to bones. Kylo then throws a Witch Bolt at the last assassin on Cyris and Aella's rooftop, killing him.

Seros shoots a magical bullet from his finger up at an assassin fighting [[Ludwig St. James]] on a rooftop, catching the assassin off guard and blasting his head off.

**Seros**: "You alright, Ludwig?"

**Ludwig**: "I don't know what's going on with me. Thanks for the save."

**Seros**: "No problem, bro."

# Solstice Gets Creative

Solstice runs to protect Lucerin.

**Lucerin**: "You want to put me in a bag?" *(confused)*

**Solstice**: "It's only ten minutes."

**Lucerin**: "I should ask my brother."

**Solstice**: "We don't have time. I'll bring you to him."

**Lucerin**: "Okay."

**Solstice**: "Oh — and ignore what's in the bag."

She puts Lucerin into her bag of holding. *(Note: something else was already in the bag.)*

Solstice sends a message to the party: *"I have the king in my bag."*

**Aella**: "You have what?"

**Seros**: "How do you have the king in your bag?"

**Solstice**: "Who should I bring him to?"

Manuel, Lucius, and Jean come to escort her.

**Manuel**: "We will speak on this another time. For now, come with us."

---

# Ground Fight

A Warden appears beside [[Kieran Arden]]. [[Damian Grimm]] shows up to save him.

Aella dodges both swings from an assassin on her rooftop. Activates her sorcery — launches him backward with wind, leaving him hanging on the ledge. She shocks his hands, causing him to fall. He snaps his neck on landing.

Kip tries to summon a beast — gets counterspelled. Transforms into a jaguar instead.

Kieran activates Divine Favor and glows golden. Lands one attack on the Warden, the other is parried.

---

# Cyris and Aella Combine

Cyris and Aella attack an assassin from range in combination: Cyris's water bullet, electrified by Aella's Witch Bolt. 55 damage.

**Aella**: "Quiet now. The storm is speaking."

Damian creates a tree of blood that sprouts from the ground and kills an assassin.

Kip fights an assassin and struggles. He's stabbed — coughing up blood. Visibly scared; he has never felt this kind of fear. He tries to heal himself — counterspelled. Uses a healing potion instead. Goes to run.

---

# Hannibal

A knife cuts through the fabric of reality. A man steps out.

Kip freezes, terrified.

The man walks past Kip. Points a thumb down at the assassin fighting Kip — the assassin drops dead.

Kip takes frost damage. The man is making the entire area cold.

Kip follows him. The man teleports — taking the **Warden** and **Kieran** with him to a rooftop. They are trapped together in a **translucent purple cube**.

**Kip**: "Damian, hey look!"

**Damian**: "Damnit."

Damian forms a spear of blood and throws it at the cube — it bounces straight back and impales Damian.

Aella and Cyris investigate the cube and take psychic backlash. They can tell it is **not a man-made spell**.

Kip snaps the spear that impaled Damian.

Damian tears his sleeve open. Grabs a short sword.

**Damian**: "Do or die."

Damian cuts into his wrist and up his arm — blood gushes out. A spear twice as thick begins to form from the blood.

**Damian**: "I'm not losing anyone else."

He throws the spear as hard as he can. When it hits the cube it shatters — then remakes itself and launches back at Damian, blasting him backward and destroying a building.

Inside the cube, the cold man speaks.

**Hannibal**: "It's very nice to meet you, Kieran. You and your friends have caused us some trouble."

**Kieran**: "I take it you're the leader?"

**Hannibal**: "I'm no assassin. I'm here to usher in a new era."

**Kieran**: "You can keep your new era — no one's buying it."

**Hannibal**: "It's my gift to the world. No one needs to buy it."

**Kieran**: "Well, as you can see, we're not going to let it happen."

**Hannibal**: "You don't have a choice. The thing about you hero types is you always talk big — but when it comes down to it, you're not anything special."

**Kieran**: "Same could be said about villain types."

**Hannibal**: "No. I *am* special. Warden — kill him."

The Warden grows to **eight feet tall**. He swings a flail, hitting Kieran twice for significant damage, knocking him down.

**Cyris**: "Kylo, you have any solution for this barrier?"

**Kylo**: "Trying to figure out the magic."

**Aella**: "Hurry the fuck up, we don't have time."

The Warden lifts Kieran up by the neck. Punches him — knocks his teeth out.

Aella launches a Witch Bolt at the barrier — it bounces back and hits her.

---

# The Wish Spell — and What It Costs

We hear Kip speaking in an unknown language.

Aella has heard priests speak this before — it is **the language of the Gods, the tongue of the Gods**. She can make out one word: **"Wish."**

Cyris recognizes what Kylo is doing: the **Wish spell**.

Hannibal turns and motions a *"shh."*

Cyris gets a **flashback** — a time and place he doesn't recognize. It's the battle between the adult party and Demon [[X]]. The demon makes his blood run cold.

Kip tries to cast a spell — the same "shh" snuffs it out, like restricting his throat.

Kylo gets launched backward.

Seros and Kip move to help Kylo.

**Hannibal** *(to Seros)*: "You're extremely disappointing in this universe."

**Hannibal** *(to Kip)*: "Kip, you're actually alive in this one. Don't worry — after this I'll be sure to pay your family a visit."

Aella talks shit and demands he give Kieran back.

**Hannibal**: "I'll give him back after I kill him."

Kip transforms into a **miniature dragon**.

**Kip** *(to Seros)*: "Don't ask questions, just get on."

**Hannibal**: "It seems there is a lot to enjoy here."

**Kip**: "You won't hurt him and you won't hurt my family."

**Hannibal**: "I've killed so many variants of my family that I've lost count."

**Aella** *(to Kip)*: "He's trying to distract you, don't let him get in your head."

**Hannibal**: "What's her name? Nixi?"

Kip **winces**. Kip slams into the top of the cube — gets launched off into a building.

**Hannibal**: "Well, Warden — do what you have to do."

Aella gets down on her knees and begins praying.

She mutters a prayer to [[Veyrana]] for Kieran's protection. It starts pouring rain in the area.

**Hannibal**: "Your religion doesn't do you any good, girl."

**Aella**: "I know this is just a futile attempt to pray for my friend's safety." *(locked in, unbroken)*

Inside the cube, Kieran gets slammed into the rooftop — coughing up blood. The pain is the only thing keeping him conscious.

**Hannibal**: "Any last words, hero? Oh — I forgot. You can't even talk, can you?"

The Warden slams Kieran into the ground. An audible **crack**.

**Hannibal**: "I'm over the limit."

Hannibal opens a gap in the barrier and tosses Kieran's body out.

Seros and Kip rush over. Kip **pukes** upon seeing Kieran's mangled body up close.

**Hannibal**: "Getting rid of you lot is my goal as well — a secondary, more menial task than the king."

**Kip** *(crying)*: "—"

**Hannibal**: "Gosh, your voice is entirely grating."

Hannibal and the Warden disappear.

# Aftermath — Kylo's Lore Drop

Cyris and Aella rush to where Kylo is, trying to investigate the magic residue.

**Kylo**: "Don't. Don't try to figure out what it was — for your own sake."

Seros argues that Kylo should have done more.

Cyris and Aella explain that Kylo's Wish spell would have worked if he'd gotten it off.

Kylo pulls up his sleeve — his right arm is **almost skeletal**.

Cyris winces.

**Kylo**: "When you use the power of the Gods as a mortal, it does not — it does not—" *(wipes blood from his nose)* "It doesn't matter how strong you are, it takes everything out of you. I won't be the same for half a year."

Seros can see that Kylo risked everything. He is exhausted and physically extremely weak.

Aella and Cyris move to investigate the magic residue. They hear something like **screaming**. Kylo quickly grabs them both by the collar and pulls them back.

**Kylo**: "That... is the Abyss. It's what most people call Hell."

**Aella/Cyris**: "So was that a demon?"

**Kylo**: "I don't know what he is."

**Kylo**: "You all know about the Celestial Realm — where the Gods, their angels, and whoever else is. Everything has to have its inverse. The Abyss is the reverse of the Celestial Realm."

**Kylo**: "When the Celestials tried to make life for the mortal plane, they made something — or someone — that proved the existence of imperfection. They wanted a universe where everything was perfect. In their attempts to reach perfection, they made something that scared them — or that they couldn't accept. So they cast it into the Abyss. What came of this pure energy being cast into the Abyss was life. I'm sure the four of you have at least heard of the demon generals once in your life."

Cyris nods grimly.

**Kylo**: "The twelve demon generals that are supposed to be folktales — one of which is the source of your power, Cyris. One of them is directly responsible for Damian."

**Cyris**: "All the deviant magics."

**Kylo**: "Gotta love the twelve forbidden magics."

**Cyris**: "What a specific number." *(noticing the connection)*

# Kieran — and the Fallout

**Kylo**: "Did your friend have any family?"

**Aella/Cyris**: "He has a brother in the military, and he mentioned his dad was an investigator."

**Kylo**: "I'll have someone get in touch with them."

Cyris looks down solemnly.

**Kylo**: "I know it doesn't mean much, but I am sorry about what happened to your friend. We may not have known him well, but we were all brought together by the king. If this can happen to him, it can happen to anyone."

Kylo questions whether Kip should even be kept around — if it's safe.

**Aella**: "The little one is strong. Kip — what do you want to do?"

**Kip** *(looking up)*: "I can't go home. Not to my family."

**Kylo**: "You can't stick around here, kid. Way too dangerous."

Kip looks up worried and scared.

**Kip**: "That man — he knew my sister's name. How does he know that?"

**Kylo**: "No clue. I don't have any idea about any of this."

**Kip**: "I can't go home if someone like that is trying to hurt my family. I have to hunt them down."

**Kylo**: "This is the price you pay for trying to take someone like that down. For fuck's sake — you're just a kid."

**Kip**: "Well so is she. Of course I can handle it. I'll pay the price."

**Kylo**: "You're just a kid. That's not your price to pay."

**Kip** *(angrily)*: "My sister's three years younger than me and he threatened her. I won't go home and do nothing."

**Kylo**: "When he ends up like that, what will you say then?"

**Seros**: "He won't."

**Kylo**: "Who says?"

**Seros**: "The rest of us."

**Kip**: "I'm not going home. I'd rather die than let something happen to my sister."

**Kylo**: "Bring her here — we could help protect her."

**Kip**: "You'd do that?"
