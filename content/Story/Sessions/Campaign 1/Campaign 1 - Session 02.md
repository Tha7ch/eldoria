# Campaign 1 - Session 02

*Present timeline. The party meets for the first time in prison, then is brought before King Lucerin.*

---

# Prison

The five of you sit behind iron bars, the aftermath of last night's chaos still lingering in the air. What began as an attempt to stop a bar fight had spiraled into something far worse — now, all of you are labeled culprits.

The guards pace slowly, eyeing each of you with suspicion.

**Guard**: "So, how'd you end up losing that bar fight, goblin?"

**Kip**: "Got caught stealing."

**Guard**: "What's wrong with your face?"

**Kieran**: "What's wrong with your face?"

**Guard**: "You've got that big red splotch on your eye."

**Kieran**: "Nothing. Now just tell me what you need so you can get moving. I don't have time."

**Guard**: "I think you've got more time than you know what to do with."

**Guard**: "You give me the creeps."

**Solstice**: "Good."

**Guard**: "It's one of you."

**Cyris**: "What do you mean by that?"

**Guard**: "Doesn't your kind ever get tired of doing bad things?"

**Cyris**: "I didn't do anything."

**Guard**: "You're in here for a reason."

**Guard**: "You aren't very womanly."

**Aella**: "I don't give a fuck what you think."

**Guard**: "You should. No one wants to marry a woman who ends up in bar brawls."

**Aella**: "Not my fault the asshole tried to hit on me."

The guards exchange glances, murmuring quietly. Their eyes flick back toward Aella.

From across the cells:

Solstice listens closely — *"...the king... big day..."*

Kip perks up — *"...security doubled... important event..."*

Cyris catches it all — one word standing above the rest: *Coronation.*

**Aella**: "Look at you two planning your next bathroom quickie."

A guard immediately draws his sword. Another grabs his arm.

**Aella**: "Yeah, listen to your boyfriend."

**Guard**: "Bitch."

Footsteps echo down the corridor. Heavy. Coordinated.

Kip leans forward, eyes narrowing. Six figures approach. The group stops in front of the cells.

**Ludwig**: "Let them out."

A man steps toward Cyris.

**Ludwig**: "H-hello."

**Cyris**: "H-hello."

**Ludwig**: "First time meeting. I'm Ludwig."

**Cyris**: "N-nice to meet you."

**Ludwig**: "Are you looking for a small gig?"

**Cyris**: "Not really... I just want to get out of here."

**Ludwig**: "He'll be down shortly to release you."

**Cyris**: "T-thank you."

Nearby, another man studies Kieran.

**Damian**: "That's an interesting mark."

**Kieran**: "So I've been told."

**Damian**: "I have something similar. Not really *on* me... more like *in* me. My blood. They don't like me saying that."

**Kieran**: "So you're... hellish?"

**Damian**: "I think. Forbidden magic."

**Kieran**: "I've heard of those."

**Damian**: "Blood magic's one of them. Not supposed to talk about it."

**Kieran**: "I don't see what's wrong with it."

**Damian**: "Maybe I'll show you sometime."

Damian slices his finger. Blood spills — then twists, forming a key. The lock clicks open.

**Manuel** (towering over Kip): "Woah, you're huge."

**Kip**: "You're surprisingly impressive for your stature."

**Manuel**: "Little one... we have a job for you."

**Kip**: "Does it involve gold?"

**Manuel**: "Yes. A lot of it."

**Kip**: "Don't let me out yet. I'm breaking out later — it'll ruin the plan."

Manuel grips the bars. They *snap* free. The door falls.

**Kip**: "What'd you do that for?!"

**Manuel**: "I don't feel like wasting time today."

**Kip**: "...Alright. You win."

A tall woman in white armor stops before Solstice's cell.

**Solstice** (arms crossed): "I've seen men try that stance before. They end up flat on their ass."

**Jeane**: "I can imagine."

**Jeane**: "Name?"

**Solstice**: "Solstice."

**Jeane**: "I'm Lady Jean."

**Solstice**: "Come to look at caged animals?"

**Jeane**: "You're not a caged animal."

Solstice gestures at the bars. Jeane glances at Ludwig. He nods. Jeane steps forward — and *kicks the door down.*

**Solstice**: "You just let prisoners out like that?"

**Jeane**: "Only the ones who deserve it."

**Solstice**: "What did I do?"

**Jeane**: "We think you *can* do something."

A hooded figure steps forward: [[Kylo Wisemoon]]. Blue sigils ignite beneath your feet. Cyris and Aella immediately recognize it — high-level teleportation magic. The world vanishes.

# Throne Room

You reappear in a grand hall.

Manuel steps forward.

**Manuel**: "You're probably wondering why you're here. It's because the king requested your presence."

**Solstice & Aella**: "Why us?"

A small figure peeks out behind Manuel.

**Lucerin**: "It's them?"

Manuel kneels.

**Manuel**: "I present to you, the king of Brittania. Prince Lucerin — no, King Lucerin."

Silence. Aella stifles a scoff.

**Solstice**: "You put a *child* on the throne?"

**Manuel**: "We haven't told the public, but the royal family is dead."

**Manuel**: "The Warmaster, Lucerin's uncle, has given up his right to the throne and let it go down to Lucerin."

The weight of that settles.

**Kip**: "Hi King!"

**Aella**: "A child."

**Manuel**: "Not just any child."

**Aella**: "But it's still a child."

**Manuel**: "He sees things."

**Solstice**: "He's what, five?"

**Kip**: "You're younger than me, and you're the *village chief*? Wow you must be awesome!"

**Lucerin**: "How old are you?"

**Kip**: "Guess my age!"

**Lucerin**: "Twelve?"

**Kip**: "Higher!"

**Lucerin**: "Fourteen?"

**Kip**: "No, I'm twenty-one obviously, just kidding I am fourteen! You actually thought I was twenty-one!"

**Solstice**: "You're all children."

**Lucerin**: "Lady Galefri—"

**Aella**: "You can call me Aella."

**Lucerin**: "No, I can't."

**Lucerin**: "Mister Arden, kneel please."

**Kieran**: "Kneel?"

**Manuel**: "It's best to do as the king says."

Kieran hesitates... then slowly lowers himself. Lucerin steps forward, small hand reaching up toward Kieran's face. His fingers touch the red marking. The skin begins to shift — returning to normal, if only briefly. Lucerin hands him a mirror.

**Kieran**: "You got rid of the scar?"

**Lucerin**: "Just for a little bit."

**Kieran**: "So this kid has healing magic?"

Cyris watches closely. He recognizes it immediately: *primordial magic.*

Kylo steps forward from the side.

**Kylo**: "Shocking isn't it, it's like he's an anomaly."

He says it directly at Cyris. Cyris nods nervously.

**Kylo**: "He's strange, and I don't mean that in a disrespectful way. He sees things, almost like you — past, present, and future."

**Cyris**: "I'm nobody."

**Kylo**: "Oh please, you think I didn't hear about you from my Strixhaven associates."

**Kylo**: "You're somebody alright."

Aella glances toward Cyris, noticing something subtle — his magic feels similar in wavelength to Damian's.

Lucerin steps between all of you. The world shifts.

## Ruined Throne Room

The throne room decays before your eyes. Dry blood stains the floor. The throne sits broken. Scattered throughout: older versions of yourselves.

**Lucerin**: "Yes, only I remain the same. You all see yourselves as you would be."

**Solstice**: "I still look the same."

**Aella**: "Eh, prettier, bigger tits, more scars. I'm cool with it."

**Kip**: "Who's this ugly guy that's a bit taller than me?"

**Aella**: "That's you, Kip."

**Kip**: "That isn't me, look at him!"

# Throne Room

The party snaps back to reality.

**Kip**: "Woah!"

**Kip**: "That was a fun illusion."

**Lucerin**: "It's not an illusion, it's what I see."

**Solstice**: "In that case, that sucks, a lot. You see us? Also?"

**Lucerin**: "Yeah."

**Solstice**: "That also sucks."

**Aella**: "Why us?"

**Lucerin**: "I don't know. I used to dream of you five."

Lucerin's voice softens — but only slightly.

**Lucerin**: "I have been tasked with leading this country to a utopia of sorts."

**Lucerin**: "My father was a great king, like his father before him. But currently... at this moment... I cannot trust anybody outside this room. The people who claim to be my greatest allies and supporters want me dead so they can take my place on the throne. I cannot let that happen. Otherwise, my family will have died for nothing."

**Aella**: "And you trust us? Because of your vision?"

**Lucerin**: "I see you leading this country into a prosperous age."

Cyris freezes. He has seen this moment before. The same room. The same words. Only one difference — Kieran looks completely different.

As Cyris blinks back to reality, Lucerin is already looking directly at him.

**Lucerin**: "I know this isn't the right way to do this, but I am going to ask for your help anyway. Will you aid me in leading this country to a better tomorrow?"

**Kip**: "I think you have the wrong person."

**Lucerin**: "No, I don't. I see you, Kip."

**Kip**: "I see me too."

**Lucerin**: "No... I see you."

Kip feels Lucerin staring into his soul.

**Kip**: "What are you doing?"

**Lucerin**: "I'm looking at you, Kip."

Manuel steps between them.

**Manuel**: "My liege, perhaps it would be better to let them process this information while you take an afternoon rest."

Lucerin kneels to the group.

**Lucerin**: "It was a pleasure meeting you all. I'm sure we'll be seeing each other very soon."

Lucerin skips off, Jean and Kylo following behind him.

**Kip**: "Alright big guy, so when am I getting the gold?"

Manuel pulls out five pouches, each containing 250 gold.

**Manuel**: "It is not a lot, but this is payment for granting the king an audience. I greatly appreciate it. He grows rather lonely these days. To be honest... none of us know how to raise a child. His mother was best at nurturing these magical phenomena."

Kip stares at the gold with a wide smile.

The guards, with the exception of Damian, leave.

**Damian**: "I will take you to the guild."

**Cyris**: "I have class."

**Damian**: "Will a letter from the king be enough to get you dismissed?"

**Cyris**: "I don't know, I've never tried it."

**Solstice**: "You realize we all have lives that we are just... expected to stop?"

Kip whispers to Kieran, asking if he should try to steal Damian's jewelry.

**Damian**: "Don't try it."

**Kip**: "Oh — not you. The other fancy guy over there."

Damian continues walking, then stops and turns back to the group.

**Damian**: "I'm not going to ask you to give your lives to a 10-year-old. But I am asking you to give his offer fair consideration. He's my brother, and I probably shouldn't be telling you all this... but I have a personal interest in his wellbeing."

**Solstice**: "If he's your brother, why didn't you take the throne?"

**Damian**: "My last name is Grimm."

Everyone, except Kip, recognize it immediately as a common name for bastard children.

**Damian**: "It is often the children who bear their parents' sins. My greatest mistake was being born. All I can do is ask that you watch over him when I cannot. He gets lonely. He gets scared. They were his family... and now they're gone. Other than the Kingsguard, he has nobody."

He leads the group behind the throne. A collection of children's toys is hidden there. Cyris notices one of the toys he made and smiles.

**Solstice**: "Proves he's a child... unfortunate."

**Damian**: "He lost all his family. He's doing everything he can to keep some semblance of a normal life."

**Solstice**: "I'm allowed to be upset at the situation. You don't need to patronize me."

**Damian**: "He used to be a jolly boy. I think he's held onto enough of that to... wake up every morning. I just want the best for him, that's all. I'm only asking that you consider his proposal."

**Solstice**: "I'm not good with kids."

**Kip**: "Will it be fun?"

**Aella**: "You're a good brother, Damian."

Damian hands a letter to Cyris.

**Damian**: "Hand this to your class administrator. It should give you a leg up in... various things."

Damian hands the group over to Ludwig.

**Damian**: "You will all be in Ludwig's care now. I hope to see the five of you very soon."

Damian glances at Kieran as he leaves.

**Ludwig**: "I think that's the first time he's ever looked at anyone like that."

Ludwig turns to Aella.

**Ludwig**: "So, Miss Galefri, how are things back home?"

**Aella**: "You can just call me Aella."

**Ludwig**: "I can't do that."

**Aella**: "Then just call me Lady."

**Ludwig**: "Lady Aella."

**Aella**: "That is... alright."

# Courtyard

**Ludwig**: "Ah yes, here it is."

Ludwig picks up a cloak of roses from a nearby bush.

**Cyris**: "And that is?"

**Ludwig**: "A robe of roses. It's a little too long, though — I'll need to get it tailored. I don't want to trip on it while fighting. That would be embarrassing."

Ludwig suggests the group should join the guild funded by House St. James. It is based in Brittania but operates across continents.

Ludwig mentions he knows of Aella departing the church. Aella says she will explain more later.

**Ludwig**: "Do any of you object to this?"

Everyone agrees.
