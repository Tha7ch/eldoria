## The Forbidden Library — Cyris & Kylo

![[Cyris and Kylo (Forbidden Library).webp]]

[[Kylo Wisemoon]] brings [[Cyris Voss]] into the forbidden area of [[Strixhaven]]. They are looking for information on ancient magic — the kind that [[Hannibal]] was using.

They want to see if [[Karsus Morningstar]] can help them find what they need. Karsus and [[Merlin Morningstar]] are already inside the library.

**Cyris**: "You sure seem to know your way around here."

**Kylo**: "Is any knowledge really forbidden under Karsus?"

**Cyris**: "Fair point. I guess your intent depends on your access — you don't want someone opening the gateway to the abyss for fun. If your heart is pure... well, no heart is pure, but good intentions and he won't object."

Cyris avoids Kylo's gaze.

Kylo picks up some books.

**Kylo**: "You seem... eager to learn."

**Cyris**: "I was top of my class when I was here. The role of protector of king seems to fit me more than a mage."

**Kylo**: "It shows. What is your magic type?"

**Cyris**: "Which one?"

**Kylo**: "Your main one."

**Cyris**: "We'll say water."

**Kylo**: "I wouldn't get far asking you for the other."

**Cyris**: "Have to get to know you a little better."

**Kylo**: "I won't pry."

Kylo turns to Karsus.

**Kylo**: "Well Karsus, I've brought back one of your favorite students."

**Karsus**: "Good to see you, Kylo. Cyris."

Cyris tells Karsus about his encounter with [[Hannibal]] and the forbidden magic that silenced Kylo's magic.

**Kylo**: "Yes, he basically shushed me and my body took the physical effect of the Wish spell, but had no Wish magic. It was silenced."

**Karsus**: "Did you catch this individual's name?"

**Cyris**: "Hannibal."

Karsus's expression remains blank.

**Karsus**: "Not who I was thinking of. Very few on the continent can counter a Wish spell — it is not an easy thing to do."

He pauses.

**Karsus**: "It couldn't be, could it..."

**Karsus**: "Did you have a reaction?"

**Cyris**: "That feeling I've told you of before."

**Karsus**: "Sounds like we are dealing with a forbidden magic user that can almost nullify a spell while leaving a caster with its aftershock."

**Cyris**: "It's... sort of terrifying. Leaving the caster like... like this."

He gestures to Kylo.

**Karsus**: "Over there — there are books on forbidden magics, users, and some still being written."

**Cyris**: "What do you mean, still being written?"

**Karsus**: "Someone needs to keep track. It falls to me."

Karsus hands Cyris an envelope.

**Karsus**: "Another thing — open when you'd like. It is an invitation to the gala."

Karsus mentions he'd introduce Cyris to some people — Strixhaven's donors, who are looking for talented individuals.

*DM note: information from the forbidden library books to be provided later.*

## The Palace Garden

![[Damian (Kieran's Death).webp]]

[[Aella Galefri]], [[Kip]], [[Solstice Shuriv]], and [[Aeron Velmont]] in a palace garden. Also present: [[Ludwig St. James]], [[Oldric Ameris]], [[Lucerin Atreides]], [[Jeane Howelle]], [[Lucius Raymor]], [[Manuel]], and [[Damian Grimm]].

About an hour after Cyris had left. Aella slept in and missed him.

[[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]] is on [[Oldric Ameris|Oldric]]'s shoulders. 

[[Damian Grimm|Damian]] doesn't yet know [[Kieran Arden|Kieran]] is dead. He is yearning.

Manuel notes that Damian seems down. 

**Solstice**: "To be blunt, why would he care?"

**Manuel**: "No, I just... I don't know. Only thing I could think to guess."

Lucerin loves talking about his day and everything that has happened — he is super friendly, especially to Aella and Solstice.

Manuel mentions it'll be interesting to hear Aella's family's reaction to her reappearance — no one really knew what would happen.

Lucius likes strawberry chocolate cake.

Aella asks if Damian is alright from all his wounds.

Damian notes that Kieran is missing.

Damian approaches the group.

**Damian**: "You're missing someone."

Solstice tells him Kieran died. He asks what happened.

**Aella**: "The prismatic barrier never broke. He was brutally killed."

Damian's face goes cold.

**Aella**: "Are you okay?"

**Damian**: "I'm fine."

Aella cannot tell his true emotions on the subject. Damian walks away.

Solstice looks at Aella as if to say "what the fuck?" Aella shrugs and makes a strange noise. They look confused.

Manuel approaches.

**Manuel**: "He doesn't take loss very well."

[[Ludwig St. James|Ludwig]] places his hand on Aella's shoulder. She instinctively smacks his hand away and glares at him. He throws his hands up.

**Manuel**: "One of your friends is aware of your return to Brittania."

**Ludwig**: "His name is Kaisel."

Aella stares into the abyss. She nervously chews her fingernail.

**Aella**: "I dread this gala every day more and more."

**Aella**: "It wasn't really my decision to leave. You really don't know what happened to me."

**Manuel**: "None of us do."

**Aella**: "Well, Solstice — let's go find a dress for this gala. I've got to stick it to my family."

**Solstice**: "Then we'll pick just the right one."

The girls leave. Ludwig turns to Kip and Aeron.

**Ludwig**: "Well, it's just us. What kind of trouble should we get into?"

**Kip**: "What kind of trouble?"

**Ludwig**: "Dammit, I forgot he was 12."

**Kip**: "I'm 14!"

**Ludwig** *(to Aeron)*: "What exactly did you have in mind?"

**Aeron**: "What did *you* have in mind?"

**Ludwig**: "Maidens, Aeron. Maidens."

**Kip**: "Maidens? What's that?"

**Ludwig**: "Beautiful young women."

**Kip**: "Beautiful young fairies!"

**Ludwig**: "More... human women."

**Ludwig**: "I can always adjust my plans. I'll be occupied at some point tonight."

Kip has a best friend named Pabu — a ferret.

**Ludwig**: "There's been a ferret in my guild hall!"

Kip calls Ludwig "Mudwig," and Ludwig takes a lot of offense. Kip says he had a friend that went by the same name.

## The King's Crown — Gala Briefing

![[Damian Catches the Septon (Council Chamber).webp]]

The [[King's Crown]] and [[The Kingsguard|Kingsguard]] are already present.

[[Merlin Morningstar]], [[Lucas Lyons]], [[Ludwig St. James]], and [[Karsus Morningstar]] come in after the party.

The whole King's Crown rises.

**[[Archibald Howell|High Septon]]**: "We've gotten word that a tavern served as a meeting for [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania|the Liberation Front]] with [[Roland Royce]], [[Samson Bloodmane]], and others. We hope that at the gala we can put a nail in the coffin of the Liberation Front. We know where [[Lanius]] will arrive and want you to intercept him."

**Cyris**: "Why? Isn't he invited?"

**High Septon**: "No. We want him killed."

**[[Jeane Howelle|Jeane]]**: "Grandfather, no."

**High Septon**: "Yes."

**Solstice** *(nearby, under her breath)*: "Bitch."

The Septon tells the party to watch for the Liberation Front.

**Cyris**: "Lucerin, do you agree with this?"

**[[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]]**: "I don't really know, but they say he is a bad guy."

**Cyris**: "My service is to you, not Brittania."

The Septon glares.

**Cyris**: "I will do as the king says."

**Solstice**: "The king's not exactly in charge."

**Solstice**: "What's the point in calling him king?"

**High Septon**: "Don't want to trouble him."

**Solstice**: "What makes you more qualified?"

**High Septon**: "Age, power, influence."

**Cyris**: "Power — ha."

---

[[Tirian Dawnmere]] enters through the door.

The High Septon says Tirian is "finally here to avenge his family."

**Tirian**: "Why isn't Lanius invited? He is the Chosen of two gods."

**High Septon**: "He spat in our face when he left."

**Tirian**: "Was he wrong?"

**High Septon**: "Of course he was."

**[[Veritas Aletheia|Veritas]]** agrees with Tirian, and says the High Septon is losing his touch — hunting down Lanius is unwise.

**Tirian**: "Trying to defeat Lanius is an improbability. The priority of the gala shouldn't be Lanius and the Liberation Front — it should be making sure the king is protected."

**Lucerin**: "Hi, Tirian!"

**High Septon**: "Tirian has potential, but he is too weak."

Tirian mocks the Septon's influence. The Septon gets up and wishes Manuel "luck" in protecting the king.

Before leaving, the Septon marches over to [[Jeane Howelle|Jeane]] and almost strikes her across the face — but [[Damian Grimm|Damian]] catches his fist.

**Damian**: "You should have better manners."

Tirian laughs.

**Tirian**: "Dick bag."

[[Oldric Ameris|Oldric]] turns to the party and tells them they have courage for standing up to the Septon. He thanks them again for helping [[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]]. He says if they need anything, his house is open to them.

Oldric, along with Ludwig, Damian, and Jeane, kneel to the party. Aella and Tirian say it is unnecessary.

**Tirian**: "Manuel and I are the only people who could threaten the Septon's position. I came to make sure he couldn't do anything crazy. I trust the rest of you to run the gala."

Oldric and Manuel thank him, and he leaves.

---

Oldric says he believes [[Brittania]] is about to enter a troubling age, with other parties vying for the crown. Lucerin needs to legitimize his claim in front of the nobles.

Oldric says he would like the party to become **knights of the realm** — recognized as the king's protectors and protectors of the innocent.

**Kip**: "What exactly is a knight?"

Oldric explains they'll be recognized as the king's protectors.

Oldric takes off his [[King's Crown]] pin and throws it on the table. He says he is resigning.

**Cyris**: "That is unwise."

[[Veritas Aletheia|Veritas]] agrees.

As people disagree, Oldric says he will stay — for Lucerin.

**Aella**: "Does anyone truly like the High Septon?"

**Oldric**: "They may not, but not many can rival his influence."

Tirian's assessment: whoever speaks the most at [[King's Crown]] will fill any vacancy, and that would likely be the Septon.

**Oldric**: "Then I will stay."

**Cyris**: "Lucerin needs as many safeguards as he can."

**Aella**: "Especially against that greasy man."

Oldric mentions [[Lumiere Ameris]] returning and that Lumiere initially had a claim to the throne.

Oldric walks the party out. Ludwig whispers:

**Ludwig**: "Let's get the fuck out of here."

**Ludwig**: "Talk about tense."

**Cyris**: "This is a different tax bracket."

**Ludwig**: "I *live* in that tax bracket. I mean, look around."

Before leaving, Cyris turns to Lucerin.

**Cyris**: "Lucerin, what do you truly want us to watch for at this gala?"

**Lucerin**: "The bad guys. Though I don't know that Lanius is one of them."

**Lucerin**: "I don't agree with everything he says, but Lanius has some points."

Everyone is glazing Tirian — the most loved noble amongst the commoners.

The party heads out: Cyris, Aella, Solstice, Aeron, Seros, Ludwig.
