# Campaign 1 - Session 06 (05-19)

*Parade briefing, training with Lucerin, Aella spars Jean, and Lanius is revealed.*

---

# Guild Hall

The party gathers with the Kingsguard and [[Ludwig St. James]].

**Manuel**: "I take it you all know why you're gathered here."

**Seros**: "No clue."

**Manuel**: "Guard duty for the king's parade."

**Seros**: "Oh. Yeah, that."

**Manuel**: "Our intelligence team thinks there will be many attempts on the king's life."

**Cyris**: "And you still think it's a good idea to have the parade?"

**Manuel**: "We have no choice."

Cyris and Aella can tell [[Damian Grimm]]'s face is pale.

**Aella**: "How serious of threats?"

**Manuel**: "Several high-bounty assassins."

**Kip**: "Don't worry, big guy — we can protect the king."

The party asks about positions. They begin to glow blue — a buff activates: +33 temporary HP and +6 to rolls.

**Positions assigned:**
- Ground: [[Kieran Arden]], [[Solstice Shuriv]], [[Kip]]
- Rooftops: [[Aella Galefri]], [[Cyris Voss]], [[Seros Ashwalker]]

Manuel says he's taken a liking to Kip and will be watching him personally.

Seros agrees not to fight any assassins. Cyris can tell he's lying. Cyris meets Seros's eyes and gives him a small grin.

**Cyris**: "Those Strixhaven bills don't pay themselves."

**Cyris**: "Hard to have an appetite after he told us to say our goodbyes."

Ludwig warns that attacks are more likely to happen on the rooftops.

**Major revelation:** [[Lumiere Ameris]] is apparently the heir to the throne if the king dies.

Ludwig says he trusts Lumiere and [[House Ameris]]. Seros and Solstice say they don't trust Lumiere.

Aella, Cyris, Kip, and Seros leave to train.

Solstice pulls Ludwig aside and asks him to take her bag to someone trustworthy if she dies. *(Recipient unrecorded.)*

---

# Training Field

Cyris makes a water ball and fire ball and spins them in his palm. Aella wraps her hands.

[[Lucerin Atreides]] walks over and checks in on the group.

**Lucerin**: "Damian said I should speak from the heart. Hearts can't speak."

Seros and Kip begin sparring.

**Seros** *(to Cyris)*: "Don't treat him like a kid."

Cyris casts Shield on Lucerin. Lucerin tries to cast it too — produces a half-shield.

Aella and Solstice talk about the best magic.

**Kip**: "Come on, old guy." *(to Seros)*

Seros dashes and punches Kip in the stomach, knocking him back.

**Solstice**: "He doesn't hold back on a kid."

Lucerin tenses up. Seros punches Kip directly in the chest again.

Kip laughs.

Kip tries to punch Seros — he dodges. Seros clocks Kip in the chest again.

**Kip**: "You haven't seen anything yet."

**Seros**: "Show me something, kid."

Kip asks if they can use powers. Lucerin covers his eyes.

**Aella**: "Come on Kip, do it — he's been kicking your ass."

**Aella**: "Do it, Kip."

**Seros**: "Do it."

Kip stretches — then wildshapes into a brown bear. Much closer fight now, both landing blows. They end after Seros does a little combo.

Lucerin heals them both — then collapses slightly. *(Using his power tires him.)*

**Lucerin**: "I trust you to protect me."

Lucerin says he wishes he could be with them all the time. Asks for a sleepover — says he'll ask Manuel.

Aella wants to fight someone. Cyris says he's not a fighter. Solstice and Kieran say no.

[[Kylo Wisemoon]] and [[Jeane Howelle]] show up and tell Lucerin he shouldn't have run off.

**Aella**: "Jean, will you spar with me?"

**Lucerin**: "Jean is really strong."

**Jeane**: "Very well. I can spare some time."

---

# Aella vs Jean

Aella focuses her energy into her spear.

Jean gracefully draws her sword.

Before Aella can blink — Jean's blade is at her neck from behind.

Jean kicks Aella's spear into the ground. Sheathes her sword. Elbows Aella in the gut.

Jean motions for Aella to try again. Picks up the spear and tosses it back.

Aella bats the spear away. Concentrates her magic in her hands instead and launches a Witch Bolt.

Jean raises a guard. The Witch Bolt hits — she basically tanks it. Residual sparks of lightning visible on her armor.

Jean tries to grab Aella — Aella bats it away. Jean grabs her again and slams her straight into the ground. Jean feels residual sparks on her hands.

**Jeane**: "You've got some heart."

**Jeane**: "Sure you could learn some things from Lanius."

*[[Lanius]] is the chosen of [[Veyrana]]. He wields storm and wind magic, fighting like a brawler who uses wind to enhance his punches.*

**Aella**: "Where can I find him?"

Jean shrugs.

**Jeane**: "Officially we can't tell anyone — but you'd have to seek out [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]."

*Cyris knows the Liberation Front — stories from the slums being mistreated, with higher-ups in on it. A group began patrolling the slums to prevent the mistreatment, and that was the foundation for the People's Liberation Front — they want to topple a tyrannical rule.*

Kylo does not like Lanius. *(Reason unspecified.)*

**Group splits:**
- Kylo takes [[Aella Galefri]], [[Cyris Voss]], [[Seros Ashwalker]]
- Jean takes [[Kip]], [[Solstice Shuriv]], [[Kieran Arden]]

*(Session ends here — what happened next is in Session 7.)*
