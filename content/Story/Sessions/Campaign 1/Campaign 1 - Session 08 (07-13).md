# Campaign 1 - Session 08 (07-13)

*Manuel kills the Warden. Damian is found barely breathing in a crater. Kieran is confirmed dead. Solstice saves the king. Cyris tries to resign.*

---

# Solstice POV — Simultaneous with the Hannibal Fight

Cutting to Solstice's perspective, happening at the same time as the [[Hannibal]] fight.

Upon hearing the chaos, Solstice looks over her shoulder but continues retreating with the king in her bag of holding. She feels a smothering, nefarious presence behind her.

**[[Jeane Howelle]]**: "We left Damian and Kylo with them. Don't worry, they can handle anything."

[[Lucius Raymor]] and [[Manuel]] look on edge. [[Ludwig St. James]] looks terrified and completely out of his element.

[[Hannibal]] and the [[The Warden|Warden]] appear. The Warden looks like what used to be a huge man — now genetically or scientifically mutated.

Ludwig reaches for a sword with a trembling hand.

**Ludwig**: "Mara, grant me strength and courage."

[[Solstice Shuriv]] grabs his arm.

**Solstice**: "Just relax, will you, geez."

**Ludwig**: "Do you not feel that?"

**Solstice**: "I do, but I'm not an idiot."

Solstice feels a hand on her shoulder and tenses up. Manuel pushes past her and Ludwig.

Hannibal's face sours — he clearly doesn't like Manuel.

**Hannibal**: "You're not supposed to be here."

**Manuel**: "Then where was I supposed to be?"

**Hannibal**: "Anywhere but here, preferably dead. I don't even know if Warden will be able to kill you now."

**Hannibal**: "I am interested in the woman you have behind you."

**Solstice** *(waving him off)*: "You're not my type."

**Hannibal**: "Not your type — that's a good one. I like that."

**Hannibal**: "Just let me kill the kid and I'll be on my way."

Assassins begin to surround them.

**Manuel** *(to Hannibal, referring to Solstice)*: "Do you really think you can kill her with us protecting her?"

**Hannibal** *(to Manuel)*: "Well, there will be other chances — to kill you, kill her, kill the kid, kill all of you."

Hannibal disappears. The Warden remains and begins walking toward the group.

Manuel gives a long, noble speech to the monstrous beast. The assassins run away, intimidated.

The Warden pulls out a twelve-foot spear made of thick solid metal. Manuel stomps on the spear when the Warden swings — snapping the tip off. The Warden looks at his broken spear and drops it.

The Warden throws a massive fist. Manuel sidesteps quickly and slashes him with his sword.

**Manuel**: "Do you think someone like you is enough to stop me?"

The Warden lunges. Manuel uses five sword strokes: slashes the Warden's Achilles, then stabs him in the head.

**Manuel**: "I don't know the last time I had to fight someone like that."

**Solstice**: "You alright, old man?"

**Manuel**: "I'm fine. Just like you said — an old man."

**Lucius**: "Should we regroup with our allies?"

**Manuel**: "No. They'll be fine."

Solstice pulls [[Lucerin Atreides]] out of the bag by his collar — like pulling a cat out of a bag.

**Lucerin**: "It was dark in there."

**Lucerin**: "Hey — what was that round thing in there?"

**Solstice**: "A globe."

**Lucerin**: "Was a really big globe."

**Solstice**: "Eh, the world's big."

The Kingsguard bow to him. Lucerin seems unbothered.

**Lucerin**: "It was like swimming, but it was weird."

Manuel goes to drag the Warden's body away.

**Solstice**: "Don't throw your back out."

**Manuel**: "Let us go. We need to make sure the king is safe."

# Return to the Castle

After half a day sneaking through alleys, the group makes it back to the castle and lets out a collective sigh of relief.

Lucerin tugs at Manuel's sleeve.

**Lucerin**: "Where's Damian?"

**Manuel**: "I do not know."

**Manuel**: "Have you heard anything?"

**Solstice**: "How would I know? I was with you all."

**Manuel**: "They didn't reach out with the telepathic bond?"

**Solstice**: "No."

Manuel sends a knight into the city to find the others.

The Kingsguard kneel to Solstice.

**Manuel**: "Your quick thinking today saved the king."

**[[Jeane Howelle]]**: "And that means more to us than you can ever know."

**Lucius**: "Truly, my lady — thank you from all of us."

Solstice raises her chin slightly.

**Solstice**: "It's my job anyway."

**Manuel**: "We are always appreciative of the fact. Things have not been easy for any of us since we lost the royal family."

**Solstice**: "Life is hard for everyone. You don't have to do all this."

**Manuel**: "[[Jeane Howelle|Lady Jean]], escort the king to his chambers."

Jean and Lucerin walk off. Lucius and Ludwig leave to attend to other matters.

Manuel mentions that whatever is inside Solstice's bag will be kept among the Kingsguard. Solstice immediately looks furious.

**Manuel**: "I will not pry. Just know it won't leave these walls."

**Solstice**: "Good. I don't need more trouble."

Manuel looks tired.

**Solstice**: "Need a nap, old man?"

Manuel opens up — talks about the deaths of the royal family, how it has affected the Kingsguard so deeply, how he lost two kids who were like grandchildren to him. *(Trauma dumping again.)*

Solstice can tell he's specifically worried about Damian.

**Manuel**: "He's reckless. I know that."

**Solstice**: "He'll show up. I'm sure."

**Manuel**: "He thinks with his heart and none of the instincts he has. How do you break that in a fight?"

**Solstice**: "Sometimes recklessness is good for fighters. Trying to suppress it just makes it worse."

**Manuel**: "But each bout is self-destructive. He tears his body apart."

**Manuel**: "I'm an old man with very many regrets. There will come a time when I'm no longer fit to be Kingsguard."

**Solstice**: "Mmhmm. It happens."

**Manuel**: "I'm going to go check in on the rest of everyone."

Solstice looks around, wondering what she's supposed to do now. Ludwig runs back.

**Ludwig**: "Here — follow us, get a drink or something."

**Ludwig**: "I have other matters to attend to."

**Solstice**: "So you think I'm a woman that just drinks alone?"

**Solstice**: "Is this to do with the nun again?"

**Ludwig**: "No, we Chosens have—" *(cut off)*

Solstice has a shit-eating grin on her face.

# Finding Damian

Cutting to [[Cyris Voss]], [[Aella Galefri]], [[Seros Ashwalker]], [[Kip]], and [[Kylo Wisemoon]] — about 45 minutes after the Hannibal fight.

[[Seros Ashwalker|Seros]] is carrying [[Kieran Arden]]'s body. Cyris summons an unseen servant to carry it instead. They walk through a clear path of destruction left by Damian's spell. The houses along the path are absolutely destroyed — people were at the parade, so no one was inside.

**Kylo**: "Are you all okay, for the most part?"

**Kip**: "I guess so."

**Aella**: "For the most part."

Cyris just sighs.

**Cyris**: "That looks like a Damian-sized hole in the wall."

**Aella**: "Sure does."

**Kylo**: "I don't think I've ever met someone with as much raw output of power than him — but it's just so destructive. No control to it or anything."

Cyris casts dancing lights around Kip to light up the building, but stays outside. Kylo calls weakly for Damian.

Kip and Aella find a bloody crater with [[Damian Grimm]]'s body in it. He is mangled, barely breathing — clearly sent through several buildings by an incredibly powerful spell.

Aella hops down and mutters a little chant. Kip looks away from the gruesome sight.

Healing Word does not work. It does the opposite — his skin is burning.

**Aella**: "How do we heal him? He's in quite a state right now."

**Kylo**: "He has to pull through on his own."

**Kylo**: "When we get him back to the castle, we cannot let Manuel find out about this. Not yet."

**Aella**: "Why?"

**Kylo**: "He'll lose his mind."

**Kip**: "Is he going to survive this?"

**Aella**: "He better."

Kylo drops his head.

**Kylo**: "I don't know."

Kip offers a Goodberry.

**Kylo**: "Let's just get him to the castle first."

Seros walks over and scoops up Damian.

**Kylo**: "Kip — we're going to let Seros walk behind us. You don't have to see this more than you have to."

**Cyris**: "So, where to now?"

**Kylo**: "We go back to the castle and we rest."

**Cyris**: "Where did the people get evacuated to?"

**Kylo**: "I don't know."

**Cyris**: "Was there not a plan in place despite us knowing there would be attacks?"

**Kylo**: "I'm not in charge of security. I can just tell you what we know of — and that was to save the king beyond all else."

**Cyris**: "So it's just 'fuck the rest of the city and save the king'? I knew I hated this continent."

**Kylo**: "We will do what we can to save those who need saving in the moment. But our oath is toward protecting the king."

Cyris rolls his eyes.

**Aella**: "Fair enough."

They start walking.

# Back at the Castle — The Crossroads

About six hours after the Hannibal fight — roughly 45 minutes after Solstice's group arrived.

They reach a crossroads inside the castle.

**Kylo**: "If you go straight, that will take you on a direct path to the throne room. If you wish to come with Seros and I, we can get you treated for any wounds you've sustained."

**Cyris**: "I'll go check in with the little King."

Aella broods in the courtyard. Cyris and Kip head to the throne room. Seros, Damian, Kylo, and Kieran's body go to the infirmary.

**Kip**: "We're friends of the king, dude. Who pays you guys?"

Jean leaves.

**Cyris**: "You were doing your job, we were doing ours — which was apparently cannon fodder."

**Manuel**: "No. You're not cannon fodder."

**Kip**: "What were you all doing? Where were the rest of you?"

**Manuel**: "Are the two of you okay?"

**Cyris**: "Physically, sure."

**Kip**: "I can't stop thinking about Kieran's corpse. And something one of them said to me — one of them knew my sister's name. One of them spoke of different times, like he had seen the future."

**Cyris**: "He should be alright."

**Manuel**: "What was that look, little one?"

**Kip**: "Oh, uh — nothing. I'm just shook from what happened."

Manuel suggests Kip go to the infirmary and Cyris join Solstice for a drink.

**Manuel**: "I don't doubt it, little one."

# Courtyard — Aella, Ludwig, and Lucius

Aella is outside polishing her spear. [[Ludwig St. James]] and [[Lucius Raymor]] spot her.

**Ludwig**: "Might as well stop and say hey, huh?"

**Lucius**: "Wouldn't hurt."

They sit down. Aella ignores them and keeps polishing.

**Lucius**: "Aella. Are you doing okay?"

**Aella**: "So to speak."

**Ludwig/Lucius**: "Was that rumbling you?"

**Aella**: "Might've been. There was a lot of rumbling."

**Ludwig/Lucius**: "The rumbling akin to an earthquake?"

**Aella**: "You're probably talking about Damian launching himself in the other direction. He's in quite the state."

She explains: a sickly man and his brutish servant showed up, put up an unbreakable barrier around them and Kieran. To try to save Kieran, Damian threw a blood spear at it — it bounced off and impaled him. He did it again with even more strength and launched himself over a mile through the city.

**Ludwig**: "Well, if there's anyone that can survive being strong and stupid, it's him. How are you doing?"

**Lucius**: "How's your friend doing?"

**Aella**: "Which one?"

**Lucius**: "The one in the barrier."

**Aella**: "He's dead."

Ludwig's sword falls to the ground.

**Ludwig**: "I'm sorry, what?"

**Aella**: "He's dead. It was an impenetrable barrier. He was beaten to death."

Lucius gets up and leaves.

**Aella**: "If it means anything, I'd rather fuck an Elvish princess than a nun."

They talk about the state of the country.

**Ludwig**: "That's why I put my faith in Lucerin. If there's anyone that can save it, it's him."

**Aella**: "I pray he doesn't get corrupted by the nobles in this country."

**Lucius**: "Well, there are always good houses."

**Ludwig**: "And the Lion Boy — the Lionheart as well, he's coming back too. Maybe you never actually met him. He's an interesting lad."

**Aella**: "What's he like?"

**Ludwig**: "He is very confident. Sure of himself. I really don't know how to describe him."

**Aella**: "Anything other than that?"

**Ludwig**: "Blonde?" *(uncertain)*

**Aella** *(pauses)*: "Huh. I wonder... no, I probably don't know him."

**Aella**: "It's nobody."

# The Distillery — Food Fight

Cyris and Kip walk into the distillery, which looks more like a mess hall or cafeteria. It smells good — not what either of them imagined.

**Kip**: "Hey, there's Solstice."

Cyris nods. Kip pulls on Cyris's robe with a mischievous grin.

**Cyris**: "Yes."

**Kip**: "You want to prank her?"

**Cyris**: "What did you have in mind?"

Kip spots some mashed potatoes.

**Kip**: "I'll pull her cloak and then you can throw the mashed potatoes at her."

**Cyris**: "I'm not doing that."

**Kip**: "Why not?"

**Cyris**: "What do you think she'll do after that?"

**Kip**: "She'll laugh. It's funny."

**Cyris**: "I will provide a distraction. You can choose if you want to throw those."

**Kip**: "What distraction?"

**Cyris**: "You'll see. I'll follow your lead, prankster."

Cyris walks up to Solstice.

**Cyris**: "You made it back in one piece, I see."

**Solstice**: "Yeah, this place isn't as impressive as Manuel talked it up."

**Cyris**: "Yeah, it's not much."

**Solstice**: "Real alcohol though, right?"

Solstice slides a drink to Cyris. Cyris lights it on fire — checking that it's alcoholic.

**Cyris**: "Just checking if it's alcoholic."

Solstice puts it out with Ray of Frost.

Kip grabs a massive wooden spoon, fills it with potatoes and gravy, and hurls it at Solstice from behind. Solstice leans back and dodges it. Cyris gets hit instead.

Kip looks shocked — then busts out laughing.

Cyris catapults a chicken leg with a spell. It hits Kip squarely in the shoulder and knocks him completely backwards onto the ground.

**Aella** *(arriving at that exact moment)*: "WAS THAT A CHICKEN LEG?!"

Cyris casts a water spell and washes the potatoes off his face. Aella picks Kip up by the collar.

**Aella**: "What happened?"

**Kip**: "Prank got out of hand."

Solstice stares at Cyris, looking very annoyed. Cyris takes a deep breath, trying to control his anger.

**Kip**: "My bad, Cyris. Didn't mean to hit you. The potatoes were meant for you." *(to Solstice)*

**Solstice**: "If you would have hit me with those mashed potatoes and ruined what I was writing, I would have personally kicked you out and frozen you to the ground."

**Aella**: "Let him learn the hard way."

**Solstice**: "The hard way was getting a full chicken leg thrown at him."

Solstice gives Aella her drink. Aella goes to drink it and nothing comes out — the glass is upside down and frozen solid.

**Aella**: "Is it frozen?"

**Solstice**: "Yeah. Cyris set it on fire. I don't like fire. Never do fire near me."

Cyris gives her a puzzled look.

**Kip**: "Doesn't like fire, doesn't like potatoes — no fun."

**Solstice**: "Glad to see you guys made it back."

**Aella**: "Most of us."

**Solstice**: "Oh yeah, where's Kieran?"

**Aella**: "Dead."

**Solstice**: "How unfortunate. Things happen."

Kip's face falls — he had been trying to forget about it. Cyris looks quietly disappointed at how quickly Aella and Solstice dismissed Kieran's death.

**Solstice**: "And the other one?"

**Aella**: "Infirmary."

**Solstice**: "Noted."

Cyris goes to get a drink. The distillery has autognomes — similar to the ones at [[Strixhaven]]. Cyris pokes one on the middle of its forehead.

**Cyris**: "Hello."

**Autognome**: "Hello."

**Cyris**: "Where do I get a drink?"

**Autognome**: "Right here."

**Cyris**: "Right where? Hello?"

**Autognome**: "The tap."

It pulls out a glass and pours a drink.

**Cyris**: "Thank you, sir."

**Autognome**: "You're welcome."

Cyris sits down and takes a sip. His face sours immediately.

**Solstice**: "You don't like the drink?"

**Cyris**: "I'm not old enough to drink."

**Kip**: "Why?"

**Cyris**: "Is there no drinking age in Elsweyr?"

**Cyris** *(to Kip)*: "You're 14."

**Kip**: "Yeah, so?"

Cyris slides the drink over to Aella.

**Aella**: "Mmm. Not bad for alcohol — it's sweet. I like it."

**Cyris**: "*That's* sweet?"

## Mysteries & Connections

**New mysteries raised this session:**

- **Why is [[Manuel]] unexpected?** — [[Hannibal]] said "you're not supposed to be here" and didn't know if the Warden could kill Manuel "now." He has multiverse awareness — Manuel's presence in this timeline is apparently anomalous. What happened to Manuel in the other timelines Hannibal knows?
- **What does Hannibal want with [[Solstice Shuriv]]?** — He said "I am interested in the woman you have behind you" before Lucerin was even revealed. His interest was in Solstice specifically, not the king. Why?
- **Why did [[Aella Galefri]] react to the Lionheart?** — [[Lucas Lyons]] (Chosen of Helios, head of House Lyons) is "the Lionheart" — already documented. But Aella thought of someone blonde when he was mentioned and said "it's nobody." She has a personal connection to him.
- **Who is "the older Morningstar"?** — Likely [[Karsus Morningstar]]; returning to Brittania alongside [[Lumiere Ameris]] and the Lionheart.
