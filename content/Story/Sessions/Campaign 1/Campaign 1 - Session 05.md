# Campaign 1 - Session 05

*The assassin is interrogated by Damian. Manuel opens up about Damian's history. Kylo and Aella bond. Kieran and Damian's relationship begins.*

---

# The Interrogation

[[Ludwig St. James]] walks through town dragging the near-dead assassin's body, returning to the [[Honest Hearts]] hall.

The party sees [[Damian Grimm]] — he is visibly pissed off. Tension in the air.

Once he realizes what's happening, [[Cyris Voss]] puts his hands over [[Kip]]'s ears and walks him out of the room. Cyris and Kip stay outside while Damian beats and tortures the assassin for well over an hour.

**Kip**: "Can you show me magic?"

Cyris teases Kip with an invisible hand yanking at his hood.

**Kip**: "What's that!"

**Cyris**: "A hand you can't see."

**Kip**: "That's pretty cool."

**Cyris**: "It's one of my favorites."

[[Solstice Shuriv]], [[Aella Galefri]], and [[Kieran Arden]] hear a door open. Damian emerges — blood all over his hands. Aella has a disgusted look. Kieran is also disgusted.

**Damian**: "He's still alive, you know. I won't ask you to understand completely. I don't expect you to."

Damian goes over to Cyris and Kip.

**Kip**: "Hey knight guy, you're bleeding."

**Damian**: "Not my blood."

**Kip**: "Then whose is it?"

**Damian**: "The assassin you brought in. It's his blood."

**Kip**: "But we already beat him, right?"

**Damian**: "Not enough. Not nearly enough."

Kip asks if there's anything else Damian has to say to Cyris.

**Damian**: "No."

**Solstice**: "You city folk are more brutal than I thought."

**Ludwig**: "That's just him and him alone."

**Solstice**: "If your city allows it—"

**Ludwig**: "We don't technically, but you can pay them to not care."

**Ludwig**: "He's very protective of his brother."

---

# Aella and Kylo

Aella steps outside the guild hall — needed a breath of fresh air. She sits in a corner with her head buried in her knees.

[[Kylo Wisemoon]] slowly approaches and sits beside her.

**Aella**: "What do you want?"

**Kylo**: "Just came to sit down, that's all. What are you doing out here? One thing the old geezer always instilled in us is a sense of compassion — so I'm supposed to ask, are you okay?"

**Aella**: "I'm fine. Don't push it."

Kylo puts his hands up.

**Kylo**: "You saw his nasty side. Ever seen anything like that before?"

**Aella**: "My father."

**Kylo**: "Interesting. Mine was my mother. My father was a hotshot mage who thought he could do anything — well, he got himself killed on some quest. Stupid, honestly. She took it out on me, too young to fight back. I made something of myself at Strixhaven and thought it would be fine. Then I met Damian and recognized that look in his eyes. It scares me too, sometimes."

**Aella**: "He can do whatever he wants — it's not him that bothers me. Just the... bloodied hands."

Kylo takes off his hood — he has a massive scar running from his hairline to his mouth over his left eye. The eye is glazed over.

**Aella**: "That is one wicked scar."

**Kylo**: "You're telling me."

**Aella**: "And I thought I had it bad with all these—"

She gestures at herself — she has scars all over, especially her arms: bite marks, slashes.

**Kylo**: "What happened there?"

**Aella**: "Nothing. Nothing at all."

**Kylo**: "When I was at Strixhaven I always kept an illusion spell up — never wanted anyone to see my eye. They weren't great at first, but I got better at hiding it."

They talk for a while.

Aella asks him about the mana crystal and spear she bought at the shop — whether she can mount the crystal on the staff. Kylo takes the spear. It glows blue — they figure out it has a shield spell on it. Kylo uses magic to meld the metal of the spear to hold the crystal. Aella tests it — responding well. Her blue lightning is almost more purple/red.

**Aella**: "Thank you. Not just for the spear — for everything."

**Aella**: "Wait — what's your name?" *(she forgot)*

**Kylo**: "Kylo. Kylo Wisemoon."

**Aella**: "Thank you, Kylo."

---

# Manuel Opens Up

Cyris, Kip, Solstice, and Kieran are at a table on one of the quieter floors of the guild hall.

[[Manuel]] comes over.

**Manuel**: "Hello, little one." *(to Kip)*

Kip lights up.

**Manuel**: "Heard you all had an interesting night. I need the exact story on what happened with Damian today. I do apologize — I didn't want him to see it."

**Cyris**: "I don't think he did. I was good enough to keep him out of the room. But Damian decided to walk directly up to us and tell Kip about it afterwards."

**Kip**: "Did I miss something?"

**Manuel**: "No. Just caught him at a bad time. Out of everyone I've ever trained, I've never understood how to help Damian."

**Manuel**: "Lucius was simple — he understood everything. Lumiere was a prodigy in anything. Kylo was the best of both worlds — magic and sorcery. Even Jean didn't need much training; she was great on her own accord. But I see Damian fight — and he fights with so much anger. I wonder why he was recommended to be in the Kingsguard."

**Solstice**: "Rage is a powerful tool."

**Manuel**: "Any of you know what magic he has?"

Manuel's gaze drifts toward Kieran.

**Kieran**: "What? No — why would you assume I know?"

**Manuel**: "He seemed open to talking to you."

**Kieran**: "He's open to more things than just talking."

**Manuel**: "He has blood magic."

*Solstice knows blood magic is a forbidden magic — taboo. In her homeland, people were executed if anyone even suspected they had blood magic.*

*Cyris knows it is one of the twelve forbidden magics, cursed upon humanity from the twelve demon generals ages and ages ago. It is strictly forbidden because its activation requires self-harm — the more self-harm, the stronger the output. The strongest blood mages bled themselves out entirely. People with blood magic have an advanced internal healing system and cannot be healed by regular healing spells.*

Manuel side-eyes Kieran, watching for a reaction.

**Solstice**: "I thought it was a myth."

**Cyris**: "Things can be mythical and still exist."

The party tells Manuel that Damian shared a lot of his backstory with them.

**Manuel**: "He doesn't tell many people. It weighs on him heavily."

**Manuel**: "I've never seen more cruelty to a child than I did when Damian roamed the halls as a child."

**Kip**: "Why did people hate him so much?"

**Manuel**: "He was— He was never meant to be a thing. He was born in an honor-bound society. He has to carry the burden of sin his father committed."

*(Manuel raised Damian when Damian was younger.)*

**Manuel**: "I wonder sometimes if I failed him. Failed to protect him from everyone around him."

Kip asks questions about what a bastard is.

**Manuel**: "I was never lucky enough to have my own children, and I tried to raise Damian how I would have raised my own. I've made a lot of mistakes in my life, and I worry every day that Damian might have to be the person that pays the price of my mistakes."

**Solstice**: "I don't think it's your mistakes. It's just the life he was given."

**Cyris** *(whispering to Solstice)*: "So why is the highest-ranked knight in the kingdom trauma-dumping on us?"

**Solstice**: "If it helps him fix his shit, so much the better."

---

Cyris feels mana fluctuations — like humidity before a storm, the air getting sticky.

*(Aella is testing her spear outside.)*

Kip stands up and looks around.

**Cyris**: "Seems like Aella is in a better mood."

---

# Outside — Aella Trains

Aella walks off. Cyris and Kip follow her like a mother and her two ducklings.

There is light rain falling in just one area. They see Aella training with her spear.

**Solstice**: "Having fun out here?"

**Aella**: "Oh — hey. Yeah. Just had to let off some steam."

**Solstice**: "Think you let off more than just steam. We could feel it from inside the guild hall."

**Aella**: "Feel what?"

**Solstice**: "The humidity."

Kip says her controlling the rain is so cool. She blows a light gust at him — he falls backwards and starts giggling.

**Aella**: "It was a light gust — are you okay, Kip?"

He jumps back up.

**Kip**: "I wish I could do magic like that."

**Aella**: "Maybe you can."

**Kip**: "I can turn into animals."

**Aella**: "You can?"

**Kip**: "Yep."

**Aella**: "Really? What kind?"

**Kip**: "I don't know — I've seen a lot."

**Aella**: "Can you show us?"

**Kip**: "Nah."

Aella holds up a gold coin.

Kip jumps for it.

**Aella**: "No — you have to do it first."

**Kip**: "But I don't want to."

**Aella**: "Okay, but then no gold coin."

He crosses his arms and pouts. Then glows green and transforms into a jackal.

**Kip** *(as jackal)*: "See?"

Aella is slack-jawed.

The jackal runs around, then does a backflip — transforming back into Kip mid-air — but doesn't land it properly and falls flat on his face.

Aella laughs but crouches down to help him.

**Aella**: "Are you okay?"

**Kip**: "Yeah. Usually I'm better than that."

Solstice asks Cyris about animagi and druids.

**Kip**: "My dad taught me. He's a really good druid."

**Aella**: "I know the leader of the northern druid grove in Elsweyr."

**Kip**: "Really? I've never been up there."

**Aella**: "It's a lovely place."

---

# Kieran and Damian

[[Kieran Arden]] is in his guild room. He hears a knock on the door. He opens it and sees [[Damian Grimm]] — cleaned up.

**Kieran**: "I was actually going to come find you."

**Kieran**: "You clean up nice."

**Damian**: "Trying to leave a better impression than earlier."

**Kieran**: "Wonder why you think the other impression was so bad."

**Damian**: "Didn't think you'd want to see me dripping with another man's blood."

**Kieran**: "No. I didn't."

**Damian**: "I am sorry about that."

Kieran tells him he should have used a different method — truth serum, or gotten someone else to do it.

**Kieran**: "We should stick to your strengths. What are you good at?"

**Damian**: "Fighting."

**Kieran**: "What else?"

**Damian**: "I'm good at, uh... getting my way with certain people."

Damian is blushing and leans in.

**Damian**: "Like who?"

Kieran leans in — then leans back.

**Kieran**: "Not telling."

Damian's ears go bright red.

They bond over anger while protecting things — agree they should find other ways to release it.

**Kieran**: *(glancing at the bed)* "It's a nice way to release anger."

**Damian**: "I think I have more than a few rounds of stress built up."

**Kieran**: "I have a lot of stamina, so—"

**Damian**: "Then we have all night ahead of us."

**Damian**: "You want to do this now?"

**Kieran**: "No time like the present."

They both stand up.

**Damian**: "After you."

Damian locks the door.

**Damian**: "Your move, Kieran."

Kieran walks to the bed.

**Kieran**: "And here I was expecting you'd want to be the one in charge."

**Damian**: "You can take the lead for tonight."

Kieran pushes Damian down onto the bed. Damian wraps his hands around Kieran's neck and pulls him down.

**Kieran**: "Don't blame me for what comes next. You wanted this too."

**Damian**: "Damn right I did."

Damian leans up and kisses him.
