# Campaign 1 - Session 01 (Doomed Future Prologue)

*This session takes place in [[The Doomed Timeline]] — a destroyed future approximately 20 years ahead of the present campaign. All characters are older versions of themselves.*

---

# Throne Room (Dream of the Past)

We open to Lucerin Atreides sitting on a throne, surrounded by knights.
Aella, Solstice, Cyris, Kieran, Kip, and Michael.

Michael is chained up and bloody, his right arm is a stump.
Lucerin, around 20 years old, the king of Brittania.

**Lucerin**: "Well Evangelist, you have anything to say for yourself?"

**Michael**: "Fuck you."

A knight stabs Michael in the mouth.

**Knight**: "That's no way to talk to the king."

**Michael**: "That's no king, that's an imposter."

**Lucerin**: "No, I am the king. Take him away."

Another knight drags him off.

**Lucerin**: "The kingdom owes you all a great deal of thanks."

**Cyris**: "It was our duty."

**Lucerin**: "You've saved the realm from a great threat and I fear thanks this time is not enough. Every day I strive to make a better kingdom. In the last 10 years you are all my greatest aide."

Cyris kneels. Lucerin pulls out a golden blade with a warm feel to it. He taps Cyris on the right shoulder.

**Lucerin**: "In the name of the warrior I charge you to be brave. In the name of the father I charge you to be just. In the name of the mother I charge you to defend this kingdom."

**Lucerin**: "Cyris, can I trust you to defend this kingdom, keep my people safe?"

**Cyris**: "I already have many times over."

*(Reference to Cyris seeing the future.)*

Asks Solstice to take the vow, somewhat reluctantly. Lucerin whispers to her it is just official business. He repeats the vow.

**Solstice**: "I suppose." *(said with a smile)*

**Lucerin**: "Lady Aella."

She will kneel slowly. He repeats the vow again.

**Lucerin**: "Will you uphold these vows for the rest of your solemn life?"

**Aella**: "Yeah..." *(begrudgingly)*

# Ruins of the City

Lucerin wakes up from a dream of the past (the scene we just saw). Kieran notices him waking up and follows him.

**Kieran**: "Something the matter?"

**Lucerin**: "A dream I had of the past."

**Lucerin**: "For all my powers, it never really amounted to much. I was gifted with the ability of foresight and thought by listening to it that it would help me make the right choice when the time mattered. But look at the city around us, just ruins, its people dead. I can't help but wonder sometimes if just a few more people lived or if I made a different decision, if things would have turned out differently."

**Kieran**: "It's no use thinking about it now. We can only do whatever we can to prevent more destruction."

**Lucerin**: "I enjoy your optimism. But we're waiting to die at this point. I no longer see a future, I just see black."

Lucerin talks about watching everyone he knew die — his brother, his wife, his son, all the previous great heroes, all of his people.

**Lucerin**: "I'm not a king, I'm a failure. When the Gods judged me, I'll be sent to hell for it."

**Kieran**: "You're being a tad bit hard on yourself. No one could have prevented this. We survived because of our fallen comrades. We need to make sure their sacrifices weren't for nothing, we can't just give up and accept that there is no future."

**Lucerin**: "I see the future though, I see nothing. A week ago I saw us standing here, but now I see nothing, I see the abyss consuming us."

**Kieran**: "Despite your foresight, I don't believe in fate. I believe the future is what we make it. Not everything follows a straight path, there are always other options. Your sight might have only seen one of them."

Lucerin gives a small smile.

**Lucerin**: "I suppose you're right. I guess, looking into the future and seeing so much negativity and death makes me a bit of a pessimist at times."

**Kieran**: "You just have to keep moving forward."

**Lucerin**: "You know he's nearby right? The Evangelist? I keep seeing glimpses of him. I'm sure we'll meet him again soon. He seems to want revenge for me executing him."

**Kieran**: "He won't escape us again, we'll make sure of it."

**Lucerin**: "Well said my friend, let's go wake the others."

They wake up the party and get them ready for the Evangelist.

**Solstice**: "Do we have a plan?"

**Lucerin**: "We just meet him head on."

**Kip**: "What more do we have to lose?"

**Lucerin**: "Our lives, but those are useless to the dead."

**Aella**: "Our lives have already been lost."

**Lucerin**: "Let us make our way then."

While walking they think about when the city was livelier, bustling with life, merchants, families.

Lucerin says he used to roam these streets when he escaped the castle, would anger his father. How distant those days are.

# Throne Room

The same throne room as the earlier scene, but now drenched in blood.

**Lucerin**: "I threw up the first time I sat on the throne."

**Solstice**: "Not surprising."

**Kieran**: "Seems fitting."

**Kip**: "Why are we back here?"

**Lucerin**: "Because I am a bitter man, and the memories of this throne room will forever haunt me."

**Solstice**: "So we've come here to lament your sorrows?"

**Lucerin**: "Yes."

We walk back to the city. Cyris thinks to himself about his time in Brittania and about how he never made it back home to the Badlands.

**Lucerin**: "You think I could have done more?"

**Cyris**: "Yes, but no one can do everything."

**Lucerin**: "If you were in my shoes what would you have done?"

**Cyris**: "I couldn't say, there's a reason I'm not King."

**Lucerin**: "I was never worthy of the title, I was fourth in line and just had foresight."

**Cyris**: "You care too much about this country to leave it entirely in someone else's hands."

**Lucerin**: "Visions of the past are ever meaningful. Hard to tell what's the past and what's a different path."

**Lucerin**: "I thought visions of the future were horrible nightmares."

**Lucerin**: "My dad said the real monsters weren't the monsters, but the people who walk amongst us. I don't think he was wrong by that. I didn't realize what he meant until it cost me everything."

**Lucerin**: "I thought my trusted advisors were there to help, I thought taking an oath meant something."

**Cyris**: "An oath is a promise to yourself, and a promise to yourself is easy to break."

**Lucerin**: "I was 10 when I took my oath to be King, and I knew every word of the responsibility it would bear. That it would mean giving up my childhood."

**Lucerin**: "I used to hate what they said about me, 'his eyes will guide us to a better tomorrow.' I'm supposed to lay the path for a utopia, and look at this destroyed land."

**Kip**: "Your grace, you're too hard on yourself, nothing that happened was entirely your fault."

We feel a gust of wind from where Aella is.

**Aella**: "Your Grace, this is great and all, but how long will we sit here lamenting our past and failures instead of doing something about it?"

**Lucerin**: "There isn't much we can do, Aella."

**Lucerin**: "I know your grief, you're not the only one that lost something that day."

Aella will scowl at him.

**Lucerin**: "I was ten years old when I met the five of you. I had no family and was stuck in a throne room I hated. I gained a second family from the five of you. I don't want you all to die."

**Kip**: "Kingdom or not, you're still our king."

**Lucerin**: "I was telling Kieran I had a dream of the day I knighted you all as protectors of the realm."

**Aella**: "Such a lofty title."

**Lucerin**: "Yes, in fact, it wasn't even my idea to give it to you all. The crown insisted that you need a title, a banquet, a feast. I didn't like the idea of making two ladies kneel before me."

**Solstice**: "She hated doing it."

**Aella**: "Call her 'lady' again and I'll strike you with lightning."

**Solstice**: "I think it suits you."

**Lucerin**: "Better times... To the battlefield of Armageddon we go."

# Battlefield

After a few hours of traveling we reached a destroyed landscape covered in dead bodies.

**Solstice**: "This place sucks."

**Lucerin**: "I guess this is the end."

**Lucerin**: "I've watched the Evangelist kill you all... again and again. This time he dies first."

**Kip**: "One last battle guys."

**Lucerin**: "Tired of being dead, Michael?"

**Michael**: "I've been alive a long time, I've never been dead."

**Lucerin**: "You died at one point, your head rolled to my feet and I kicked it away."

**Michael**: "Well here I am, alive as I ever could be."

**Lucerin**: "Unfortunately so. How many innocent people did you kill?"

**Michael**: "No one in life is truly innocent. I am just a messenger — my word is to spread the word of the gospel."

**Lucerin**: "You're a bastard."

**Michael**: "And what are you?"

**Lucerin**: "Same as you. I'll see you in hell."

Solstice brings out her white dragon wings.

Michael tries to cast a fireball — "Burn" — but Solstice counters it.

**Solstice**: "Nothing came out huh? Sucks doesn't it?"

Cyris lifts his staff, swirling it and the stars within it around. He casts *Chronal Fracture* and hits Michael, imposing disadvantage on his next save/roll.

*Chronal Fracture: You draw a thin line through a creature's timeline, severing a fraction of what it was from what it is becoming. For an instant, the target flickers — two versions overlapping imperfectly — before one possibility is forcibly erased. Temporal Recoil: The target has disadvantage on the next attack roll or saving throw it makes before the end of its next turn.*

Michael casts Hellish Rebuke, but Cyris passes.

Solstice flies up into the air and casts an ice storm around Michael, dealing bludgeoning and cold damage.

Kip summons an earth elemental, then hides behind a wall.

**Michael**: "Is the Goblin boy hiding from me? Come out come out wherever you are, Goblin boy."

Kieran conjures minor elemental sparks along his weapon and stands outside the ice storm ready to fight.

Aella is flying in the air with her wind magic. She flies further up and launches a level 9 Witch Bolt at him — lightning so dense it becomes purple — directly into Michael's chest. 120 damage.

**Aella**: "It's over Michael, I've had enough of your bullshit."

**Michael**: "I actually felt that, yeah! Now this is a real fight!"

**Michael**: "By my word, you will feel the wrath of [[Veyrana]], even though she has forsaken me, I will strike her down."

Michael does a fiery teleport behind Kieran.

**Michael**: "All heil [[Erebus]]."

Michael makes a huge attack on Kieran, but Cyris rewinds time to undo it and make him fail. Michael lands his next two sword attacks, slashing Kieran twice.

**Michael**: "Ahh that felt good."

Cyris casts Chronal Fracture again, using Fate Stutter — *the target cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn.*

A demon appears and stands in the middle of everything. *(This is [[X]].)*

Cyris feels an overwhelming sense of black magic and demonic energy coming off of X, with a similar energy coming from Michael.

The earth elemental Kip summoned crumbles away.

Lucerin stumbles to the ground, backing away from the demon.

**Lucerin**: "What are you?"

Solstice summons a white dragon behind Michael and puts a cocky look on her face.

A second demon appears: [[Kronos]]. Time stops.

Kronos goes to each person, puts his fingers to people and they feel like they're moved to a different time.

**Cyris**: "You again."

He punches Cyris in the chest. Kronos draws a symbol on the ground that is beamed into the sky.

Kip climbs over the wall he was hiding behind, then ducks behind another wall.

**Kip**: "King Lucerin, are you alright?"

**Lucerin**: "Be careful, I don't know what the hell that thing is."

We notice the ground opening up from the symbol Kronos drew. [[Luciferus]] comes out. We hear burning hands and screams also trying to come out.

Kip launches a fireball, but X instantly snuffs it out. X moves his finger to his mouth and shushes Kip. Hands wrap around Kip's neck and squeeze him before he can speak. Kip runs over to protect Lucerin.

**X**: "You're not going to protect the king."

X stares directly at Kip. Kip transforms into a tree.

Kieran slashes at Michael with his elementally-infused blade, cutting Michael in the chest and down to the ground.

**Kieran**: "Any last words?"

**Michael**: "Burn."

Kieran goes to behead Michael. X walks over and touches Kieran's blade. The blade begins to rust before dissolving in his hands. Kieran feels a burning sensation — his hand is dissolving off.

X and Kronos kneel before Luciferus. As Luciferus walks over, Solstice, Lucerin, and Kip are forced onto the ground.

Solstice growls.

**Luciferus**: "Ant."

Luciferus looks at Michael.

**Luciferus**: "Unravel."

Michael unravels out of existence.

Luciferus dispels all the magic in the area. Luciferus has one demonic wing and one angel wing on his back.

**Luciferus**: "I herald your end, but I do have to give you your props. You survived much longer than I thought you would."

**Kip**: "Who the hell are you?"

**Luciferus**: "Silence."

A sword goes through Kip's back. He can no longer feel his legs.

Luciferus walks over to Aella, who is glaring at him. He puts his hand on her head, frying her brain.

**Luciferus**: "You all fought valiantly, I can give you that, but now everything must end."

Luciferus makes way for another figure.

*Null.*

**Luciferus**: "And here it is, the end of everything."

The world goes black. Everyone dies.
