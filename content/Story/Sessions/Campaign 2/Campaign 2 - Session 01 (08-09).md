## The King's Crown — Tirian's Summons

![[Tirian (Council Meeting).webp]]

[[Tirian Dawnmere]] received a late-notice summons to [[King's Crown]]. The note said it was on personal matters.

Tirian is brought into the council room, where five members of King's Crown are present — the Archmaester, the Grand Arbiter, the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]], [[Manuel]], and the Warmaster — along with members of [[The Kingsguard]]: [[Lucius Raymor]], [[Damian Grimm]], and [[Jeane Howelle]].

The room is tense and serious.

**Tirian** *(purring)*: "All this security just for me?"

**High Septon**: "Well, we figured after the recent attack on the king at the parade, it is beneficial to have security devoted towards us."

**Tirian**: "Well, you're all capable enough."

**High Septon**: "Oh, out of all of us it's only Manuel and Oldric who would make it out of a dangerous situation."

**Tirian**: "Mm, sure."

The **Arbiter** puts his hands on the table. "Do you have any idea why you were summoned?"

**Tirian**: "I have some ideas."

**Arbiter**: "By all means, indulge us."

**Tirian**: "I assume it has to do with one of the two events of nobility being slaughtered on this continent."

**Archmaester**: "It's unfortunate what's happened. We have word that [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania|the Liberation Front]] is planning to make several moves."

**Tirian**: "When the acting king is a child, I doubt they could ask for a better opportunity."

**High Septon**: "We summoned you of course because we are under the belief that the Liberation Front killed your family."

**Tirian**: "It's a possibility."

**High Septon**: "Well, if our inside source is correct, then they had something to do with it. Have you heard the name [[Roland Royce]]?"

**Tirian**: "I'm familiar."

**High Septon**: "Well, recently we sent two of our associates to investigate him, and based on their information we are almost certain that Roland was the one who killed your family."

**Tirian**: "Would you provide this information?"

**High Septon**: "I don't have them here. We communicate through magic stones."

**Tirian**: "Mm, unfortunate."

**High Septon**: "I had hoped to give you something more concrete. Losing your family was a devastating blow to [[Brittania]], and now losing another family — we hope to put down these people."

Tirian, being perceptive, can tell the High Septon is laying it on thick and just trying to get him to do a favor.

He can tell [[Jeane Howelle|Jeane]] disagrees with the Septon's approach — she is clenching her fist.

Tirian notices [[Damian Grimm|Damian]] is injured and in a sling.

**Tirian**: "Why bring me to eliminate a threat? I'm far from your best solution."

**High Septon**: "You have a great many connections that many families could never achieve. *We* are under the assumption—" *(he looks at Oldric and the Arbiter)* "—that you, with your connections, with a healthy relationship with the lowborns of the city — well, the slums — could unearth the location of the Liberation Front. We are bringing this up because we believe this upcoming gala could be attacked and infiltrated by the Liberation Front. [[Lanius]] walking in scot-free."

**Tirian**: "Yeah, he's a force of nature."

The High Septon has a quick scowl on his face.

**High Septon**: "I don't think he's a criminal, but turning his back on the nobility and the people of this country — when I think of what we invested into him, I get very easily angered."

**Tirian**: "Well, he had plenty of reasons to not want to deal with this world anymore."

**[[Oldric Ameris|Oldric]]**: "There is no pressure on you to do this. We know it is a big ask."

**Tirian**: "I'm not sure exactly what you want me to do. Just asking around likely won't yield many results."

**High Septon**: "You have a good relationship with the commoners. I'm sure a bit of the vast wealth of the [[House Dawnmere|Dawnmere]] family being waved around could convince some loose mouths to talk. It is something we wanted to bring up so you could bring justice to your family. We don't want to force you to do anything you don't want to do."

**Tirian**: "My priority is my family and their reputation. This would betray the common people. This is your job to deal with, not mine."

**Arbiter**: "I agree with that, Tirian. The High Septon has no shortage of people under his command to work tirelessly to find the location."

The Septon looks kind of pissed at him.

**Tirian**: "If you can give me any concrete information from your spies, I would love to see that at a later date."

The Septon puts on a fake, condescending smile.

**High Septon**: "My people will be in touch at earliest convenience. We'd love to help you avenge your family."

**[[Oldric Ameris|Oldric]]** looks up at the High Septon: "No, you just want that thorn out of your side."

[[Manuel]] gets up and walks over to Tirian.

**Manuel**: "It might be best if you leave before the yelling starts."

**Tirian**: "It seems you all are the same as ever. Well, give my regards to [[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]], the young king."

He nods to [[Jeane Howelle|Jeane]] on the way out. He has been able to tell that she has been meek throughout the Septon's speech — there is clearly some unspoken history.

## The Hallway — Tirian & Lucius

![[Tirian and Lucius (Hallway).webp]]

[[Lucius Raymor]] walks Tirian out through the hallway.

**Lucius**: "You handled that better than most."

**Tirian**: "I know their games well enough."

**Lucius**: "I worry for the group coming in later today."

**Tirian**: "The people that were with Damian at the parade?"

**Lucius**: "Yes."

**Tirian**: "Well, it seems the Septon is hungering for power, with a child as king."

**Lucius**: "Yes. I'm not sure how much my father and mother have divulged, but if they won't, I will. The [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]] is trying to gain control of all four churches. He has placed my parents in an unwanted position — he is leveraging public opinion against them to take close control over the floor."

**Tirian**: "Well, he already talks like he owns [[King's Crown]]."

**Lucius**: "With [[Manuel]] and [[Oldric Ameris|Oldric]], he is too scared to make a move."

**Tirian**: "So how's your fancy new title treated you, Ser Lucius?"

**Lucius**: "I hate it. I'd like nothing more than to spend my time at [[Strixhaven]] with [[Orelia Greenleaf|Orelia]], but duty calls. When I am not needed, I am allowed to go to Strixhaven on 'official business.'"

**Tirian**: "Ah, official business. You learned from the best."

**Lucius**: "I will say that as many things as he is dishonest about, we do believe several people will try to infiltrate the gala."

**Tirian**: "I mean, all these events you are throwing are suicide notes."

**Lucius**: "The High Septon's bankroll makes it hard to say no. [[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]] does not have [[Leo Atreides|Leo]]'s firm hand on the High Septon. He is but a child, and I do not know how sustainable that will be for this country."

**Tirian** sighs. "I agree."

**Lucius**: "But, onto less depressing topics — I hear [[Lumiere Ameris|Lumiere]] is set to return any day."

**Tirian**: "Good. I've been meaning to grill him."

**Lucius**: "What'd he do this time?"

**Tirian**: "Refuse to take the throne."

**Lucius**: "Noble to a fault."

**Tirian**: "Annoyingly so."

**Lucius**: "I wonder what it'd been like if he had the will to take the throne. I think he could change this country in a meaningful way."

**Tirian**: "I don't think we need change, but we need to not be under constant threat."

**Lucius**: "The goddess [[Aria]] has talked to me and [[Aevos]] and told us to protect the king and [[Orelia Greenleaf|Orelia]]."

**Tirian**: "Is your goddess that invested in your love life?"

**Lucius**: "Who knows. I believe she sees something in Princess Orelia."

**Lucius**: "Always good seeing you, Tirian."

**Tirian**: "Likewise. I believe this is where we part."

**Lucius**: "I'm sure I'll see you soon enough at the gala."

**Tirian**: "Be well, friend."

## The Bar — Vernan, Roland & Samson

![[Roland Vernan Samson (Bar Briefing).webp]]

[[Vernan Hawkes]] at a bar with [[Samson Bloodmane]] and [[Roland Royce]]. Vernan was told to come here and meet whatever Front members showed up.

[[Samson Bloodmane|Samson]] is a big brute of a leonin barbarian that was sought out by [[Lanius]] for his strength. Loud, draws a lot of attention to himself, and prone to fight. 7 feet tall. From a noble line of leonin in [[Elsweyr]] — fairly nomadic.

A guy comes and offers to get them drinks.

**Vernan**: "Just a draft, please."

The man looks at the other two. Samson doesn't respond.

**Roland**: "Bring us a keg... please."

**Vernan**: "You have a presence as ever, huh Samson?"

**Samson**: "Oh, those small fries — they're nothing. They won't mess with *us*."

**Roland**: "Sorry we were late. We had unpleasant business to take care of."

**Vernan**: "What brings both of you here? Must be big business."

**Roland**: "Typically [[Lanius]] would come — he likes giving assignments — but he is busy currently. Helping those he can, trying to make a better [[Brittania]]."

**Vernan**: "Yep."

**Roland**: "We have caught wind of a gala coming up soon for the king."

They briefly discuss the attack on the parade and that it was not [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania|the Liberation Front]].

**Roland**: "High Septon will link us to the attack, but we are also trying to get a bit of dirt on him."

Roland seems to really want to kill the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]].

**Roland**: "Closer I'll be to ending his miserable life."

Roland mentions he and Lanius have an associate that will be sneaking into the gala.

**Vernan**: "Would I know them?"

**Roland**: "Only me and Lanius have talked to them."

**Vernan**: "He must be skilled."

**Roland**: "You are just as skilled."

**Samson**: "You are telling me this one is skilled?"

**Roland**: "Everything we have told him to do he has accomplished without issue. Show Vernan some respect."

**Vernan**: "If even old Roland praises you, I imagine you are a force to be reckoned with."

**Roland**: "You as well, Samson."

**Roland**: "When we send our person in, we don't want to send him alone. You seem to be a natural at sneaking in. We want you to shadow him. If you can get close to the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]], while avoiding his knights — [[Pollux]] and [[Castor]] — we would appreciate any information on his dealings or plans the noble families have."

**Roland**: "All of our sources went dark after the attack on the king."

**Vernan**: "They're rooting us out."

**Roland**: "It's a dangerous game we play, Vernan."

**Vernan**: "I'll do my best, but the Septon seems to be guarded, so no promises on this one."

**Roland**: "We have some trinkets to help you acclimate better. A ring of illusion to mimic your appearance and voice. You can pose as a lower noble no one cares about. And if you can get into any conversations..."

**Vernan**: "And this person I've been shadowing? Any info on him?"

**Roland**: "His name is [[Callum]]. He is traveling in with [[House Morningstar]]. He will be virtually untouchable while tied to that name."

**Vernan**: "He has [[Karsus Morningstar|Karsus]] on his side? Impressive."

**Roland**: "Have you met Karsus?"

**Vernan**: "You could say I've seen, not met."

**Roland**: "He's an interesting person, to say the least. You wouldn't think a noble like him would be so welcoming of knowledge."

**Vernan**: "I'll give that to him — he seems to want people to learn."

**Roland**: "He doesn't gatekeep knowledge."

**Roland**: "There are new players, and I do not know much about them, which is why I am taking you. At the parade there were six new faces — a goblin boy, a tiefling with a broken horn, a half-elf girl, a girl with white hair, and another one that I don't know much of. The half-elf girl is also a mystery. If you can tail any of them, hear what they say, we can determine if they might be helpful."

**Vernan**: "OK."

Vernan takes out a notebook and writes down the descriptions.

**Roland**: "If you think your cover is burnt, do not engage further. The High Septon will have many people there. Avoid [[The Kingsguard|the Kingsguard]]."

**Vernan**: "And Cal? Should he be with me, or are we separate forces?"

**Roland**: "Cal can make his way out, being a guest of Karsus."

Vernan gazes up at the ceiling, formulating a plan.

Roland tosses Vernan a bag of around 100 gold coins.

**Roland**: "Think of this as half your payment for last mission and half a deposit for this one."

Vernan pushes back 20–30 gold.

**Vernan**: "You know where this usually goes. I'd appreciate if you could give it to my family."

**Roland**: "Of course."

Vernan takes the rest.

**Roland**: "If there's anything you need, let us know. We're on the verge of big changes in Brittania — we need all the help we can."

**Vernan**: "Hopefully Lanius is back before then."

**Roland**: "Hopefully."

**Vernan**: "Is that all then?"

**Roland**: "We will stick around for a bit. We have other visitors, and Samson is itching to fight."

**Vernan**: "Sounds like a fun time for you, Samson."

**Samson**: "These people are nowhere near enough to do anything."

**Samson**: "I'll tell you who was a good fight though — the blood boy from [[The Kingsguard|the Kingsguard]]."

Roland scowls through his bandages.

**Roland**: "Don't remind me."

**Vernan**: "One last question — is Cal aware of me?"

**Roland**: "He is not. He won't know."

## The Bar — Callum & Roland

![[Callum (Bar).webp]]

At the same bar, [[Callum]] strolls over to the same table around a half hour later. [[Samson Bloodmane|Samson]] seems antsy. Callum kicks his legs up on the table.

**Callum**: "Afternoon, Roland. It's been a while."

**Roland**: "How have things been?"

**Callum**: "Here and there. Waiting for this gala to start — I think that's why I'm here."

**Roland**: "What do you know of the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]]?"

**Callum**: "I know he deserves to die, but not much more. You know I haven't been around long enough to know these things."

**Roland**: "He's the worst of them all."

**Callum**: An artifact will be at the gala. He doesn't know what it will look like or who will have it, but [[Karsus Morningstar|Karsus]] said he would be able to feel it.

**Roland**: "You are a rather unique person."

**Callum**: "If you want me to do another job, what's it about?"

**Roland**: "We need eyes and ears on the Septon while you're there. [[Pollux]] and [[Castor]], his primary guards — the sun and moon knight — they'll be there watching and close to him, so it will be difficult."

**Callum**: "I've been happy to kill who needs to die, steal whatever — this isn't exactly my wheelhouse."

**Roland**: "I'll imagine you'll meet some interesting characters that could give you info."

**Callum**: "Well, I suppose that about concludes it. Samson looks like he's gonna explode if he doesn't leave."

**Roland**: "Leave or fight, and I don't think it's leaving he wants to do."

**Callum**: "No one strong here to fight. What are you going to do, big guy?"

**Samson**: "I'll deal with whatever."

**Callum**: "I don't see much competition. You'll have to outsource that, big guy."

**Samson**: "If only I was back in [[Elsweyr]] — never a shortage of fights back in my pride."

**Callum**: "If there's anything else, tell me. Otherwise I have a busy night."

**Roland**: "That concludes it."

**Callum**: "Alright, wish me luck I suppose."

**Roland**: "You'll have our full support as always. Do well, friend."

**Callum**: "You sending anyone else?"

**Roland**: "No, not for now. We don't want to spread information around."

**Callum**: "Only me. Lot of faith in me, Roland."

**Roland**: "Lot of new figures that will likely be taking more attention than you, so you should be fine."

**Callum**: "I'll update you when the mission is done. Have a good night, fellas."

**Roland**: "You too."

As Callum walks out, he hears things getting thrown around — probably Samson starting a fight.
