# The First Generation Mages

*An era of ancient history, approximately 3,000+ years before the present campaign.*

---

## Overview

The First Generation Mages were the first humans to ever unlock and utilize arcane power — discovering and awakening their latent magical potential. They represent the origin point of human magic. Their level of magical attainment became the benchmark against which all later mages are measured, though no one has matched it in over 3,000 years.

[[Lanius]] is the first person in recorded history to reach this level again.

## Defining Traits

All First Generation Mages shared three key characteristics:

- **Dual casters** — capable of wielding two distinct forms of magic simultaneously
- **Exceptional mana levels** — far beyond what modern mages can achieve
- **Spell strength** — their spells were remarkably more powerful than anything produced in the millennia since

These traits together defined what it meant to reach "First Generation" level. Lanius achieving this status implies he possesses all three.

## The Demon War

During the era of the First Generation Mages, **demons were actively waging war on both the mortal and celestial planes simultaneously**. This was a period of existential conflict — humanity's magical awakening occurred in the context of an active demonic invasion of reality itself.

The [[The Forbidden Magics|Twelve Forbidden Magics]] were cursed upon humanity during this era by [[The Twelve Demon Generals]] *(unconfirmed — timeline fits)*. The First Generation Mages may have been humanity's response to or product of this conflict.

## Significance of Lanius

That [[Lanius]] has reached First Generation Mage status after 3,000 years of no one achieving it raises profound questions — why now, and what does it mean for the present conflict with Hannibal, Luciferus, and the Demon Generals?
