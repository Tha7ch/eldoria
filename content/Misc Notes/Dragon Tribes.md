## Overview

Dragon tribes are communities of humans who live in close proximity to a specific dragon, following it across territory and building their culture around it. Each tribe takes its name from its dragon. There are two broad categories — **Chromatic** and **Metallic** — with fundamentally different philosophies, naming structures, and relationships to both their dragons and the outside world.

A dragon can live up to **2,000 years** at full term. It is rare for a tribe to witness their dragon's natural death — most dragons are hunted, starved, or killed by another dragon. When a dragon dies, the tribe renames itself under the youngest kin of that dragon and continues.

---

## Chromatic Tribes

### Philosophy

Chromatic tribes are insular and solitary. They do not form connections with large cities or other groups. Even tribes worshipping the **same dragon species** will clash over territory, each believing their dragon is superior. The only exception is mating season — two same-species tribes will meet diplomatically to bring their dragons together, then return to distant strangers. If the dragons form a mated pair, the tribes may merge and grow.

The Chromatic relationship to their dragon is one of worship from the people and indifference from the dragon. Chromatic dragons do not particularly care about their worshippers beyond the perks — treasure offerings, meat, elder sacrifices. In return, the dragon unintentionally protects the tribe simply by being present: enemies avoid the nest.

**Elder Sacrifice:** When an elderly member decides it is their time, they offer themselves as a meal to the dragon. This is considered an honor.

The tribes have become so integral to the dragons' mating process that Chromatic dragons may now rely on their human tribes to locate potential mates across vast territories.

### Naming Convention

*"WE are the tribe of [Dragon]."* — asserting identity and superiority.

**Format:** `[Chromatic Prefix]'[Dragon's Name]`

| Dragon Color | Prefix | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| White | Unvy' | Uun-Vee |
| Blue | Iervy' | Ee-er-vee |
| Red | Enai' | Ae-naye |
| Green | Coghe' | Cog-He |
| Black | Deseo' | Des-Sae-oh |

**Example:** *Unvy'Ezil* — "We are the tribe of Ezil [the white dragon]."

### Origin Myth

In the time of old, a child was born from the blood of a dragon and a human woman. That dragon was **Amanagodonus** *(Ama-nah-go-do-nus)* — the great wraith, equivalent to Tiamat. While losing the child, Amanagodonus offered their blood in turn: a deal — eternal worship for all descending bloodlines.

When Amanagodonus split into the original five Chromatic dragons, that child grew into a woman who bore quintuplets — one child for each color (Red, Blue, White, Black, Green). From those five children, the Chromatic tribes grew and spread, each following their dragon.

## Metallic Tribes

### Philosophy

Metallic tribes are diplomatic and outward-facing. They encourage cooperation with other Metallic tribes and with the general public, staying near populated areas such as [[Brittania]] rather than keeping to the wilderness. Their relationship with their dragon is less about worship and more about mentorship — the dragon guides and teaches rather than receiving tribute.

The relationship between Metallic and Chromatic tribes is rocky. Chromatic tribes view Metallic tribes as a threat to their dragon. Metallic tribes see Chromatic values as corrupt. When scuffles occur, it is nearly always Chromatic tribes that strike first. In the current, more diplomatic era, outright tribal conflict is less common — Metallic tribes have moved toward civilization and Chromatic tribes have stayed to the nomadic old ways.

### Naming Convention

*"We are [Dragon]'s tribe."* — welcoming and unifying, acknowledging the dragon's ownership.

**Format:** `[Dragon's Name]'[Metallic Suffix]`

| Dragon Type | Suffix | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | 'Rumviale | Rum-vi-allae |
| Silver | 'Olunis | Oh-lun-iss |
| Bronze | 'Neilve | Neil-vee |
| Brass | 'Ekrid | Ek-rid |
| Copper | 'Kadlit | Kad-lit |

### Origin Myth

In the time of old, a child was born from the tears of a dragon and a human woman. That dragon was **OSIAR** *(Oh-Sigh-Are)* — the great Light, equivalent to Bahamut. While losing the child, OSIAR wept in turn: a blessing — eternal fortune for all descending bloodlines.

When OSIAR split into the original five Metallic dragons, that child grew into a woman who bore quintuplets — one for each type (Gold, Bronze, Silver, Brass, Copper). From those five children, the Metallic tribes grew.

## Known Tribes

### Unvy'Ezil *(White Dragon — Chromatic)*

[[Solstice Shuriv]]'s tribe. Named for **Ezil**, the great white dragon of the northern peaks.

The Unvy'Ezil lived high in the snow peaks of the far north of [[Frostgard]] — the [[Ycehelm-Thermguard]] region — in temperatures reaching -40°C, far too cold for most human habitation. They were completely solitary.

The beginning of the end for the tribe came with **the great migration**: Ezil moved down from the peaks to hunt closer food, making rest at an ice cave at the base of the peaks. This brought the tribe into territory and circumstances that eventually led to the tribe's collapse.
