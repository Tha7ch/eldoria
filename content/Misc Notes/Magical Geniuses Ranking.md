## Overview

A ranking of the greatest magical minds currently alive. This is not a raw power ranking — it measures magical genius specifically, in terms of magical power and mastery of magics. [[Lanius]], for example, is arguably the most powerful living combatant but is described as "more a force of nature than a magical genius" and does not appear on this list.

Note: [[Cyris Voss]] is not on this list because his genius is a different kind — ingenuity rather than raw magical power or mastery. Where [[Merlin Morningstar]] is a genius in terms of sheer magical ability and mastering magics, Cyris is a genius in terms of creative problem-solving and inventive application.

## The Ranking

1. **[[Merlin Morningstar]]** — arguably the strongest and most gifted mage alive; inherent time magic *(secret)*
2. **[[Lumiere Ameris]]** — was supposed to be 5th Kingsguard and king, but chose to be a general to actually contribute to the kingdom; possesses a lunar domain
3. **[[Kylo Wisemoon]]** — actual 5th Kingsguard; former Lorehold mage at [[Strixhaven]]
4. *Unknown*
5. **[[Damian Grimm]]** — gained mastery over forbidden blood magic remarkably fast; enormous raw power but low battle IQ, fights with his heart
6. **[[Saelen Brightfeather]]** — 4th year Prismari student at [[Strixhaven]]

## Notes

- [[Lanius]] is excluded not because he is weak but because his strength is a different category — raw elemental force rather than magical innovation. He is the first dual caster mage in 3,000 years and could take on multiple members of the former top 10 simultaneously.
- [[Karsus Morningstar]] is not on this list but is known as one of the best magic users in the world — his forbidden magic (Magic) is public knowledge and is the reason he is Headmaster of [[Strixhaven]].
