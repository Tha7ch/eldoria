# The Ten Heroes

*A group of ten notable heroes. The roster is only partially known, and the "official" list collectively omits one member out of spite.*

---

## Overview

The Ten Heroes are a recognized group of notable individuals. Five members are currently known by name, plus one further member ([[Razaar Blackwing]]) at #7. Three slots remain entirely unknown. Officially, the list only has nine names — the tenth spot, which by rights should belong to [[Roland Royce]], was collectively left off by the others because they dislike him so much.
