# The Ancient Seven Heroes

*A group of legendary heroes from ancient Brittanian and Eldorian history. Little is currently known beyond their existence.*

---

## Overview

The Ancient Seven Heroes are a legendary group from deep history. At least one of them, **Magnus St. James**, is the founder of [[House St. James]] — one of Brittania's great noble houses. The full roster and their deeds are largely unknown.

## Known Members

- **Magnus St. James** — founder of [[House St. James]]

## Unknown Members

Six of the seven remain unidentified.
