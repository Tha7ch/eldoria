# Cosmology of Eldoria

*Revealed by [[Kylo Wisemoon]] in Session 7. This is the most comprehensive lore explanation given so far.*

---

## Divine Chosens

Each god may have up to **two Chosens** at any time — mortals chosen to bear a god's power and serve as their champions. All Chosens are fated to die by age 40. This is not unique to any one Chosen — it is the universal cost of carrying divine power in a mortal body.

**Known Chosens:**
- [[Aria]] — [[Lucius Raymor]] and [[Aevos]]
- [[Luna]] — [[Lumiere Ameris]]  *(second Chosen unknown)*
- [[Helios]] — [[Lucas Lyons]] *(second Chosen unknown)*
- [[Wylder]] — [[Kaisel Irvenest]] *(second Chosen unknown)*
- [[Veyrana]] — [[Lanius]] *(second Chosen unknown)*
- [[Mara]] — [[Ludwig St. James]] *(second Chosen unknown)*

## The Celestial Realm

Where the Gods, their angels, and related beings exist. The Celestials attempted to create life for the mortal plane.

## The Abyss

The inverse of the Celestial Realm. What most people call **Hell**.

**Origin:** When the Celestials tried to create life for the mortal plane, they created something — or someone — that proved the existence of imperfection. They wanted a universe of perfection. In their attempts, they made something that scared them or that they could not accept. So they cast it into the Abyss.

The pure energy cast into the Abyss did not die. It became something. **What came of this was life — and the demon generals.**

## The Mortal Plane

Where the campaign takes place.

## The Demon War (~3,000 Years Ago)

During the era of the [[First Generation Mages]] — the first humans to ever unlock arcane power — demons were actively waging war on both the mortal and celestial planes simultaneously. This is the earliest known period of large-scale demonic conflict. The [[The Forbidden Magics|Twelve Forbidden Magics]] were cursed upon humanity during or around this era. What ended this war is unknown.

## The Twelve Demon Generals

Born from the Celestials' rejected creation being cast into the Abyss. Supposed to be folktales — but they are real.

- One is **the source of [[Cyris Voss]]'s power** *(unconfirmed which one — likely [[Kronos]], Demon General of Time, given Cyris's chronomancy)*
- One is **directly responsible for [[Damian Grimm]]** *(the Blood General)*
- Each wields one of the twelve [[The Forbidden Magics|forbidden magics]], which were cursed upon humanity

## [[Hannibal]]'s Nature

Unknown. Kylo does not know what he is. His magic is not man-made. The residue of his cube is Abyssal — it screams. This suggests Abyssal origin or connection, but Kylo explicitly could not confirm he is a demon.

## The Wish Spell

Mortals can access divine power. When they do, it costs enormously — Kylo's right arm became nearly skeletal from one attempt. He will not recover for half a year. The cost scales with power used.

## Multiverse / Alternate Timelines

[[Hannibal]] referenced alternate timelines and versions of people ("in this universe," "you're actually alive in this one"). He has awareness of — and possibly travels between — different versions of reality. Kylo flagged this to Cyris privately and proposed a trip to the restricted library at [[Strixhaven]] to investigate.
