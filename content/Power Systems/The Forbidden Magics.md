## Overview

The Forbidden Magics are a set of prohibited magical disciplines — each wielded by one of [[The Twelve Demon Generals]]. They are considered forbidden, likely because of their destructive or destabilizing potential. [[Damian Grimm]] has blood magic running in his blood — one of the forbidden magics — and is not supposed to discuss it.

There are 12 forbidden magics total.

## Rules & Mechanics

**Blood Magic (most detailed known):**
- Activation requires self-harm — the more self-harm, the stronger the output
- The strongest blood mages historically bled themselves out entirely using their power
- People with blood magic have an advanced internal healing system
- Cannot be healed by regular healing spells
- Considered so dangerous it is taboo — in some regions (including Solstice's homeland), people were executed merely on suspicion of having it

**General:**
- The forbidden magics were cursed upon humanity by the twelve demon generals ages ago
- Strictly forbidden across most of the world

**Magic:**
- Two living wielders represent opposite approaches. [[X]] (Demon General) twists and forces magic to bend to his will — a violent, dominating relationship with magic itself. [[Karsus Morningstar]] (mortal) weaves magic intuitively and gently — where most mages forcibly put magic together through their body, Karsus does it cooperatively. They are mortal enemies.

**Inherent magic awakening:**
- Wizards and sorcerers both have inherent magic types that typically awaken around age 10, often manifesting as a cantrip-level version of the magic type. After awakening, any mage with Detect Magic can identify a person's inherent type. Most people have one inherent magic. Having two is extraordinarily rare — only [[Cyris Voss]] (time + water) and [[Lanius]] (storm + sky) are known to have two. A dual-inherent wielder cannot become a **dual caster mage** until they master both. Lanius is the first dual caster mage in 3,000 years.

## Lore & Origin

Revealed by Kylo (Session 7): The forbidden magics were cursed upon humanity from the twelve demon generals — who themselves originated when the Celestials cast an imperfect creation into the Abyss. This cursing occurred during or around the era of the [[First Generation Mages]], approximately 3,000 years ago, when demons were actively waging war on both the mortal and celestial planes. The demon generals are the product of that cast-off energy becoming something. The forbidden magics are their legacy on the mortal plane.

One demon general is the source of [[Cyris Voss]]'s chronomancy. One is directly responsible for [[Damian Grimm]]'s blood magic. These powers are not chosen — they are inherited or cursed.
