## Overview

[[Lucerin Atreides]] possesses an anomalous primordial power that allows him to perceive the past, present, and future simultaneously. [[Kylo Wisemoon]] describes him as an "anomaly." The power appears to be innate — not taught or granted by a god — though his mother was best at nurturing it. It is distinct from Cyris's time magic, though Kylo implied a similarity between them.

## Rules & Mechanics

- Lucerin sees past, present, and future at once — not as a choice but as a constant experience
- He can project visions to others, showing them what he sees (demonstrated in Session 2 — showed the party their future selves in the ruined throne room)
- Can briefly manipulate the physical state of a person — temporarily healed/concealed [[Kieran Arden]]'s face marking with a touch (Session 2)
- Using the power has a cost — he collapsed slightly after healing Kip and Seros in Session 6, suggesting it drains him physically

## Known Users

- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — the only known user; described as anomalous even among magic users

## Limitations & Costs

- Physically tiring — collapsed briefly after exerting it in Session 6
- In the doomed future, Lucerin can no longer see ahead at all — only black. The power appears to fail or be blocked when the future holds only destruction
- Cannot prevent what he sees — he experienced every death in the doomed timeline before it happened and could not stop them

## Lore & Origin

Unknown. Lucerin was born with it. His mother understood it best. Kylo calls it an anomaly and compares it loosely to [[Cyris Voss]]'s abilities — both perceive time in unusual ways, though their mechanisms differ.

## Related Organizations

- [[Brittania]] — Lucerin's foresight is the foundation of the kingdom's hope for a utopian future; the prophecy "his eyes will guide us to a better tomorrow" is tied to it
