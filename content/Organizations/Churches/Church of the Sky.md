## Overview

One of the four major churches of [[Brittania]]. The Church of the Sky worships [[Indra]], God of the Sky. Controlled by a noble family appointed by [[The High Septon]].

## Relationships

- [[Indra]] — deity worshipped
- [[The High Septon]] — placed a noble family in control of this church
- [[Lanius]] — Chosen of Indra *(unconfirmed)* and [[Veyrana]]
- [[Pantheon of Gods]] — Indra is a member
