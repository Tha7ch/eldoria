## Overview

One of the four major churches of [[Brittania]]. The Church of the Storm worships [[Veyrana]], Goddess of Storms. Controlled by a noble family appointed by [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]]. [[Aella Galefri]] grew up within the church before being banished to [[Elsweyr]].

## Leadership

- [[Torr Galefri]] — current head of the church

## Known Members

- [[Aella Galefri]] — former member; banished *(circumstances unknown — her father leads the church)*
- [[Lanius]] — Chosen of Veyrana; operates outside the institutional church entirely; no formal connection to it
