## Overview

The Kingsguard is an elite group of five royal protectors handpicked by the king of [[Brittania]] to serve and protect the crown. Established by [[Raevyn Whitlocke|Raevyn the Redeemer]] in 150CC. Currently serves [[Lucerin Atreides]].

## Leadership

- [[Manuel]] — Lord Protector of the King's Guard

## Goals & Ideology

Protection of the Brittanian crown and royal family. Absolute loyalty to the king.

## Known Members

- [[Manuel]] — Lord Protector
- [[Damian Grimm]] — Blade of the King
- [[Kylo Wisemoon]] — Royal Protector of the Royal Family
- [[Jeane Howelle]] — Queen's Guard
- [[Lucius Raymor]] — Chosen of Aria

## History

Founded by [[Raevyn Whitlocke|Raevyn the Redeemer]] in 150CC. Current incarnation serves the young king Lucerin Atreides following the assassination of the rest of the royal family.
