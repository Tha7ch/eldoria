## Overview

A revolutionary group in [[Brittania]] that wants to topple tyrannical rule. Founded by people who began patrolling the slums after mistreatment by higher-ups became widespread — the group grew from grassroots protection into an organized resistance. [[Lanius]], a chosen of [[Veyrana]], is associated with them. [[Jeane Howelle]] unofficially pointed [[Aella Galefri]] toward them to find Lanius.

## Leadership

- [[Lanius]] — head of the organization
- [[Roland Royce]] — acting chief; runs the organization day-to-day

## Goals & Ideology

End the classism of [[Brittania]] — by any means necessary. They started as people protecting slum residents from abuse and evolved into an organized force willing to do whatever it takes to dismantle the systems that enable it. Not a gentle reform movement.

**Motto:** *"It is the duty of the strong to protect the weak."*

## Known Members

- [[Lanius]] — chosen of Veyrana, brawler fighter

## Associates

- [[Callum]] — collaborator; not a full member, shares common goals and works with them on occasion
- [[Veyr]] — information broker; not aligned with their cause and views them as dangerous idealists, but sold [[Lanius]] information once for an absurd price, which he paid

## History

Founded by people patrolling the slums against mistreatment — higher-ups in Brittania's power structure were involved in abusing slum residents. The group evolved from protection into organized resistance. Stories about them circulate in Cyris's knowledge from Strixhaven.

## Relationships

- [[Brittania]] — in opposition to its ruling establishment
- [[King's Crown]] — likely their primary target *(unconfirmed)*
