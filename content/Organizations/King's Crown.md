## Overview

King's Crown is the ruling council of [[Brittania]], identified by a six-pronged crown sigil representing its six members. They are actively grooming [[Lucerin Atreides]] to lead the country in their direction. Lucerin himself suspects they may be responsible for the massacre of his family. They cannot be trusted.

## Known Members

- [[Archibald Howell]] — The High Septon; pope-like religious authority; monetized the four major churches and placed noble families in control of them
- [[Veritas Aletheia]] — The Grand Arbiter; wields a manifesting scale
- [[Oldric Ameris]] — The Warmaster; High General of the Brittanian Army
- [[Elric Fletcher]] — The Archmaester; the warmest of the council members
- [[Manuel]] — The Lord Protector; recently inducted in place of someone unknown
- *Unknown* — the sixth member, currently away

## Goals & Ideology

To control Brittania from behind the throne. Using the young king as a puppet.

## History

Six members, six-pronged crown sigil. Have positioned themselves as Lucerin's greatest allies while potentially orchestrating his family's deaths. The High Septon's control over the church infrastructure gives King's Crown significant religious and financial reach across the country.

## Relationships

- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — ostensibly his council; actually his greatest threat
- [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]] — member; controls religious apparatus
- [[The Great Houses]] — several noble families likely involved or complicit
