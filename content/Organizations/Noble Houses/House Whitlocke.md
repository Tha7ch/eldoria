## Overview

House Whitlocke are the direct descendants of [[Raevyn Whitlocke]] — the legendary founder of [[The Kingsguard]]. By divine law, they are strictly forbidden from having more than one heir at a time. Little is publicly known about the house beyond folklore. They hold the legendary weapon **Heaven's Hope**.

## Known Members

- [[Raevyn Whitlocke]] — the original founder of the Kingsguard; their legendary ancestor
