The great noble houses of [[Brittania]] — the highest level of nobility beneath royal blood. *(Structure still being established — more detail coming.)*

**Upper Echelon Houses:**
[[House Ameris]] — second most important house after the Royal Family; chosen successors to the throne; lunar and star magic; a third of the Brittanian army
[[House Raymor]] — upper echelon; tied to the [[Church of Light]]; celestial and light magic; many knights devoted to Aria
[[House Aberra]] — upper echelon; the other half of the Church of Light; specializes in unique healing magic
[[House St. James]] — founded by Magnus St. James, one of the ancient seven heroes; currently has a Chosen of Mara and the largest guild in Brittania

**Other Noble Houses:**
[[House Morningstar]] — powerful mages; Headmaster of Strixhaven; benefactor of Strixhaven alongside House Hope
[[House Justicar]] — half-elven house; represents the [[Church of Love]]; led by High Priestess Vera
[[House Howelle]] — proud tradition of powerful women; produced Lady Jean, the Queen's Guard; no male heirs by curse
[[House Greenleaf]] — emissary house from the Elven Kingdom; houses Orelia Greenleaf, one of the ten strongest in Eldoria
[[House Hope]] — Tiefling house from the Badlands; specializes in magic; benefactor of Strixhaven
[[House Blackthorne]] — Redacted
[[House Whitlocke]] — descendants of Raevyn Whitlocke, founder of the Kingsguard; divinely limited to one heir; hold the legendary weapon Heaven's Hope
[[House Lyons]] — newly formed (last 100 years); houses Lucas Lyons, the Lionheart, Chosen of Helios and one of the ten strongest in Eldoria
[[House Dawnmere]] — house of dawn and aurora magic; massacred 3 years ago; Tirian sole survivor (Campaign 2)
[[House Galefri]] — [[Church of the Storm]] family; Torr is head; Aella estranged (Campaign 1)
[[House Irvenest]] — major royal family; Kaisel's adopted family (Campaign 2)
