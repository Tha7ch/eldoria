## Overview

House Lyons is a newly formed noble house, established within the last hundred years and therefore lacking major historical lineage. Despite this, the house currently boasts [[Lucas Lyons]] — known as the Lionheart and Chosen of [[Helios]] — one of the ten strongest people in all of Eldoria.

## Known Members

- [[Lucas Lyons]] — the Lionheart; Chosen of Helios; one of the ten strongest in Eldoria
