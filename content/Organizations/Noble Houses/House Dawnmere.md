## Overview

House Dawnmere was a noble house centered on the ideals of hope, healing, beauty, heroism, and noble leadership. Three years before Campaign 2, the house was massacred — its members murdered in an organized attack. [[Tirian Dawnmere]] is the sole surviving heir and is determined to rebuild the house's legacy.

The house was best known publicly for its dazzling displays of aurora magic — [[Auris Dawnmere]]'s sweeping aurora performances were the most popular and recognized in the family's history, though [[Sorin Dawnmere]]'s fire magic and [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s ice magic were also considered beautiful. Sorin's record as a powerful fighter for the kingdom in its wars gave the house a reputation for combat prowess as well.

## History

Among the noble houses, House Dawnmere was considered the black sheep — looked down on or whispered about by some other houses for its ideals and its sympathy toward commoners, though House Dawnmere remained on friendly terms with most of the Great Houses despite this. Among the common people, it was beloved — in part because [[Lirien Dawnmere]], the lady of the house, was herself a commoner by birth, and in part because of the house's beloved public magic performances. This reputation was noted even by people far outside noble circles, including [[Lanius]], who encountered the contrast firsthand during his time in [[Caer Brittanis]]'s slums.

Three years before Campaign 2, House Dawnmere was massacred. The assassins who carried out the killings are dead. The individuals who ordered the murders are unknown but alive. Tirian, who was approximately 20 at the time, survived and has since worked to uncover the conspiracy while publicly maintaining the house's legacy.
