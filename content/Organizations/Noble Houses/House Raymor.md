## Overview

House Raymor is a noble house in [[Brittania]], known for celestial and light magic. [[Lucius Raymor]] is a member — the Chosen of [[Aria]], Goddess of Light — and his celestial/light affinity aligns naturally with the house's magical tradition.

## Known Members

- [[Silas Raymor]] — Duke; head of House Raymor; formerly the paladin Silas the Strong
- [[Lucius Raymor]] — Chosen of Aria; member of [[The Kingsguard]]
- [[Asterin Raymor]] — High Priest of the [[Church of Light]]
- [[Aevos]] — member of House Raymor but does not carry the name; introduces himself as Aevos Raymor; Church of Light champion (Campaign 2)

## Relationships

- [[The Great Houses]] — member
- [[Brittania]] — serves the crown
- [[House Aberra]] — Lucius's mother is from House Aberra, making him a member of both
