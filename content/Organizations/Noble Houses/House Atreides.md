## Overview

The Royal Family Atreides are the ruling dynasty of [[Brittania]], descended from **King [[Arthur Atreides]]** — the last king of the Ancient Kingdom of [[Camelot]]. Chosen by the Celestial Realm to lead humanity into an era of prosperity, the Atreides line has always possessed abnormal magic types that mark them as something beyond ordinary nobility. They have ruled with fairness and genuine concern for the wellbeing of the kingdom.

Throughout almost their entire rule, they have been aided by [[House Whitlocke]].

## History

Descended from King [[Arthur Atreides]] of the Ancient Kingdom of [[Camelot]]. The Celestial Realm chose the Atreides line to guide humanity, and the family has historically honoured that mandate — ruling with fairness rather than self-interest. Their abnormal magic types across generations are widely believed to be a mark of that divine selection.

The family was entirely wiped out approximately one month before the campaign begins — [[Leo Atreides]], [[Myrcella Atreides]], and all their children except [[Lucerin Atreides]], who survived only because he had secretly slipped away to watch a magic duel at [[Strixhaven]]. [[Oldric Ameris]], Leo's brother — born Oldric Atreides, but having married into [[House Ameris]] and given up the Atreides name — subsequently surrendered his claim to the throne, passing it to Lucerin.
