## Overview

House Justicar is a noble house made up of half-elves, representing the [[Church of Love]] and the goddess [[Mara]]. The house is led by High Priestess [[Vera Justicar]].

## Known Members

- [[Vera Justicar]] — High Priestess; leads the Church of Love; one of the few mortals who routinely converses with a goddess
- [[Vero Justicar]] — Vera's son; travels Brittania and Eldoria spreading the word of Mara

## Relationships

- [[The Great Houses]] — member
- [[Church of Love]] — their church
- [[Mara]] — their goddess
