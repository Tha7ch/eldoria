## Overview

House Galefri is a noble house in [[Brittania]] with strong ties to the [[Church of the Storm]] and [[Veyrana]], Goddess of Storms. The current head of the house is [[Torr Galefri]], who also leads the Church of the Storm. [[Aella Galefri]] is a member by birth but actively avoids using the family name — it carries associations she wants no part of.

## Known Members

- [[Torr Galefri]] — head of the house and head of [[Church of the Storm]]
- [[Arashi Galefri]] — eldest son
- [[Whiro Galefri]] — second son
- [[Aella Galefri]] — daughter; estranged, banished from the church, does not use the name
- [[Urami Galefri]] — wife of Torr

## Relationships

- [[Church of the Storm]] — deeply intertwined; Torr leads both
- [[The Great Houses]] — member *(unconfirmed)*
- [[Veyrana]] — the house's divine patron
