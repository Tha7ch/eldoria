## Overview

[[House Ameris]] is a military noble house in [[Brittania]], known for lunar and star magic. Named by [[Lucerin Atreides]] as one of the somewhat trustworthy houses alongside [[House St. James]] and [[House Howelle]].

## Leadership

- [[Oldric Ameris]] — War Master of [[King's Crown]]; High General of the Brittanian Army; senior-most member
- [[Lumiere Ameris]] — most notable current member among the younger generation; military commander

## Goals & Ideology

Military excellence. Known for producing powerful commanders.

## Known Members

- [[Lumiere Ameris]] — military commander, 13–0 in campaigns
- [[Oldric Ameris]] — War Master of [[King's Crown]]; High General of the Brittanian Army; born Oldric Atreides, brother of the late [[Leo Atreides]], married into House Ameris and gave up the Atreides name; father of [[Lumiere Ameris]]

## History

Produced [[Lumiere Ameris]], who fought the Battle of the Blackwood — 40,000 vs 100,000 against a country of orcs. Active in military campaigns over the last 3 years.

## Relationships

- [[The Great Houses]] — member
- [[Brittania]] — serves the crown; considered trustworthy by Lucerin
- [[House Atreides]] — tied by marriage through [[Oldric Ameris]], who was born into House Atreides
