## Overview

A noble house in [[Brittania]] with a proud tradition of powerful women. One of [[The Great Houses]]. Named by [[Lucerin Atreides]] as one of the somewhat trustworthy houses. House Howelle is cursed to produce only daughters — no male heir has ever been born into the line. This has not hindered them in the slightest.

## Known Members

- [[Olenna Howelle]] — grandmother of Jeane; significant political influence; married to [[The High Septon]]
- [[Jeane Howelle]] — Queen's Guard of [[The Kingsguard]]
- [[Lanius]] — ward (adopted from the slums of [[Caer Brittanis]] as a child); grew up alongside Jeane; later founded [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]

## History

Three years before Campaign 2, House Howelle hosted a gala announcing the engagement of [[Jeane Howelle]] and [[Lanius]] — a surprise to most of the city, who hadn't expected the household's quiet ward to be the news of the evening. He had been considered for [[The Kingsguard]] at one point but ultimately passed over. The gala took place the same night [[House Dawnmere]] was massacred; [[Tirian Dawnmere]] was in attendance and received word of the slaughter there. *(unconfirmed whether the engagement ultimately proceeded — see [[Tirian - One Night]])*
