## Overview

House Morningstar is a noble house that has produced powerful mages throughout its lineage. Blessed by the arcane and its limitless potential, House Morningstar leads Brittania's efforts to push the limits of spellcasting and magical-infused technology. They are a benefactor of [[Strixhaven]] alongside [[House Hope]].

## Known Members

- [[Karsus Morningstar]] — Headmaster of Strixhaven and Grand Mage of Brittania
- [[Merlin Morningstar]] — "The Prodigy of Potential"; Karsus's son; Strixhaven student; constantly pushes the arcane to new heights

- [[Strixhaven]] — major benefactor
