## Overview

The Chosen are mortals selected by the gods of the [[Pantheon of Gods]] to serve as their earthly champions. Each god may select a Chosen to carry their divine power and act in their name. Being Chosen is not a formal organization — there is no shared hierarchy, no collective agenda. What unites them is the weight of divine selection and the extraordinary power that comes with it. However, they do hold round-table meetings on occasion — [[Lumiere Ameris]] called one at the start of Campaign 2 to warn the others of incoming danger.

## Notes

- All living Chosen are remarkably young — the oldest is [[Lanius]] at 28, the youngest are [[Kaisel Irvenest]] and [[Lucas Lyons]] at 20.
- [[Aria]] is the only god known to have had multiple Chosen across generations (Seyrell, then both Lucius and Aevos).
- [[Lanius]] is the only known dual Chosen, selected by both [[Veyrana]] and [[Indra]] *(unconfirmed)*.
- Being Chosen does not appear on the [[Magical Geniuses Ranking]] by default — [[Lanius]] is excluded from it because his strength is raw elemental force rather than magical innovation.
