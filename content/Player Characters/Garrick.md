## Overview

Garrick is a Monk/Rogue played by Gio in Campaign 2. At 16, he is the youngest of the Campaign 2 player characters. He was trained by a master whose core teaching shaped everything about how Garrick fights and learns.

## Appearance

- Short wild spiky dark brown hair — voluminous and energetic
- Dark amber/golden-brown eyes — warm and bright; carries a faint suppressed smile as a default
- Clearly young — softer jaw, youthful face, not yet hardened; looks his age
- Slightly pointed elf ears visible through the hair
- Thin gold ornamental headband with intricate geometric/filigree detailing worn across the forehead
- Black martial gi/kimono-style robe with gold-stitched trim at the collar and seams, crossover front
- Braided leather belt at the waist with a small round bronze medallion hanging from it
- Dark wrist wraps on both forearms

## Personality

A deep, book-fed curiosity about the world — the kind that builds up in someone who read everything but was rarely allowed to go anywhere. The sheltered upbringing didn't dampen his hunger for the world; it sharpened it.

## History

Garrick grew up in a humble elven household at the edge of an ancient forest — close, hardworking, and full of stories told around the fireplace each evening. He wasn't permitted to venture far; his world was small by design. So he expanded it the only way he could: through books and scrolls, devouring everything he could find on history, geography, ancient civilizations, monsters, kingdoms, languages, and — above all — martial arts. It built in him a curiosity about everything beyond his reach that bordered on hunger. Notably, neither of his parents could use magic, and the family built their lives around what they could earn through effort rather than what they lacked *(unconfirmed whether this holds true for Garrick himself)*.

His combat training came from home, not from any academy. His father sparred with him every afternoon for years — footwork, falling safely, striking, and eventually full exchanges that ended with Garrick face-down in the dirt more often than not. His mother drilled him in balance and agility. It was relentless and unglamorous, entirely his family's own making. By sixteen, that daily discipline had carried him to the level of a capable amateur fighter — enough to hold his own, though not yet anything more.

Shortly after his sixteenth birthday, Garrick set out to explore the mountains beyond his village and discovered a forgotten temple buried in the overgrowth. Inside, he found an old monk practicing a martial art unlike anything he had ever read about or seen — and he knew immediately it was what he'd been searching for without realizing it. He asked the monk to teach him. The monk warned him plainly of what the path would cost — exhaustion, early mornings, days of doubting it was worth it — and Garrick answered that finding out what existed beyond his limits was exactly what he wanted. The monk agreed, on the condition that Garrick prove himself first, with a test before sunrise the next morning. *(Full account: [[Garrick - The Boy Who Chased the Unknown]])*

His master took that foundation and rebuilt it around a single principle: **learn from every battle, no matter the gap in skill between you and your opponent.** Whether facing someone weaker or vastly stronger, every fight is information. Every loss is a lesson. Every gap in skill is a distance to close. The master didn't just teach him to fight — he taught him to study combat itself.
