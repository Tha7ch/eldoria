## Overview

Aeron Velmont is a half-elf noble from [[House Velmont]], assigned to the party by the [[King's Crown]] — specifically by [[Archibald Howell|the High Septon]] — as a replacement for [[Kieran Arden]]. [[Manuel]] and [[Cyris Voss]] both recognize his name. He joins the group despite the circumstances of his appointment being somewhat forced.

## Appearance

- 5'9, approximately 20 years old
- Half-elf
- Dark brown curly/wavy hair, medium-short and loose
- Bright olive-green eyes
- Three visible scars on his face: one running across the bridge of his nose; one cutting through his left eye (the eye itself undamaged); and a fresh, ragged scar on his cheek — the most visually striking of the three, from the primal creature attack *(see Mysteries below)*
- Medium-brown/olive skin
- Warm, approachable smile
- High-quality tailored leather armor
- Two sheathed swords worn on his back
- A few pouches on his belt
- Looks pampered and noble — the bearing of someone who grew up with money

## Personality

Noble and composed. Gave a formal bow upon meeting the King's Crown. Aware that his addition to the party was forced and diplomatically acknowledged it. Star-struck around [[Lucas Lyons]]. Comfortable with [[Lucas Lyons|Lucas]] — the two appear to know each other.

## History

Son of a noble family from [[House Velmont]]. His father is described as "chipper as ever." His mother is a researcher of old ancient nature magic and magic artifacts — [[Cyris Voss]] perked up at this. He was out in the woods approximately three days before his introduction when he was attacked by a creature he describes as "tall and lank — like a primal beast." The attack left a fresh scar on his face from which [[Cyris Voss]], [[Kip]], and [[Aella Galefri]] all detected primal magic — and not in a good way.

Manuel knows Aeron and his family, though they hadn't talked much before. [[Ludwig St. James]] apparently approved of his assignment without objection.

## Relationships

- [[House Velmont]] — his noble family
- [[Manuel]] — knows him through prior acquaintance; Manuel introduced him to the group
- [[Lucas Lyons]] — clearly familiar with each other; Lucas called him out immediately and threw an arm around him; Aeron was star-struck
- [[Cyris Voss]] — recognized the name with a look of recognition

## Story

**Session 9:** Introduced by the High Septon to the party as Kieran's replacement. Assigned by King's Crown over the party's objection that the king should decide. Attended the King's Crown meeting and then immediately joined the group. Revealed the fresh scar on his face from a creature attack three days prior.

## Build

Hollow Warden Ranger. Khoravar — a half-elf who identifies as neither human nor elf, but as their own people.
