## Overview

Leandros "Leo" Thalassor is a wood half-elf swashbuckler and sea-blessed warlock. Years ago he drowned in a supernatural shipwreck and was saved by [[Melan Thalassa]], an ancient and monstrous primordial sea goddess who marked him as her champion. Now Leo sails through life chasing freedom, treasure, glory, and adventure — while trying to prove that not even the goddess who saved him can truly own him.

## Appearance

- Warm tan skin; heroic, sun-warmed, sea-born presence
- Loose curly black hair — thick, dark, naturally tousled by wind and salt; falls in loose curls around his forehead and ears, never past his shoulders
- Storm gray-blue eyes (natural); flash sea-green when his magic awakens
- Athletic swimmer-duelist build — broad shoulders, narrow waist, toned
- Subtly pointed half-elven ears
- High cheekbones, expressive eyes, charming mouth — Greek heroic beauty blended with elven sharpness
- A reckless, charming smile; looks like someone who belongs on the deck of a ship in a supernatural storm, grinning like the waves are cheering him on

**Outfit:** Open-collared cream or white shirt beneath a fitted sea-black pirate coat. Leather belts and sword harness, sea-green sash, tall worn boots, bronze and gold jewelry, shell and coral details, light studded leather worked into the outfit. Glasswake at his hip. Adventurous, romantic, stylish, dangerous.

**Magic appearance:** When his magic awakens, his storm-gray eyes flash sea-green. Luminous tide glyphs ripple over his skin. Spectral water curls around his blade like living surf. Beautiful at first glance — radiant, fluid, almost divine — but anyone watching too closely sees something older beneath it: abyssal shadows, impossible shapes in the foam, and the suggestion of a vast feminine smile under dark water.

## Personality

Heroic sea-brat with a soft-hearted menace streak. **Cocky, flirty, and ambitious** at his core — he wants glory, he pursues it openly, and he has no interest in pretending otherwise. Brave, impulsive, playful, good-hearted, protective, reckless, vain enough to enjoy glory, greedy enough to appreciate treasure, sincere underneath the swagger.

He acts like he only wants freedom, adventure, glory, and money — but when people are in danger, he does the right thing. He hates being controlled by captains, kings, tyrants, slavers, corrupt nobles, or even gods.

## History — The Pact

Leo was aboard a ship caught in a storm that should not have existed — black clouds, green lightning, waves like cathedral walls, and a voice singing beneath the thunder. The ship broke apart in the dark. He was dragged under, lungs burning, body crushed by cold, sinking into water so deep it felt like falling through the sky.

That was where [[Melan Thalassa]] found him.

She appeared first as a woman's voice in the storm — seductive, amused, ancient, and terrifyingly intimate. She offered him life, power, and the promise that the sea would never fully reject him again.

Leo accepted.

## Relationships

- [[Melan Thalassa]] — his warlock patron; ancient sea goddess who found him drowning and offered him a pact. Their relationship began as a debt of survival and became something stranger. Leo is grateful, fascinated, and wary. He does not want to belong to anyone — but he cannot deny she saved him and chose him. She treats him like a favorite blade, a beautiful mistake, a reckless little champion. Something she may genuinely adore. Something she may eventually consume. Every storm seems to carry her voice a little closer. **He cannot be away from the sea for more than 72 hours** — if he is, Melan grows too jealous, and her jealousy has consequences.

## Goals

- Freedom
- Adventure
- Glory
- Money
- Answers about [[Melan Thalassa]] — what she truly is, what she truly wants from him

He refuses to be owned, even by a goddess. But he is drawn to her mystery.

Extremely agile, charismatic, and durable. A fast duelist, strong face character, and surprisingly sturdy skirmisher.

**Combat loop:** Move in with rapier → Booming Blade or attack → Sneak Attack → Fancy Footwork to disengage → Tentacle of the Deeps for pressure → warlock magic for control and defense. Not a tank — a slippery high-charisma duelist who punishes enemies for chasing him.

**AC:** 17 with studded leather and 20 DEX. 18 with Armor of Shadows.

**Weapon loadout:**
- **Glasswake** (rapier) — primary signature weapon
- Scimitar + Shortsword — aggressive dual-wielding option
- Shortbow — ranged backup

**Glasswake:** Leo's signature sea-glass rapier.
- Silver or pale bronze swept hilt
- Sea-green gemstone set in the guard
- Translucent blue-green blade with a sheen like polished glass under waves
- Faint foam-like glow when drawn
- Tiny etched wave patterns along the fuller
- Dark leather grip, worn from actual use
- Beautiful, but not fragile

**Weapon mastery:** Rapier (Vex) as primary — sets up advantage on future attacks. Scimitar (Nick) as secondary — extra attack without spending the bonus action.

**Feats considered:** Fey Touched *(strongest — +1 CHA, Misty Step, bonus spell)*, Shadow Touched, Skill Expert, Actor, Telepathic, Elven Accuracy.
