## Overview

Cyris Voss is a 19-year-old Purple Tiefling artificer/wizard from [[The Badlands]], raised by his parents until age 7, then taken in by merchant [[Roland Voss]] and brought to [[Brittania]]. He earned a scholarship to [[Strixhaven]] where he excels academically despite facing constant prejudice. He is enrolled in Prismari, one of Strixhaven's schools, and is nicknamed the **Prismari Prodigy**. At 19, he is a 2nd year student. He has a gift for time magic and recently developed a spell capable of rewinding a small object ten seconds in time. He currently lives in [[Strixhaven]]'s dormitories, located in [[Caer Brittanis]].

- Glowing yellow eyes with slit pupils
- Curved, ram-like horns emerging from his temples, pale beige — right horn is broken off
- Pointed, elven-like ears

- Small conservative gold hoop earrings
- Expression: a somber, knowing smile

## Personality

Intelligent, principled, and gentle. Driven by obsessive curiosity and a deep need to justify the sacrifices that brought him where he is. He fights not for glory but to protect. Despite academic excellence he is reserved and nervous in social situations. Faces constant prejudice for his tiefling heritage, poverty, and Badlands origin — yet has not grown bitter.

## History

Born in [[The Badlands]] to [[Dorn Ashkel]] and [[Serapha Ashkel]], a warm and devoted tiefling couple who nurtured his curiosity. At age 7, his parents were killed fighting a mutated wasteland monster. They saved him, but both died of their wounds. His right horn was shattered in the attack — a visible mark he carries still.

He survived alone by tinkering and selling small enchanted devices to travelers. At age 9, wandering merchant [[Roland Voss]] recognized his talent and offered him passage to [[Brittania]], education, and stability — in exchange for taking Roland's surname. Cyris Ashkel became **Cyris Voss**.

Roland was not warm but not unkind — he saw Cyris as an investment. He drilled Cyris relentlessly toward [[Strixhaven]], which Cyris reached on a hard-won scholarship. At Strixhaven he flourished academically while fighting constant discrimination. He secured a position in a time magic research lab, where he developed a spell that rewinds a single small object ten seconds — a noted breakthrough.

## Story

**Session 8:** After the parade attack, searched the city with Aella, Seros, Kip, and Kylo for Damian — following a clear path of destruction. Found Damian mangled in a bloody crater; Healing Word burned rather than healed him (blood magic incompatibility). Scooped up by Seros; the group agreed to hide Damian's state from Manuel. At the castle crossroads, went to the throne room to check on Lucerin. Immediately tried to resign to Manuel — told him about Kieran's death and the magical barrier neither Kylo nor Damian could break. Covered for Damian's condition when Manuel asked. In the distillery afterward: set Solstice's drink on fire to test alcohol content (she froze the next one as retaliation); got hit by Kip's mashed potato prank instead of Solstice; catapulted a chicken leg at Kip in response. Tried to drink and discovered he's too young — slid the drink to Aella.

**Session 4:** Purchased a Chronolometer (1250g), Clockwork Amulet, and Failed Experiment Wand. Ludwig correctly identified him as a time wizard. During the rooftop ambush, shot two water bullets to knock an assassin into crates below, then held a gun to a downed assassin's head. His Steel Defender jumped from the roof to land on a second assassin.

**Session 2:** Arrested after a bar brawl. Released by the Kingsguard on behalf of King Lucerin. Recognized by [[Kylo Wisemoon]] from his Strixhaven reputation. When Lucerin demonstrated primordial magic on Kieran, Cyris identified it immediately. Experienced a déjà vu — had seen this exact moment in a vision before, with one difference: Kieran looked completely different. Received a letter from [[Damian Grimm]] to excuse him from class. [[Aella Galefri]] noticed Cyris's magic felt similar in wavelength to Damian's. Agreed to join the [[Honest Hearts]].

## Build

Likely multiclass: **Chronurgy Wizard** (fits time magic research) + **Battle Smith Artificer** (tinkering, invention, protecting others). Background: **Haunted One** — grew up in the Badlands, has seen horrors; inspires sympathy and admiration from common people.

Notable spell — *Chronal Fracture*: draws a thin line through a creature's timeline, causing disadvantage on next roll (Temporal Recoil), or preventing reactions (Fate Stutter).

Personal spell under development: rewinding a single object or creature's body 10 seconds — could function as an emergency heal.
