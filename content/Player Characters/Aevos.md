## Overview

Aevos is an Aasimar Sorcerer played by Jeff in Campaign 2. He is one of the [[Church of Light]]'s greatest champions — a willing experiment who voluntarily underwent a series of experimental divine rites as a child to strengthen the bond between mortal souls and celestial power. The experiments succeeded beyond all expectations, dramatically amplifying his celestial abilities. To the public he is a radiant symbol of hope and faith. To the Church hierarchy, he is living proof that their work succeeded. He is not a victim — he chose this path.

## Appearance

**Normal form:**
- Medium-length white hair — silky, slightly wavy, windswept, falls around the neck or just above the shoulders; refined and angelic without being excessive
- Molten gold irises with a subtle sapphire-blue ring around the outer edge — the blue noticeable upon closer inspection, like sunlight surrounded by a celestial halo
- Strong jawline, elegant bone structure, refined handsome features — mature and charismatic rather than youthful
- Wears a simple gold band ring with delicate decorative etchings that converge on a unique holy/magical symbol — a gift from his mother [[Seyrell]]. *(Unknown to Aevos: [[Aella Galefri]] has the matching ring.)*
- Luxurious dark robes: deep navy and black fabrics with intricate gold embroidery, ornate shoulder pieces, celestial motifs, sacred jewelry, layered ceremonial cloth — elegant, noble, and holy yet practical
- Wings hidden; no visible celestial manifestation unless he chooses

**Aasimar form** *(during intense magic, prayer, or strong emotion)*:
- His hair grows longer — soft, flowing, past his shoulders, silky and voluminous, moving with even the slightest breeze
- **Large wings of radiant white-gold light** manifest behind him — luminous, angelic, majestic; the wings of an angel rather than a bird
- The air around him shimmers with celestial energy and golden embers

## Magic

**Spellfire** — his primary power, dramatically amplified by the Church's divine experiments. Manifests as **blue flames** — vibrant azure, with thin streaks and sparks of divine gold woven through like threads of liquid sunlight. The gold is a hint, not a dominant color. Feels holy, beautiful, and awe-inspiring rather than destructive or demonic.

## Personality

Charismatic and suave — confident, but not in a loud way. As a Chosen of [[Aria]], his presence is naturally commanding, and he's fully aware of the effect he has on a room; that awareness feeds directly into his personality. He acts before others finish debating, and doesn't apologize for it. But he is also eloquent, educated, and completely put together when he speaks. The confidence is not bluster; it's the settled certainty of someone raised to believe in his own greatness who has since had every reason to keep believing it. He openly acknowledges his gifts and sees them as tools given to him for a purpose. He does not perform humility.

He has a good eye for a person's true nature — a read on character that helps him decide who will benefit from his light, and who will be smited by it.

Beneath the confidence lies genuine compassion and a sincere desire to help people. He wants to be a hero, and he takes that responsibility seriously. He is willing to get his hands dirty for what he believes is just — his morality is not passive or ceremonial.

## History

Aevos is a member of [[House Raymor]] but does not carry the family name. He often introduces himself as **Aevos Raymor** in social situations to avoid the headache of explaining the distinction.

He was sheltered away for much of his childhood, kept largely out of public view. It was only four years ago — when he was 21 — that he first appeared publicly. To the world, his emergence was striking: a radiant Aasimar champion of the [[Church of Light]] with extraordinary power.

Among other nobles, he is perceived as a sheltered Raymor — someone who was kept away and protected, and is only now stepping into the world. Whether they underestimate him because of it is their problem.

As a child, Aevos willingly volunteered for a series of experimental divine rites conducted by the [[Church of Light]]. The Church sought to better understand and strengthen the connection between mortal souls and celestial power. While others feared the risks, he eagerly accepted — believing that if sacrifices were necessary, he should be the one to bear them. The experiments succeeded beyond anyone's expectations, transforming him into one of the Church's most powerful assets.

Was present, alongside [[Tirian Dawnmere]], [[Lumiere Ameris]], [[Ludwig St. James]], and [[Damian Grimm]], at the [[House Howelle]] gala the night of [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] — see [[Tirian - One Night]].
