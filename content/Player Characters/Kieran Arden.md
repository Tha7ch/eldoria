## Overview

Kieran Arden is a dhampir paladin/bard from [[Brittania]]. Reserved, somewhat curious, carries a red marking on his face of unknown origin. Has a brother in the military.

## Appearance

- Male Dhampir
- Red splotch/marking on his eye *(partially healed by Lucerin in Session 2)*

## Personality

Somewhat curious and edgy. True neutral disposition. Calm under pressure — didn't flinch when Damian used blood magic to open his cell.

## History

From [[Brittania]]. His family ran an apothecary. His father was an investigator. Has a brother in the military.

## Relationships

- [[Damian Grimm]] — romantic relationship begun in Session 5; first noticed each other in Session 2 over their shared connection to forbidden/unusual power; Damian has "recognized that look" in Kieran from the start
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — Lucerin briefly removed/masked his face marking using primordial magic

## Story

**Session 7:** Targeted by [[Hannibal]] and trapped in a purple cube with the Warden. Fought back but was overwhelmed — teeth knocked out, arm cracked audibly, body mangled. Tossed out of the cube when Hannibal declared he was "over the limit." **Presumed dead.** Kip puked upon seeing the state of his body.

**Session 5:** Stayed present during Damian's interrogation aftermath. Told Damian he should have used a different method. Damian came to his room that night — they bonded over controlled anger, and began a romantic relationship.

**Session 2:** In prison — a guard mocked his face marking. Released by Damian, who used blood magic to form a key. Knelt before Lucerin when asked; Lucerin touched his face marking and temporarily healed/hid it, then handed him a mirror. Exchanged a significant look with Damian on departure.

## Build

Paladin / Bard multiclass. *(Subclasses unknown.)* Uses an elementally-infused blade in combat.
