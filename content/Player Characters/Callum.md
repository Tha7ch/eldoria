## Overview

Callum, nicknamed Cal, is a physical fighter played by Bonner in Campaign 2. He is from [[The Badlands]].

## Appearance

- 6'1", toned and muscular — lean but powerful, built through hard living rather than training regimens
- Hair sits somewhere between dirty blonde and dark brown — short and messy, shifts depending on the light
- Amber-brown eyes, sharp and steady
- Tan, darker skin
- Scar on his cheek
- Wears a worn, open-collared white shirt, practical and well-used — simple, light clothing overall
- A ragged rust-orange scarf/wrap draped around his neck and shoulders, tattered at the ends
- Dark pants, heavy leather boots with buckle and lace detailing
- Leather wrist wraps on both arms
- A belt with pouches and holsters
- **A pendant on a chain around his neck** — not just jewelry, but a relic: when activated, it can morph into any weapon of its wearer's choosing. *(The sword he's most often seen carrying is very likely this relic in its current preferred shape.)*
- Overall aesthetic: rugged Badlands survivor, worn practical clothing — nothing noble about it

## Personality

Cal's defining value is **freedom** — he despises anything that restricts it: religion, government, tradition, or any institution that tells people how to live. The feeling is instinctive, woven into him at a level deeper than memory. Beside it sits an equally core belief: that **strength is the only path to true freedom**. Without the power to defend it, freedom is just an illusion waiting to be taken away. That belief gives him real courage — he'll stand against impossible odds without hesitating, and once he commits to something he follows it relentlessly. He's resilient, hard to break, and fiercely protective of anyone he sees being oppressed or controlled, holding that everyone deserves the chance to choose their own path. Status means nothing to him; a king and a peasant are equals in his eyes, judged only by character and the willingness to struggle. He is authentic to a fault — no masks, no shifting who he is to please others — and prizes honesty, self-determination, and personal responsibility. Above everything, he's fiercely independent, trusting his own ability over anyone else's hands.

That same devotion has a darker edge. Cal's love of freedom can turn self-destructive — he instinctively bristles against anything imposed on him, even good advice or reasonable compromise, and ends up creating obstacles for himself that didn't need to exist. His independence shades into isolation: he struggles to trust, and rarely lets himself lean on anyone, because dependence feels uncomfortably close to losing control. He cannot tolerate weakness — especially in himself — and pushes past his own limits rather than admit to vulnerability or failure. Manipulation and control are the things he fears most; even harmless commitments can start to feel like cages closing around him. He also has a blind spot: he instinctively measures worth by physical strength, determination, and survival, undervaluing the kind of strength found in artists, scholars, or anyone whose power isn't obvious — though he'd never admit it outright. And at the center of it all sits a contradiction he hasn't resolved: he hates being controlled and fears becoming someone else's pawn, yet his convictions can just as easily turn into him forcing his ideals onto others. The stronger he gets, the more he risks becoming the very thing he despises — someone who decides what freedom should look like for everyone else.

## History

Cal doesn't remember where he came from. He woke with no name beyond "Cal," no memory of family, home, or how he came to be — only fragments too foggy to hold onto: a castle in the distance, golden banners, swords and shields, sunlight. He came back to consciousness inside a cave, sealed in glowing blue crystal that shattered around him as his senses came roaring back all at once — cold, sound, smell, feeling, life, after what felt like nothing at all.

The first person he ever (knowingly) met was [[Roland Royce]], who found him standing dazed among the wreckage of his own prison. Where anyone else might have panicked, Cal didn't — and Roland noticed. He laid out what the world outside the cave looked like: corrupt nobles, starving districts, people ground down by systems built to keep them powerless while the people on top grew fat off their labor. The description hit something in Cal immediately — a furious, instinctive revulsion at the very idea of it, despite having no memory of ever having lived in such a world. Roland offered him a place in the fight against it, and Cal — with nothing else to hold onto but a name and an instinct he couldn't explain — took it. *(Full account: [[Callum - Out of the Crystal]])*

That choice is what eventually connected him to [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]. These days Cal calls [[The Badlands]] home, though whether that's where he was born or simply where he ended up after the crystal remains *(unconfirmed)*. He isn't chasing his memories out of nostalgia — he's chasing them because he feels incomplete. Every value he holds, every instinct he trusts, is built on a foundation he can't see or explain: why religion enrages him, why the idea of being controlled fills him with such fury, why certain places, names, and symbols put him on edge for no reason he can name. He's slowly come to realize that the search itself — refusing to let his own past keep dictating him from the shadows — is just another form of the freedom he's always fought for.

Members of the Front have taken to calling him "Sword of Roland" — a nickname he dislikes but hasn't managed to shake. In the city of [[Thaylen]], he carried out a job for the Front — assassinating the corrupt [[Lord Hector Vance]] — in exchange for [[Roland Royce]] passing him a lead on one of the relics he's searching for. He's careful to frame these arrangements as trades, not loyalty: he isn't a member of the Front and never intends to be, but a clue toward his memories is worth a noble's life to him. *(Full account: [[Callum - Sword of Roland]])*

## Relationships

- [[Roland Royce]] — found him newly woken and memoryless in the crystal cave; recruited him into the fight against Brittania's corrupt systems, the choice that eventually tied him to the PLFB
- [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]] — not a full member, but shares many of their goals and works with them on occasion
- [[Karsus Morningstar]] — helping Callum get into the upcoming gala
- [[Pollux]] and [[Castor]] — the High Septon's guards, flagged by Roland as a threat to avoid

## Story

**Session 1:** Met [[Roland Royce]] and [[Samson Bloodmane]] at the same bar [[Vernan Hawkes]] had been at, roughly half an hour after Vernan left. Briefed to be eyes and ears on the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]] at the upcoming gala. Learned an artifact will be present — he doesn't know what it looks like, but [[Karsus Morningstar|Karsus]] said he'd be able to feel it. Roland warned him about [[Pollux]] and [[Castor]], the Septon's guards. Cal doesn't know Vernan is shadowing him at the gala.

## Build

Fighter or Barbarian. Physical combat focus, little to no magic of his own. His primary weapon is a sword — though it's likely just one shape taken by **the relic pendant** he wears: an item that can morph into any weapon its bearer chooses when activated.
