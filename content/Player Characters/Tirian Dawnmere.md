## Overview

Tirian Dawnmere is the surviving heir of [[House Dawnmere]], a noble house massacred three years ago when he was 20. Publicly, he is a beloved, prince-like young noble known for his stunning aurora ice magic and genuine warmth toward common people. Privately, he operates as **Noctis** — a shadow-wielding urban legend, vigilante, and information broker who hunts the people responsible for his family's deaths. He is building a legacy that will never be forgotten.

## Appearance

- Fair skin
- Deep royal purple eyes
- Soft, fluffy golden-blonde hair, medium length, layered and gently wavy, silky, falls around the neck
- Lean, athletic build, ~6'1"
- Extremely handsome — prince-like, elegant, warm, youthful but mature
- Voice: gentle, persuasive, seductive, intelligent — the kind of voice that makes people want to keep listening and trust what they hear

**Public attire:** White, gold, and royal purple noble clothing. Golden rapier at his hip.

**As Noctis:** Hood up, face obscured, simpler dark clothing. Less noble in appearance, unrecognizable.

## Personality

Warm, charismatic, and socially gifted. Tirian remembers names, makes people feel seen, and earns loyalty effortlessly. His kindness is genuine — he is soft-hearted, and that is not a performance. People naturally trust him because he actually deserves it.

He enjoys strategy games such as chess — a reflection of his calculating side. He is a natural charmer, popular at balls and galas, and often sought out as a dance partner. He gravitates toward older, more mature women — both because they tend to hold greater political influence, and because he genuinely finds them more beautiful. The combination of charm and calculation makes him formidable in noble social settings.

His gentleness does not preclude ruthlessness. He is fully capable of being **scheming, strategic, and ambitious** — these traits exist alongside his warmth, not beneath it. The same person who goes out of his way for a stranger will coldly dismantle an enemy if he judges it necessary. He wants legacy, influence, a lasting family name. His deepest fear is being **forgotten**.

He occupies a middle ground morally — he will lie, manipulate, and kill if he believes it protects innocent people, serves justice, or furthers his revenge. He is simultaneously a noble hero and a shadow vigilante.

He associates publicly with the [[Church of Light]], which worships [[Aria]], Goddess of Light. The church's values — truth, justice, hope — align naturally with [[House Dawnmere]]'s ideals, and he genuinely believes the people within it have good intentions. He is not, however, a true believer in worship itself. His affiliation is partly sincere and partly calculated: associating with one of Brittania's most popular religions is a significant political asset.

## History

Before the massacre, Tirian attended [[Strixhaven]], enrolled in the **Silverquill** college — the school of politics, rhetoric, ambition, and social power. A natural fit for someone building a legacy through influence and words. He completed his first two years before [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] forced him to drop out. With no family left, he could not justify continuing — he had a house to hold together and a murder to investigate. [[Karsus Morningstar]], the Headmaster, did everything he could to try to convince Tirian to stay.

Three years ago, [[House Dawnmere]] was massacred. Tirian was 20 years old. Every member of his family was murdered:

- **[[Auris Dawnmere]]** — his grandmother; very old, a bit senile, but deeply loving
- **[[Lirien Dawnmere]]** — his mother; a former commoner, beloved by the common people; warm and caring
- **[[Sorin Dawnmere]]** — his father; gruff on the surface, softie underneath; head of the house
- **[[Finn Dawnmere]]** — his younger brother, 4 years younger, 16 at the time; idolized Tirian completely
- **[[Lyra Dawnmere]]** — his younger sister, 8 years younger, 12 at the time; spoiled by Tirian, treated like a princess

The assassins responsible are dead. The people who **ordered** the murders are not. This is the core of Tirian's story.

**Dark Mantle** *(Wondrous Item, Uncommon, requires attunement)*

A leathery cloak made from the remains of a darkmantle. While wearing it:
- Climbing costs no extra movement; Advantage on Athletics checks to climb
- **Command word (1×/dawn):** Emit a 15-foot radius aura of magical darkness for up to 1 minute (concentration). Darkvision can't pierce it. Nonmagical light can't illuminate it. Dispels any light spell of 2nd level or lower it overlaps with.

*Fits perfectly over the Noctis identity — literal darkness on command.*

**Elegant Noble Duelist** — golden rapier, graceful swordsmanship, refined movements. The Fighter level gives him the combat backbone his sorcerer side needs.

**Inherent magic — Shadow:** Tirian's awakened magic type, manifesting around age 8 — before the aurora ice, before Strixhaven, before Noctis. Shadow is what was always in his blood. The awakening horrified him; he didn't understand what it was, only that it felt wrong and nothing like the light and ice his family carried. Any mage with Detect Magic identifies his inherent type as shadow. This is the source of the Noctis identity, not a mask over it.

**Aurora Mage (public)** — Aurora Ice Magic: a fusion of ice and light that manifests in aurora colors (pink, purple, green, blue). The ice reflects shifting aurora hues across its surface. Appears elegant and beautiful rather than destructive. Used for crystalline constructs, defensive barriers, healing, support, and battlefield displays. Unlike the rest of [[House Dawnmere]], Tirian was not born with this magic — he began learning it from scratch at age 8 or 9, driven by a need to belong to his family's identity. His displays are beautiful and hard-won, but never reached the heights of his grandmother [[Auris Dawnmere]]'s celebrated performances. The effort behind every aurora display is invisible to anyone watching.

**Shadow Mage (secret / Noctis)** — Shadow Magic Sorcerer subclass. Black shadow tendrils, living darkness spreading from walls and alleys. Glowing purple eyes when in use. Not evil — simply hidden. Used for ambushes, crowd control, information gathering, and intimidation.

**Darkbolt** *(2nd level evocation — Book of Ebon Tides)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action | **Range:** 60 ft | **Components:** V, S | **Duration:** Instantaneous | **Attack/Save:** CON 16

Hurl 3 rays of darkness at one or more targets within range (divided as you choose). Make a ranged spell attack for each target — each ray that hits deals **1d10 cold damage**. A target hit by one or more rays must make a CON save or be unable to use reactions until the start of its next turn.

*+1 ray per slot above 2nd. Cold Caster: first cold-damage hit triggers Frostbite (−1d4 on target's next save).*

**Shadow Portal** *(3rd level conjuration — Book of Ebon Tides)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action | **Range:** 200 ft | **Components:** V, M (silver door latch worth 50 gp, consumed) | **Duration:** 1 minute

Create a door in the nearest wall, cliff, tree, or structure within 10 feet, bound to a corresponding door up to 200 feet away (by line of sight, or by stating direction and distance). Any creature can open and pass through to teleport between the two portals while the spell lasts. The spell ends after the duration or when someone closes the distant portal as a Bonus Action.

*At 5th level: can open to a shadow road (may require additional components).*

**Shadow Tendrils** *(3rd level necromancy — Tales from the Shadows)*
**Casting Time:** 1 action | **Range:** 30 ft | **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute | **Attack/Save:** DEX 17

Writhing ribbons of quasi-real darkness extend from your hand. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, target takes **2d8 cold damage** and must succeed on a DEX save or be **restrained**. A restrained creature takes 2d8 cold damage at the start of each of its turns and can use an action to DEX check against your spell save DC to escape. As a bonus action, you can drag a restrained creature up to 15 feet closer.

The tendrils have AC 13, 12 HP. Immune to bludgeoning and nonmagical piercing/slashing. Vulnerable to radiant. If destroyed while the spell persists, re-form them as a bonus action.

**Aurora Spikes** *(Homebrew, 3rd level evocation)*

A line of razor-sharp aurora ice erupts from your hands in a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a DEX save — 8d6 cold damage on a fail, half on success. ## The Noctis Identity

*(Secret — not publicly known)*

Noctis is an urban legend-like nighttime figure associated with [[House Dawnmere]]'s shadow operations. Known to be powerful, shadow-magic wielding, and influential. Nobody knows Noctis is Tirian Dawnmere. He operates as an information broker and vigilante, active mostly at night.

## House Dawnmere Aesthetic

The house centers on sunlight, dawn, aurora colors, beauty, hope, nobility, warmth, legacy, and protection. Colors: white, gold, royal purple. Symbol: a golden sun rising over silver waters.

Tirian does not merely inherit House Dawnmere — he is actively **building** its legacy, treating it like a work of art.
