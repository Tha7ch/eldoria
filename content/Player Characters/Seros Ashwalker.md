## Overview

Seros Ashwalker is a former criminal from [[The Badlands]] who joined the [[Honest Hearts]] to get back on the right path. Searching for his friends. Has a punk aesthetic and can be an asshole.

## Appearance

- 6'1
- Deep dark brown skin
- Intricate braided/locked hair — complex, tight braids styled back, with natural texture
- Amber/golden eyes, sharp and intense
- Small facial markings or dots on the forehead
- Bead and chain jewelry around the neck
- Strong angular features; a stern, serious default expression
- Tattered jacket, punk aesthetic
- The kind of face that tells you he's seen things
