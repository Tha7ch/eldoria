## Overview

Kip is a 14-year-old hobgoblin wildshape druid from [[Petalton]] in [[Elsweyr]]. Goofy, chaotic, loves animals, nature, and stealing from the rich. Somehow endearing despite being chaotic neutral.

## Appearance

- Male Hobgoblin, 5'0", small and compact
- Green skin
- Short dark brown hair pulled back messily with a red ribbon
- Orange/amber-red eyes
- Wide sharp-toothed grin — the mischievous kind that means trouble is coming
- Small hoop earring and chain earring on one ear
- Large pointed goblin ears
- Cream loose shirt with a frilled collar
- Dark brown leather vest
- Brown leather arm bracers/wrist wraps
- Brown belt, red pants
- Usually seen with arms crossed and a smug expression

## Personality

Chaotic neutral. Impulsive and funny — considered breaking out of prison on his own just for the bit. Loves gold and animals. Has a good heart under the chaos; Lucerin looked at him in a way that suggested there is depth there.

## History

From [[Petalton]], a small village in [[Elsweyr]]. Got arrested for stealing.

## Relationships

- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — Lucerin looked directly into Kip's soul, said "I see you, Kip" — implying something significant about Kip's potential or destiny
- Nixi — his sister, three years younger; threatened by [[Hannibal]] by name during Session 7; Kip refused to go home because of this
- [[Hannibal]] — Hannibal knows his sister's name and referenced alternate versions of Kip across timelines ("you're actually alive in this one")

## Story

**Session 7:** Stabbed during the parade fight — coughed up blood, felt fear for the first time. Followed Hannibal and transformed into a miniature dragon to help. Hannibal named his sister Nixi as a threat. Slammed into Hannibal's cube, launched into a building. Cried over Kieran's mangled body. Refused to go home — chose to stay and hunt Hannibal to protect his sister.

**Session 2:** Arrested for stealing. Released when Manuel literally snapped the bars off rather than waste time. Immediately asked about gold. Excited by everything. Received 250 gold from Manuel. Asked Kieran if he should steal Damian's jewelry — Damian heard. Agreed to join the [[Honest Hearts]].

## Build

Wildshape Druid. In the doomed future he summoned an earth elemental (which [[X]] instantly destroyed) and hid behind walls. Was transformed into a tree by X. Was stabbed through the back by Luciferus's sword, losing feeling in his legs.
