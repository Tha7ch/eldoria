## Overview

A half-elf operative and field agent for [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]. Vernan grew up watching ordinary people go without while the crown and noble houses sat on every resource needed to fix it. Six years of trying the direct way — sharing what little he had, speaking up where he could — moved nothing. At 20, the Front offered what asking never did: a way to make the crown pay attention. Four years of training and fieldwork later, he's a self-made infiltrator and self-taught mage who believes most things worth having have to be taken.

## Appearance

**Skin:** Warm olive-bronze, sun-darkened from years of outdoor fieldwork.

**Hair:** Black, short-to-mid length, tousled and slightly messy — swept loosely off the forehead with pieces falling where they want.

**Eyes:** Green-hazel, sharp and alert.

**Face:** Strong jaw, defined cheekbones, a face that reads older than 24. Handsome in a rough, lived-in way rather than a polished one.

**Ears:** Subtly pointed — easy to miss at a glance.

**Build:** Lean and athletic. Broad shoulders for his frame but built for moving fast and getting out clean, not for standing and trading blows.

**Clothing:** Layered and practical — a loose olive-grey shirt open at the collar under a dark leather vest with crossed chest straps and buckles, leather belt, leather bracers at the wrists. Everything worn-in and functional, nothing decorative. Whatever the job calls for when on assignment.

## Personality

Idealistic at the core, pragmatic in practice. Vernan genuinely believes everyone deserves a fair chance at a decent life and always has — that conviction predates the Front by years. But six years of watching small kindnesses change nothing hardened the delivery. He's not bitter, but he's past asking. Lies as easily as he breathes. Reads rooms before anyone notices he's in one. Takes pride in everything he's built for himself — his skills, his magic, his place in the Front — because none of it was handed to him.

## History

**Age 0–14 — An ordinary childhood.** Raised by his family with no particular hardship of his own, but he noticed the hardship of everyone else. People around him went without what they needed while those who could have changed it never did. He thought it was unfair — that everyone deserved a decent life, and that it wasn't right how few people actually got one.

**Age 14–20 — Trying to help.** He tried the direct way first. Small acts — whatever a teenager with no money and no power could manage. Sharing what little he had, speaking up where he could. Watching, again and again, as none of it moved anything. The crown and the noble houses had every resource in the world to fix what he was seeing and simply didn't spend it on people like his neighbors.

**Age 20 — The Front.** [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]] was just beginning to take shape when he found it — or it found him. It depends on who you ask. What it offered was exactly what he'd been looking for: if asking hadn't worked in six years of trying, something more direct might. He joined believing immediately and completely that this was the only approach left that could actually make the crown pay attention.

**Age 20–24 — Training.** Four years of being built into an operative. The Front taught him everything he currently knows how to do: reading a room, lying as easily as he breathes, moving unseen, and when a job calls for it, fighting his way back out of a bad one. The Front couldn't hand him formal arcane training, but that didn't stop him — he found his own way to it, slipping into [[Strixhaven]]'s libraries at night, teaching himself piece by piece from whatever texts he could get his hands on, making quickly scribbled notes to refer to later. [[Karsus Morningstar]] almost certainly knew and turned a blind eye — Karsus believes everyone deserves the right to learn. He practiced on his days off. He's never lost the sense that his magic was something he *took* rather than something he was given — and he finds pride in that. Most things worth having, in his experience, have to be taken.

**Now.** He's spent these four years running information back from wherever the Front needs eyes. A job here, a room there, whatever the Front requires.

**Session 1:** Met [[Roland Royce]] and [[Samson Bloodmane]] at a bar. Briefed on a mission: infiltrate the upcoming king's gala using a ring of illusion to pose as a minor noble. Shadow a Front-adjacent operative named [[Callum]], who will enter with [[House Morningstar]]/[[Karsus Morningstar|Karsus]]. Get close to the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]] while avoiding his guards, [[Pollux and Castor]]. Gather intel on the Septon's dealings and noble family plans. All Front sources went dark after the parade attack. Vernan pushed back 20–30 gold from his payment to send to his family.
