## Overview

Solstice Shuriv is a human draconic sorcerer. Cold, sharp-tongued, and highly capable. Described as traumatized. She carries a dragon egg — kept secret in her bag of holding. *(Only [[Lucerin Atreides]] knows, after she put him in the bag during the Session 7 parade attack.)*

## Appearance

- Human female, 5'7
- Striking two-toned hair — one side pure white falling loose and wavy, the other jet black pulled up in a knot/bun; the contrast is sharp and deliberate
- Pale fair skin
- Dark, heavy-lidded eyes with dark markings beneath them — almost like shadows permanently settled under her gaze
- Dark markings/lines running across her face and down her neck — like dark veins or arcane scarring
- Long pointed ears with ear cuffs
- Small and slender, deceptively so
- Wears dark teal and black layered clothing — high-collared, scale-like armor panels, form-fitted
- Teal/blue gemstone pendant at her throat
- Her expression defaults to something between cold and mildly amused — the look of someone who finds most situations beneath full attention

## Personality

Very cold and sharp-tongued. Keeps people at arm's length. Not good with kids by her own admission. Despite her edge, shows dry wit and occasional warmth. Pragmatic to a fault — questions the logic of every situation before accepting it.

## History

*(largely unknown)*

## Relationships

- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — reluctant but warming; Lucerin says she is one of the people he trusts

## Story

**Session 7:** Hid [[Lucerin Atreides]] in her bag of holding to protect him during the parade attack — he discovered the dragon egg inside. Sent a message to the party: "I have the king in my bag."

**Session 2:** In prison after the bar brawl. Released by [[Jeane Howelle]]. Was skeptical of everything — "You put a child on the throne?", "We all have lives that we are just expected to stop?" Agreed to join the [[Honest Hearts]] with minimal enthusiasm.

## Build

Draconic Sorcerer. In the doomed future she summoned a white dragon and carried white dragon wings. She countered Michael's fireball with ease — "Nothing came out huh? Sucks doesn't it?"
