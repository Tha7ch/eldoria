## Overview

Aella Galefri is a half-elf storm mage of approximately 21 years. Blunt, aggressive, and sharp-tongued. Was banished from her church to [[Elsweyr]] for unknown reasons. Worships [[Veyrana]], Goddess of Storms. Does not like her family name mentioned.

## Appearance

- Dark tan skin
- Curly, wavy deep blue hair, medium length — blue, not purple
- Bright blue eyes
- Pointed half-elven ears
- Gold chain and crystal drop earring
- Blue outfit with purple accents
- Scars covering her body — facial scars visible on her face, and especially her arms; bite marks and slashes *(she deflected when Kylo asked)*
- Wears a simple gold band ring with delicate decorative etchings that converge on a unique holy/magical symbol — a gift from her mother [[Seyrell]], though she may not know the full significance. *(Unknown to Aella: [[Aevos]] has the matching ring.)*

## Personality

Very aggressive and blunt. Sharp tongue, does not tolerate condescension or sexism. Despite her edge, she has moments of loyalty — she told Damian "you're a good brother" in Session 2. Dislikes being called "Lady Galefri" but reluctantly accepts "Lady Aella."

Growing up, she frequently heard *"Why can't you be more like [[Lucius Raymor]]?"* — he was the model child parents invoked to shame troubled kids.

## History

Was banished from the [[Church of the Storm]] and sent to [[Elsweyr]] seven years ago — when she was 14. *(Circumstances unconfirmed — she said she would explain more later.)* She doesn't even know why she came back to Brittania — told Ludwig she'd explain over a drink sometime. Her family ([[House Galefri]] / Church of Storm) now knows she's back; word spread through the palace nobles.

**She is a bastard child.** Her father [[Torr Galefri]] had an affair with a woman named [[Seyrell]], and Aella was the result. She was raised in the Galefri household, but her illegitimacy explains the cruelty, neglect, and eventual banishment she experienced. Her family name "Galefri" carries associations she wants no part of.

**Unknown to Aella:** [[Seyrell]] is also the mother of [[Aevos]], making them half-siblings. They have never met.

## Relationships

- [[Veyrana]] — her goddess, Goddess of Storms; worshipped despite Veyrana having "forsaken" her *(per Michael's words in Session 1)*
- [[Torr Galefri]] — her father; head of the [[Church of the Storm]]; cold and cruel; when Kylo described his abusive mother, Aella simply said "my father"
- [[Urami Galefri]] — described as miserable
- [[Arashi Galefri]] — older stepbrother (age 26, 5 years older); takes after their father; a dickhead
- [[Whiro Galefri]] — older stepbrother (age 24, 3 years older); more laid back, especially recently
- [[Ludwig St. James]] — knows of her departure from the church; familiar with her somehow; she especially enjoys teasing him about his playboy reputation — but despite his bullshit and flirting, she has a genuine soft spot for him
- [[Lucius Raymor]] — knew each other from childhood; both chosen figures of gods, ingrained in their churches before Aella's banishment
- [[Kylo Wisemoon]] — bonded in Session 5; both carry trauma and physical scars; Kylo helped her with her spear and they talked honestly
- [[Lanius]] — a chosen of Veyrana like herself; Jean pointed her toward PLFB to find him and learn from him
- [[Jeane Howelle]] — sparred in Session 6; Jean completely dominated but acknowledged her heart; told her about Lanius
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — likely friendship and mutual respect (Campaign 2); he doesn't carry the snobbishness of most nobles

## Story

**Session 10:** Slept in and missed [[Cyris Voss]] leaving. In the palace garden, told [[Damian Grimm]] that [[Kieran Arden|Kieran]] was dead — "the prismatic barrier never broke, he was brutally killed." When [[Ludwig St. James|Ludwig]] placed his hand on her shoulder, she instinctively smacked it away. Learned from Manuel and Ludwig that [[Kaisel Irvenest]] knows she's back in Brittania — stared into the abyss and nervously chewed her fingernail. Went dress shopping with [[Solstice Shuriv]] and [[Jeane Howelle]], saying she needs to "stick it to my family." Told Manuel it wasn't her decision to leave. At the [[King's Crown]] meeting, asked if anyone truly likes the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]] and called him "that greasy man."

**Session 9:** Accompanied the group to the King's Crown meeting. Refused to give the High Septon her name ("I don't feel the need to identify myself here"). Defended Kip when the Grand Arbiter called him "goblin" ("Hey, he has a name") and was told to shush. Challenged Cyris's objection about Aeron's appointment by nodding her agreement. Upon hearing about [[Aeron Velmont]]'s mother's research, pointed out she's also a spellcaster — got a mild apology from Manuel and reminded him she spent seven years in Elsweyr. Shared warmly in Manuel's reminiscence about Leo and Oldric. Immediately clocked Aeron's strange scar — pressed him for details, suspected there's more to it than he let on. Reacted with visible shock to learning about [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] — "I always liked House Dawnmere. They were good people." Asked for a list of the ten strongest at the gala. Pushed back hard on holding the gala at all, citing personal reasons for not wanting to attend. Was present when Damian was found in the crypts between Elio and Iris's graves.

**Session 8:** After the parade attack, searched for Damian with the group. Found him mangled in a bloody crater — Healing Word burned him instead of healing (blood magic). At the castle crossroads, brooded alone in the courtyard polishing her spear. [[Ludwig St. James]] and [[Lucius Raymor]] sat down with her. She told them Kieran is dead — Ludwig's sword fell to the ground; he fell out of his chair. Dismissed her own grief: "I barely knew the guy. I've dealt with loss before — it's no big deal." Shut down Ludwig's commentary about the nobility. Heard from Lucius that [[Lumiere Ameris]], "the older Morningstar" *(unconfirmed — possibly [[Karsus Morningstar]])*, and someone called "the Lionheart" (blonde, very confident) are all coming back to Brittania. When Ludwig mentioned the Lionheart was blonde, Aella paused — seemed to think of someone — then said "it's nobody." Learned she'll meet the heir of [[House Dawnmere]] officially at the upcoming gala. Found out her family (Church of Storm / House Galefri) knows she's back. In the distillery: froze drink retribution, cold reaction to Kieran's death ("how unfortunate, things happen"), gave Kip a look when he brought it up again.

**Session 5:** Stepped outside after Damian's interrogation — the blood affected her. Kylo found her, sat with her without pushing. Revealed her father was similar to Damian. Showed Kylo her scars; deflected when asked about their origin. Bonded genuinely. Tested her new crystal-spear; her lightning runs almost purple-red. Revealed she knows the leader of the northern druid grove in Elsweyr.

**Session 2:** In prison after a bar brawl — a guard tried to hit on her, she responded, things escalated. Released by [[Jeane Howelle]] who kicked her cell door down. Refused Lucerin's formal address of "Lady Galefri." Noticed [[Cyris Voss]]'s magic felt similar in wavelength to [[Damian Grimm]]'s — a perceptive read that others missed. Defended Damian emotionally when he opened up about Lucerin. Agreed to join the [[Honest Hearts]].

## Build

Storm magic user. *(Class unknown — likely Sorcerer or Druid.)* Her lightning is blue. Casts level 9 Witch Bolt in the doomed future — lightning so dense it turns purple-red, 120 damage.
