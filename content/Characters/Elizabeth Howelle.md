## Overview

Elizabeth Howelle is the grandmother of [[Jeane Howelle]] and a figure of significant political sway in [[Brittania]]. She is married to [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]] — connecting [[House Howelle]] directly to [[King's Crown]] through marriage. She is also a past lover of [[Manuel]].

## Appearance

Unknown.

## Personality

Protective of Jean. Pragmatic — willing to ask Manuel to nudge Jean toward leaving the Kingsguard rather than confront her directly.

## History

Had a romantic relationship with [[Manuel]] at some point in the past. The relationship did not work out, but they parted on respectful terms. She went on to marry [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]]. Jeane's mother is from an earlier relationship with a different man, so Jeane is not a blood relative of the High Septon.

## Relationships

- [[Manuel]] — past lover; they still respect each other
- [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]] — her husband; member of [[King's Crown]]
- [[Jeane Howelle]] — her granddaughter; worries about her wellbeing in the Kingsguard
- [[House Howelle]] — senior member

## Story

**Session 11:** Seated at the gala table with the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]] and [[Jeane Howelle|Jean]]. Danced with [[Manuel]] — their conversation revealed they were past lovers. Asked Manuel to nudge Jean toward leaving the Kingsguard, noting Jean has had it especially hard "between Archibald and how Lanius leaving hurt her." Manuel agreed to talk to Jean but made no promises.
