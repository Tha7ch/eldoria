## Overview

Orelia Greenleaf is the Elven Princess housed with [[House Greenleaf]], the emissary house from the Elven Kingdom. She is one of the ten strongest people in all of Eldoria. She has a romantic interest with [[Lucius Raymor]], a detail Ludwig St. James referenced as Lucius being "charmed by Elven students at Strixhaven."
