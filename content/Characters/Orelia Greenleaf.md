## Overview

Orelia Greenleaf is a Princess of [[Valenwyth]], the elven country. She is one of the ten strongest people in all of Eldoria. A 4th year **Vigorbloom** student at [[Strixhaven]], currently studying abroad. She has a romantic interest with [[Lucius Raymor]], a detail Ludwig St. James referenced as Lucius being "charmed by Elven students at Strixhaven." She will be attending the upcoming gala in [[Caer Brittanis]].

## Relationships

- [[Lucius Raymor]] — romantic interest
- [[House Greenleaf]] — her house; the Valenwyth emissary family in Brittania
- [[Valenwyth]] — her home country; she is one of its princesses
