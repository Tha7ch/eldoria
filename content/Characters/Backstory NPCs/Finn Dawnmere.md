## Overview

Finn Dawnmere was [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s younger brother, 4 years his junior. He idolized Tirian and looked up to him completely. He was 16 years old when he was killed in [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]].

## Appearance

- 5'10" at 16, still growing into a tall frame — had Sorin's build in early form
- Dark brown hair, worn slightly messy and unkempt in a boyish way
- Bright royal purple eyes full of enthusiasm and openness
- Young, unguarded face — the kind that showed everything he was feeling
- Wore white and gold Dawnmere noble attire, always a little carelessly

## Personality

Genuinely, effortlessly kind. The sort of person who remembered everyone's name, noticed when someone was having a bad day, and did something about it without making a production of it. Friendly to everyone — not in the performed, noble-social way, but because he actually liked people and they could tell. Easy to be around. Easy to love.

He idolized Tirian completely, which Tirian found both flattering and occasionally overwhelming. He wanted to be wherever his brother was, learn what his brother knew, do what his brother did. But he wasn't a shadow — he had his own warmth, his own laugh, his own way of pulling people in. He just happened to think Tirian hung the moon.

The kind of person whose absence leaves a room quieter than it should be.

## Magic — Aura of Aurora

Aurora that wraps around him, strengthening himself or those he extends it to. Strongest when kept on himself — when shared, the power spreads thin and weakens with distance. In combat it made him faster, sharper, more resilient. He could extend it to others, but the more he gave away the less remained on him. His most natural target was Tirian — when they trained together, Finn's aura would drift toward his brother almost unconsciously.

## Story

Finn was close friends with [[Kaisel Irvenest]] from a young age — the two were similar in age and their friendship formed naturally through the overlap between their noble families. What it looked like in practice: stealing pastries together and blaming each other, sitting side by side on the floor of the Irvenest library, sparring in the courtyard. Finn had been pushing Kaisel to teach him sword technique for months — *"You seriously need to teach me how to use that sword of yours"* — and Kaisel's stock deflection was *"Maybe when you get taller than me."* They both found this funnier than it was.

He was killed in [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] when he was 16. Kaisel was away from [[Caer Brittanis]] at the time — on the mission that would become [[Kaisel - What Was Left]] — and came home to the news. [[Roland Irvenest]] told him. Kaisel had known for years that the world was dangerous. He hadn't understood until that moment that it could take someone so specifically his.
