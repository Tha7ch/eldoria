## Overview

Sorin Dawnmere was [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s father and head of [[House Dawnmere]] before the slaughter. Gruff on the surface, but a genuine softie underneath. He was killed in [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]].

![[Sorin Dawnmere (4x5 Portrait).webp|350]] ![[Sorin Dawnmere.webp|350]] ![[House Dawnmere Family.webp|350]]

## Appearance

- 6'2", broad-shouldered and powerfully built
- Short dark brown hair with grey beginning to show at the temples
- Strong, weathered jaw — a face that looks stern at first glance
- Deep royal purple eyes, sharp but with quiet warmth beneath them
- Wore ornate white and gold noble attire with House Dawnmere sun motifs
- Expression gruff and serious, but never unkind

## Personality

Gruff and reserved in presentation, but warm and soft-hearted at his core. The kind of man who shows love through actions rather than words.

## Magic — Aurora Flames

Aurora-tinted ember and flame — amber, deep red, the colors of a fire at the end of the day rather than dawn breaking. Controlled and slow-burning by nature, warm rather than aggressive. In combat, a formidable weapon. Outside it, the kind of warmth that doesn't announce itself but that you notice the moment it's gone. He was quite strong with it — capable of real flame when pushed, but the ember was his nature.

Sorin's flames were beautiful in their own right, though never as publicly celebrated as [[Auris Dawnmere]]'s aurora displays. He was also a powerful fighter who served the kingdom in its wars — much of House Dawnmere's reputation for combat prowess rested on his record.
