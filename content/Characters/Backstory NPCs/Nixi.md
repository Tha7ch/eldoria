## Overview

Nixi is [[Kip]]'s younger sister, approximately 11 years old. She is from [[Petalton]] in [[Elsweyr]]. [[Hannibal]] knew her name during the parade attack in Session 7 — threatening her by name to destabilize Kip mid-battle. This prompted Kip to refuse to go home, fearing endangering her. [[Kylo Wisemoon]] offered to bring her to [[Brittania]] for protection, which Kip accepted.

- [[Kip]] — older brother; three years older than her
