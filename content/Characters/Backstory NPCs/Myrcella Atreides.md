## Overview

Myrcella Atreides was the Queen of [[Brittania]] and wife of [[Leo Atreides]]. She had brown hair — passed on to [[Godwyn Atreides|Godwyn]], [[Violet Atreides|Violet]], and [[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]]. She was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family alongside her husband and most of her children. [[Lucerin Atreides]] survived only by chance.

## History

Wife of [[Leo Atreides]] and mother of seven children. She was reportedly the parent best able to nurture [[Lucerin Atreides|Lucerin]]'s primordial foresight — Lucerin mourns that she is gone and that he can no longer be guided by her. She was also the primary source of cruelty toward [[Damian Grimm]] during his upbringing within the castle — she arranged for him to be made a Kingsguard member, keeping him permanently close to the royal family while ensuring he could never belong to it.

Killed in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.
