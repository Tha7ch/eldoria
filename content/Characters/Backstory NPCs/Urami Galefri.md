## Overview

Urami is the wife of [[Torr Galefri]], head of the [[Church of the Storm]]. Miserable in disposition. She did not actively abuse [[Aella Galefri]] — she simply neglected her. Knowing that Aella is a bastard from Torr's affair with Seyrell, her cold indifference toward Aella is perhaps unsurprising.
