## Overview

Catherine Atreides was the First Princess of [[Brittania]], third in the line of succession. She was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.

## Appearance

- ~16 years old
- Blonde hair — inherited from her father [[Leo Atreides]]
- Blue-grey eyes — the Atreides family trait

## Relationships

- [[Leo Atreides]] — her father
- [[Myrcella Atreides]] — her mother
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — younger brother; now King
- [[Damian Grimm]] — hated him; did not accept the royal bastard
