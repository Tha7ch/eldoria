## Overview

Cedric Irvenest is the eldest child of [[House Irvenest]], 8 years older than [[Kaisel Irvenest]]. He is one of the family members who never fully accepted Kaisel's adoption. He believes bloodline matters and resents the attention Kaisel receives. His resentment deepened when Kaisel became the Chosen of [[Wylder]] at 12 — Cedric struggles to understand why someone of non-blood would receive such a divine honor.

## Personality

Arrogant, ambitious, elitist, and politically minded.
