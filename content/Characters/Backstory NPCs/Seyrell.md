## Overview

Seyrell is a very powerful sorceress and the biological mother of both [[Aella Galefri]] and [[Aevos]]. Neither child knows they share a mother, and the two have never met. She died giving birth to Aella, at age 31.

She had an affair with [[Torr Galefri]], which produced Aella — making Aella a bastard child raised in the Galefri household. She also has a connection to [[House Raymor]] that produced Aevos, though the details of that relationship are unknown.

## Appearance

- Human, 5'2
- Long, light blonde hair
- Golden eyes
- A sweet woman with a soft expression
- Wore flowy white silky robes — religious in style, but relaxed rather than formal

## Personality

Sweet, soft-spoken — a gentle presence by all accounts.

## History

Before her death, Seyrell was the previous [[Aria|Chosen of Aria]], affiliated with the [[Church of Light]] — preceding both [[Lucius Raymor]] and [[Aevos]] in that role. She died giving birth to [[Aella Galefri]] at age 31.

## Relationships

- [[Aella Galefri]] — her daughter (by [[Torr Galefri]]); Aella does not know Seyrell's full story; Seyrell gave her a matching ring
- [[Aevos]] — her son (father from [[House Raymor]]); Aevos does not know he has a half-sister; Seyrell gave him a matching ring
- [[Torr Galefri]] — had an affair with him; produced Aella

## The Rings

Seyrell gave each of her children a simple gold band ring bearing delicate decorative etchings that converge on a unique holy/magical symbol of her own design. Both children carry the rings without knowing the other exists. If they ever met and noticed the rings, the symbol would be the first clue that connects them.
