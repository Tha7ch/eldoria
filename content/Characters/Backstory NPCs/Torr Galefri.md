## Overview

Torr is the head of the [[Church of the Storm]] and [[Aella Galefri]]'s father. He is cold, cruel, and authoritarian — and was physically and emotionally abusive to Aella. In Session 5, when [[Kylo Wisemoon]] described his own abusive mother, Aella responded simply: *"My father."* His scars likely explain at least some of Aella's.

## Appearance

- 62 years old
- Deep blue hair — the same shade as [[Aella Galefri]]'s, blue not purple — worn up in a man bun
- Significant facial hair
- Wears religious-inspired clothing befitting the head of a church

## Personality

Cold, cruel, and authoritarian. A hard man who wields religious power without warmth. His son [[Arashi Galefri]] takes after him.

## Relationships

- [[Seyrell]] — a woman he had an affair with; produced Aella
- [[Aella Galefri]] — his bastard daughter by [[Seyrell]]; was abusive toward her
- [[Urami Galefri]] — his wife; neglects Aella
- [[Arashi Galefri]] — his son; takes after him; hates Aella
- [[Whiro Galefri]] — his son; has mellowed out over time
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — hates him; Kaisel is aware of this and deliberately rage-baits him
- [[Church of the Storm]] — he leads it
