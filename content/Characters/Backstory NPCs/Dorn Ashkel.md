## Overview

Dorn Ashkel was [[Cyris Voss]]'s biological father. A large, broad-shouldered, loud-laughing man from [[The Badlands]], he was devoted to his son and delighted by his endless curiosity. He was killed defending Cyris from a mutated wasteland monster when Cyris was 7 years old.

## Appearance

- Large, broad-shouldered
- Red/Purple Tiefling
- In his 30s at time of death

## Personality

Warm, loud, and joyful. Laughed easily and often. Believed a sharp mind was as vital as a sharp blade. Stood between his child and death without hesitation.

## History

Lived with his wife [[Serapha Ashkel]] and son Cyris in [[The Badlands]]. Killed defending Cyris from a mutated monster. Together with Serapha, they killed the creature, but both died of the wounds.

## Relationships

- [[Cyris Voss]] — his son; everything gentle in Cyris's personality comes from his parents
- [[Serapha Ashkel]] — his wife
- [[The Badlands]] — homeland
