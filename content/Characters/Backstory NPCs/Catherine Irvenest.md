## Overview

Catherine Irvenest is the Matriarch of [[House Irvenest]] and [[Kaisel Irvenest]]'s adopted mother. She loves Kaisel and considers him her son, never having opposed his adoption. Where [[Roland Irvenest]] is protective and sheltering, Catherine is the opposite — she believes hardship builds character and pushed consistently for Kaisel to experience the real world.

## Personality

Blunt, strict, practical, strong-willed, and occasionally abrasive. She is the stricter of the two parents. Most of the opportunities Kaisel received outside the estate — including attending the paladin academy — happened largely because Catherine insisted on it.
