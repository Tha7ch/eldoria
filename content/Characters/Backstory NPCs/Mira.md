## Overview

Mira was [[Veyr]]'s younger sister. She lived alone in a quiet town near [[Caer Brittanis]], kept largely to herself, and kept a journal. By the time [[Kaisel Irvenest]] found her house, she had been dead for some time — her body possessed and consumed by a demon. Kaisel buried her behind her house and brought her journal to Veyr.

## Appearance

- Dark red hair, longer than Veyr's — fell past the shoulders in loose waves
- Sharp green eyes (same as Veyr)
- Freckles across her cheeks

## History

Mira lived in a modest two-story stone house on a quiet road at the edge of a small town near [[Caer Brittanis]]. Her journal records a slow, terrible unraveling: whispers when she was alone, waking in rooms she didn't remember entering, losing time, finding scratches on her hands. She knew something was wrong with her. She knew she was slipping away.

Her final journal entry: *"If anyone finds this, tell my sister I'm sorry. I didn't want to be alone."*

The demon had fully consumed her by the time Kaisel arrived. He killed it, searched what remained of the house, found her journal beneath a loose board under the study desk, and buried her in the garden behind the house beneath a small tree. He brought the journal to [[Veyr]].

## Relationships

- [[Veyr]] — older sister; described in the journal as "overprotective"
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — found and buried her; brought her journal to Veyr
