## Overview

Lyra Dawnmere was [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s younger sister, 8 years his junior. She was utterly spoiled by Tirian and treated like a princess — his little girl to protect and adore. She was 12 years old when she was killed in [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]].

![[Lyra Dawnmere (4x5 Portrait).webp|350]] ![[Lyra Dawnmere.webp|350]] ![[House Dawnmere Family.webp|350]]

## Appearance

- 4'11", small and delicate even for her age
- Long soft golden-blonde hair like her mother — worn with ribbons braided in because Tirian always insisted on it
- Wide royal purple eyes full of curiosity and innocence
- Sweet, open face — a cherished little princess
- Wore beautiful white and gold dresses with sun and dawn embroidery

## Personality

Beloved and cherished. The baby of the family, doted on by her older brother.

## Magic — Aurora Petals

Light petals of aurora that drift and settle, healing her on contact. Soft, instinctive, effortlessly beautiful. She probably thought of them as decoration more than power — they simply appeared when she was happy or hurt. The healing worked on herself; whether it could extend to others was never tested.
