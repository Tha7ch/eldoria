## Overview

Serapha Ashkel was [[Cyris Voss]]'s biological mother. A gentle Blue Tiefling from [[The Badlands]], she taught Cyris his letters by scratching them into dirt and ash. She was killed defending Cyris from a mutated wasteland monster when he was 7. Everything gentle in Cyris comes from her.

## Appearance

- Blue Tiefling
- Late 20s at time of death

## Personality

The gentlest soul the Badlands could produce. Smiled when food was thin and storms rolled in. Taught curiosity as a form of hope. Believed knowledge was not power, but safety.

## History

Lived with her husband [[Dorn Ashkel]] and son Cyris in [[The Badlands]]. Killed alongside Dorn defending Cyris from a mutated monster. Together they killed the creature but died of their wounds.
