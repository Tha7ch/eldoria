## Overview

Amelia Irvenest is the second eldest child of [[House Irvenest]], 7 years older than [[Kaisel Irvenest]]. She looks down on him because of his adoption. Publicly she maintains noble etiquette, but privately shows her disdain through subtle remarks about his status within the family. She views Kaisel's rise — especially his status as Chosen of Wylder — as unfair.

Approximately two years ago, she had a brief fling with [[Tirian Dawnmere]] that lasted 2–3 weeks — she was 25, he was 21. Tirian eventually saw through it — she was pursuing him for political reasons, drawn to his status as heir of House Dawnmere. When he ended it, he told her: *"You inherited your mother's beauty, but clearly not her heart."*

## Appearance

- Long, flowing deep red/auburn hair — wavy, with braided sections decorated with silver and blue crystal/snowflake hair accessories
- Warm amber-brown eyes
- Fair skin
- Long sapphire drop earrings; sapphire necklace
- Wears elegant ballgowns — white and cream with blue velvet trim and silver embroidery and lace; off-shoulder, blue shawl/cape draped over the shoulders
- A knowing, slightly pleased smile — someone completely aware of how she looks and what it does to a room
- The Tirian breakup line makes more sense every time you see her: beautiful in a way that stops conversations

## Personality

Snobby, judgmental, proud, and status-conscious. Beautiful but calculating — she pursues relationships for power and position rather than genuine connection. Her mother [[Catherine Irvenest]] is warm and principled; Amelia did not inherit that.

## Relationships

- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — adopted brother; looks down on him; privately disdainful; her treatment of him was one of the things that turned Tirian against her
- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — had a 2–3 week fling; he ended it when he realized she was using him for political gain; parted with his cutting remark about her mother
- [[Catherine Irvenest]] — her mother; Tirian's breakup line pointedly invoked the contrast between them
- [[House Irvenest]] — second eldest child
