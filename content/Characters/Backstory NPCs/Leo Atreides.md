## Overview

Leo Atreides was the King of [[Brittania]] and father of [[Lucerin Atreides]]. He had blonde hair — passed on to [[Gideon Atreides|Gideon]], [[Catherine Atreides|Catherine]], [[Elio Atreides|Elio]], and [[Iris Atreides|Iris]]. He was murdered approximately one month before the campaign begins as part of the plot that wiped out the entire royal family. He was described by Lucerin as "a great king, like his father before him."

## History

Descended from King [[Arthur Atreides]] of the Ancient Kingdom of [[Camelot]]. Son of the previous king, grandson of the king during whose reign [[Manuel]] first joined the Kingsguard. Ruled Brittania for approximately 30–38 years before his assassination approximately one month before the campaign begins. His death, along with the rest of the royal family, was part of an organized plot — likely orchestrated by [[King's Crown]] or a related faction.

Lucerin says of him: *"My father was a great king, like his father before him."*
