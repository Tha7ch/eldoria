## Overview

Kane Orso is a people trafficker and criminal operating out of the [[The Crooked Boar]] tavern in [[Caer Brittanis]]. He was [[Kaisel Irvenest]]'s first lead in the search for Aella Galefri — ultimately a dead end on Aella, but he pointed Kaisel toward [[Veyr]].

## Personality

Pragmatic and street-smart. Unsentimental about his work but not gratuitously cruel. Smart enough to cut his losses when a sword is pinned through his mattress. Has a wry, slightly offended quality when dealing with Kaisel's moral frankness.

## History

Moves people, cargo, and products across [[Brittania]] — explicitly not an information dealer. Has connections to gangs, smugglers, brokers, merchants, and bounty hunters across Brittania. Maintains a private room on the upper floor of [[The Crooked Boar]], protected by guards.

Knew of Aella Galefri's disappearance only as a widely-known story — no firsthand knowledge. When Kaisel pressed him for a lead on information brokers, a slip of the word "somebody" gave Veyr's existence away before he'd decided to share it.

## Relationships

- [[Veyr]] — knows of her; directed [[Kaisel Irvenest]] to her
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — Kaisel beat him and the entire tavern, pinned a sword through his mattress, interrogated him, then told him sincerely to turn himself in to the authorities

## Story

**Kaisel's age-17 backstory:** Kaisel was directed to Kane looking for information on Aella. After clearing the Crooked Boar's patrons and guards, Kaisel found Kane in his room. Kane admitted knowing of Aella only as a public story, but inadvertently revealed he knew of a dedicated information broker — [[Veyr]] — which was the lead Kaisel actually needed. He was left alive with an earnest suggestion that he turn himself in.
