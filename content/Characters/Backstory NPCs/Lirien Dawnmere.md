## Overview

Lirien Dawnmere was [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s mother. Originally a commoner who married into [[House Dawnmere]], she became one of the most beloved figures the house ever produced — not despite her origins, but because of them. She was loving, caring, and deeply adored by the common people. She was killed in [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]].

![[Lirien Dawnmere (4x5 Portrait).webp|350]] ![[Lirien Dawnmere.webp|350]] ![[House Dawnmere Family.webp|350]]

## Appearance

- 5'6", slender and graceful
- Soft golden-blonde hair worn loosely — Tirian inherited her coloring
- Bright, kind eyes and a warm open expression that put people immediately at ease
- Wore simple but elegant white and gold noble attire, understated compared to full nobility — she never lost her commoner instincts for understatement

## Personality

Loving, warm, and genuinely kind. Her common roots kept her grounded and made her accessible to people of all walks of life, which made her beloved in a way most nobles never achieve.
