## Overview

Iris Atreides was the Third Princess of [[Brittania]] and twin sister of [[Elio Atreides]], the youngest of the royal children. She was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.

## Appearance

- 5 years old, small and slight
- Golden-blonde hair braided up in an elegant crown braid with loose curly tendrils framing her face; small gold and blue tiara
- Fair skin
- Blue-grey eyes, wide and calm
- White high-collared blouse with ruffled collar; blue and white royal dress with elaborate gold embroidery and blue gem accents; structured blue outer layer/cape with gold embroidery and star motifs; white gloves
- Expression: small composed smile — adorable without trying, a miniature queen who takes herself very seriously

## Relationships

- [[Leo Atreides]] — her father
- [[Myrcella Atreides]] — her mother
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — older brother; now King
- [[Elio Atreides]] — twin brother
- [[Damian Grimm]] — adored him; she and [[Elio Atreides|Elio]] were especially attached to Damian of all the siblings
