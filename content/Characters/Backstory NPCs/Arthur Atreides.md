## Overview

Arthur Atreides was the last king of [[Camelot]], the Kingdom of All Kingdoms. He gave his life trying to protect Camelot from a demon assault. His death left the kingdom without a leader, and it was subsequently torn apart by those who sought to seize what remained. He is the founding ancestor of [[House Atreides]] and, through it, the royal line of [[Brittania]].

## History

The last king of [[Camelot]]. Gave his life defending the kingdom from demons. After his death, Camelot — once nearly a utopia — fell to power-hungry opportunists who picked apart what remained of it. His bloodline survived and became [[House Atreides]], the Celestial Realm's chosen line to lead [[Brittania]].

*Selflessness killed the King. Selfishness killed the Kingdom.*
