## Overview

Godwyn Atreides was the First Prince of [[Brittania]] and heir apparent to [[Leo Atreides]]. He was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.

## Appearance

- 25 years old, tall and commanding
- Short dark brown hair, slightly tousled
- Fair skin
- Blue-grey eyes, sharp and calm — the expression of someone born to lead and fully aware of it
- White and gold royal attire with intricate golden chain and lattice detailing across the chest
- Black fur trim at the shoulders
- Long white cape
- White gloves
- Rides a black horse adorned with golden chain harness — the image of a crown prince at the head of an army

## Relationships

- [[Leo Atreides]] — his father
- [[Myrcella Atreides]] — his mother
- [[Gideon Atreides]] — younger brother
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — youngest surviving brother; now King
- [[Damian Grimm]] — hated him; did not accept the royal bastard
