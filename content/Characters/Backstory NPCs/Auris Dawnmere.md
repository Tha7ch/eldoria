## Overview

Auris Dawnmere was [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s grandmother. Very old and a bit senile toward the end of her life, but deeply caring and loving. She was killed in [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] along with the rest of the family.

## Appearance

- Small and slightly hunched with age, 5'3"
- Long gray hair worn in a thick braid over one shoulder, with loose wisps framing her face
- Purple eyes, soft and clear with a warm, knowing smile
- Soft, creased face with warm eyes that still held clarity in kind moments
- Cream and gold robes with delicate embroidery, paired with amethyst and gold jewelry — drop earrings, a pendant necklace, and a ring
- Always seemed to be reaching for something or someone she was remembering

## Personality

Caring and loving despite her age and mental fog. The kind of grandmother who fills a home with warmth.

## Magic — Aurora Magic

The oldest and most classical expression of Dawnmere power. Auris could paint the aurora across the entire sky — broad sweeping strokes of pink, green, and gold, visible for miles. Not the most powerful in the family, but the most beautiful, and the staple the house is known for. The source expression from which all other Dawnmere magic branches.

Her public aurora displays were massively popular with the common people — the most recognized and celebrated magical performances [[House Dawnmere]] ever produced, even more so than [[Sorin Dawnmere]]'s fire or [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s ice.

In old age, control faded with her mind. She would paint the sky without knowing she was doing it — sitting in the garden, half-asleep, and the aurora would simply appear overhead. The family learned to read the sky to know what she was feeling.
