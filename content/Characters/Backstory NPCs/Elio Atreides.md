## Overview

Elio Atreides was the Fourth Prince of [[Brittania]] and twin brother of [[Iris Atreides]]. He was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.

## Appearance

- 5 years old, small
- Short curly/wavy golden-blonde hair, naturally tousled and full of volume
- Fair skin, rosy cheeks
- Blue-grey eyes, bright and warm
- White high-collared royal jacket with blue and gold trim; blue and white cape with gold embroidery; gold chains and blue gemstone brooch at chest; heraldic belt in blue and gold
- Expression: warm closed-mouth smile — happy, brave, a genuinely good soul

## Relationships

- [[Leo Atreides]] — his father
- [[Myrcella Atreides]] — his mother
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — older brother; now King
- [[Iris Atreides]] — twin sister
- [[Damian Grimm]] — adored him; he and [[Iris Atreides|Iris]] were especially attached to Damian of all the siblings
