## Overview

Roland Irvenest is the Patriarch of [[House Irvenest]] and [[Kaisel Irvenest]]'s adopted father. He personally chose to adopt Kaisel at age 3 and has always loved him as much as any of his biological children — never treating him differently. He is wise, protective, and compassionate. His extreme protectiveness of Kaisel stems from knowing the weight of what Kaisel carries.

## Personality

Wise, protective, patient, compassionate, and responsible. He tried for years to give Kaisel a normal life and shield him from the harsher realities of the world. When Kaisel was revealed as the Chosen of Wylder at age 12, Roland recognized he could no longer shelter him from his destiny — but his love and protection never wavered.
