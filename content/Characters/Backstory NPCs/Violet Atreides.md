## Overview

Violet Atreides was the Second Princess of [[Brittania]]. She was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.

## Appearance

- ~8 years old
- Brown hair — inherited from her mother [[Myrcella Atreides]]
- Blue-grey eyes — the Atreides family trait

## Relationships

- [[Leo Atreides]] — her father
- [[Myrcella Atreides]] — her mother
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — younger brother; now King
- [[Damian Grimm]] — loved him; one of the younger siblings who treated him with genuine warmth
