## Overview

Elizabeth Irvenest is [[Kaisel Irvenest]]'s closest friend and adopted sister, 2 years older than him. She was one of the earliest to fully accept him. Kaisel spends more time with her than anyone else in the family and worries constantly about her safety and happiness.

**Unknown to Elizabeth:** Kaisel has romantic feelings for her that he refuses to confess. The reason is his fate as the Chosen of Wylder — he knows he is destined to die by age 40, and he cannot bring himself to build a future with her only to leave her behind. So he keeps his feelings hidden despite their closeness.
