## Overview

Lord Hector Vance was a corrupt noble of [[Thaylen]], assassinated by [[Callum]] at the request of the Front in exchange for a lead on one of the relics.

## Appearance

*(Unknown.)*

## Personality

Cruel and confident in his wealth and connections — sat on royal committees and counted governors, generals, and merchants among his contacts. Believed his money and status could buy his way out of any threat, even with a sword at his throat.

## History

Vance crushed his subjects with heavy taxes that left families starving, extorted local businesses, and quietly removed rivals. Whether every story about him was true is unconfirmed, but enough were that the Front marked him for death.

## Relationships

- [[Callum]] — assassinated him in his study after he refused to believe his wealth couldn't save him
- [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]] — wanted him dead; arranged his assassination via [[Callum]]

## Story

Killed by [[Callum]] in his study late at night, after Callum infiltrated his estate in [[Thaylen]] and disabled two guards outside the door. See [[Callum - Sword of Roland]].
