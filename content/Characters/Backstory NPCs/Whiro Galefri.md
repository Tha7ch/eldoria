## Overview

Whiro is [[Aella Galefri]]'s older stepbrother, 1 year her senior. Unlike his brother [[Arashi Galefri]] and their father, Whiro is a little more chill — especially recently.

## Appearance

- Dark navy/blue hair, medium-long, naturally messy and wild
- Dark tan/brown skin
- Blue-grey eyes, calm and slightly heavy-lidded
- Pointed ears
- Light facial stubble
- Blue crystal diamond drop earrings on both ears
- Gold star/pendant necklace with chains
- Dark navy and white noble attire with gold trim
- Default expression: calm, reserved, unhurried — the mellow version he grew into

## Personality

As a child, he was a brat — a classic middle child trying to impress his father and older brother [[Arashi Galefri]]. He was also mean to [[Kaisel Irvenest]] when they were younger — around age 9 when Kaisel was 7. He mellowed out in his early teenage years, and by the time he was 13 (Kaisel 11) they had become genuine friends. They've been close ever since — about nine years of friendship.
