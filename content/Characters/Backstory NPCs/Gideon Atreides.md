## Overview

Gideon Atreides was the Second Prince of [[Brittania]], second in the line of succession after [[Godwyn Atreides]]. He was part of the same circle of influential young nobles as [[Tirian Dawnmere]] and [[Lumiere Ameris]] — close friends with both. He was murdered in the Assassination of the Royal Family approximately one month before the campaign begins.

## Appearance

- 23 years old
- Medium-length golden-blonde hair, slightly wavy and naturally tousled — falls around the jaw, swept back loosely
- Fair skin
- Blue-grey eyes, warm and open
- White high-collared jacket with blue and white cape/mantle, heavy gold embroidery in vine and floral patterns throughout
- Gold ornate shoulder clasps; gold chain with blue gemstone pendant at the chest
- Expression: genuine, warm smile — the kind of person who makes you feel immediately at ease

## Personality

Tender-hearted, soft-spoken, and emotionally mature beyond his years. Gideon was full of love — for his family, his friends, and anyone who needed it. He treated people with genuine warmth without agenda, which is why he was so beloved in return. Among the royal children, he was the one who saw Damian as a person rather than a problem. He had the kind of grace that made hard conversations feel safe and heavy grief feel shared.

## History

Second Prince of Brittania, second child of [[Leo Atreides]] and [[Myrcella Atreides]]. Moved in a circle of prominent young nobles that included [[Tirian Dawnmere]] and [[Lumiere Ameris]]. When the [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] killed Tirian's family three years ago, Gideon was among those who helped him through the grief. He was killed in the Assassination of the Royal Family alongside the rest of the Atreides line.

His death is one of Tirian's most personal losses — and a significant part of why Tirian wants to know who ordered the massacre.
