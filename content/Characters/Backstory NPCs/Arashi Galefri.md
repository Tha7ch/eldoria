## Overview

Arashi is [[Aella Galefri]]'s older stepbrother, 5 years her senior. He takes after their father — [[Torr Galefri]], the cold and cruel head of the [[Church of the Storm]] — in personality and disposition.

## Appearance

- Dark navy/blue hair, medium-long and messy, falling around his face
- Dark tan/brown skin
- Blue eyes, sharper and colder than [[Whiro Galefri]]'s
- Pointed ears
- Full, well-groomed beard and mustache
- Dark navy and cream noble attire with gold trim, layered with a gold star pendant necklace and matching star-shaped pins
- Default expression: cold, stern, looking down on others

## Personality

A dickhead. Cut from the same cloth as [[Torr Galefri]]. Actively hates Aella.

## Relationships

- [[Aella Galefri]] — stepsister; hates her
- [[Torr Galefri]] — his father
- [[Whiro Galefri]] — younger brother
- [[Church of the Storm]] — affiliated through the family
