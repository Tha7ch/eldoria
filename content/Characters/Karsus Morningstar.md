## Overview

Karsus Morningstar is the Headmaster of [[Strixhaven]] and the Grand Mage of [[Brittania]] — the highest magical authority in the country. He leads a house known for producing some of the most powerful mages in Brittanian history.

## Appearance

- Middle-aged, with dark, tousled shoulder-length hair streaked with grey and a short beard
- Wears mostly navy clothing, layered with a white robe accented in red
- A long blue-grey scarf wrapped around his neck
- Wields a large glowing blue spellbook etched with arcane circles, and carries a cane
- Surrounded by drifting magical pages/sigils, conveying immense arcane power
