## Overview

Goddess of Storms. Member of [[Pantheon of Gods]]. [[Aella Galefri]] worships her. In the doomed future, Michael the Evangelist claimed Veyrana had "forsaken" Aella, and threatened to strike Veyrana down.

## Relationships

- [[Aella Galefri]] — her worshipper; *(unconfirmed)* may have forsaken Aella
- [[Lanius]] — her Chosen; wields storm and wind magic; she gave him the name "Stormbringer" around the time he founded [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]
- [[Pantheon of Gods]] — member
- [[Church of the Storm]] — her church *(unconfirmed)*
