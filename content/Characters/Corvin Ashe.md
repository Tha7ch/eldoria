## Overview

Corvin Ashe is the political and financial advisor helping [[Tirian Dawnmere]] rebuild [[House Dawnmere]]. A commoner who rose entirely on merit, he previously worked in [[House St. James]]'s administration before [[Ludwig St. James]] recommended him to Tirian. Close in age to Tirian, the two have more of a peer relationship than a typical lord-and-advisor dynamic.

## Appearance

- Dark, sun-deepened skin
- Short dark brown hair, slightly curly and tousled
- Sharp, dark eyes — quick, assessing, often a little amused
- Soft facial features — a youthful, almost boyish face that undercuts how sharp he actually is
- Scrawny rather than muscular build, around Tirian's age and height
- Dresses smartly but practically — well-tailored but less ornate than noble fashion, clothes meant for someone who works for a living
- Confident bearing — the posture of someone used to proving himself in rooms full of people who underestimate him at first

## Personality

Confident, quick-witted, and competitive in a friendly way — he genuinely enjoys being good at things, not just the advancement that comes with it. Dry, understated sense of humor. Respects competence over titles, a habit picked up the hard way in noble circles that don't always share that view.

## History

Corvin grew up a commoner — sharp, restless, and good with words from a young age. [[Ludwig St. James]] recognized his talent and recruited him into [[House St. James]]'s service, where Corvin spent several years working with the family's administration on political and logistical matters — not closely with Ludwig personally, but enough to learn how the noble world actually works.

When [[Tirian Dawnmere]] went looking for an advisor to help rebuild House Dawnmere, Ludwig recommended Corvin — both because he rated Corvin's abilities, and likely because he trusted Tirian to give him more room to grow than a house the size of St. James ever could. Corvin took the opportunity: a smaller house meant his work would actually be visible and matter, and he believed in the comeback story House Dawnmere represented.

## Relationships

- [[House Dawnmere]] — political and financial advisor
- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — close in age and temperament; more of a peer relationship than employer-and-staff. Tirian respects that Corvin sees the board clearly; Corvin is invested in House Dawnmere's success because he chose to be.
- [[Ludwig St. James]] — recruited Corvin into House St. James's service and later recommended him to Tirian. "The guy who gave me my shot" — not a close personal friendship, but Corvin owes him his start.
- [[House St. James]] — former employer; worked with the family's administration
