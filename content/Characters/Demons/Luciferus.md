## Overview

Luciferus is the Demon Prince — half-demon, half-angel. He commands [[The Twelve Demon Generals]]. In the doomed future, he arrived at the final battle as the ultimate power, casually disposing of both Michael the Evangelist and the party's resistance. He referred to Null as "the end of everything."

## Appearance

- One dark demonic wing and one pale radiant angel wing on his back
- Long, wild pale-blond curly hair
- Curling black ram-like horns
- Pointed ears, pale skin, sharp elegant features
- Black and white layered robes with ornate gold embroidery, lined with gold chains and an ornate cross/star pendant
- Immense presence — forced party members to the ground simply by approaching

## Personality

Coldly theatrical. Called Solstice "Ant." Acknowledged the party's survival was longer than expected before ending them. Has a sense of grand inevitability.

## History

Commands the ten [[The Twelve Demon Generals]]. In the doomed future, orchestrated or presided over the end of the world.

## Relationships

- [[The Twelve Demon Generals]] — commands them; [[X]] and [[Kronos]] kneel before him
- [[Michael the Evangelist]] — Michael called "All heil Erebus" before Luciferus unraveled him — relationship and motives unclear

## Story

**Session 1 (Doomed Future):** Arrived from the ground (summoned via [[Kronos]]'s symbol). [[X]] and [[Kronos]] knelt before him. Dispelled all magic in the area. Spoke to Luciferus, forced Solstice, Lucerin, and Kip to the ground. Called Solstice "Ant." Said "Unravel" — Michael unraveled out of existence. Stabbed Kip through the back. Fried Aella's brain. Made way for Null. Said "here it is, the end of everything." The world went black.
