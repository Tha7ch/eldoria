## Overview

Kronos is the Demon General of Time, one of [[The Twelve Demon Generals]]. He wields time-based forbidden magic. He has a specific and unexplained connection to [[Cyris Voss]] — he sought Cyris out directly in the doomed future, and Cyris recognized him as though they had encountered each other before.
