Eerily calm and precise. Shushed Kip rather than attack him directly. Methodical in his cruelty.

## History

Demon General of Magic under [[Luciferus]]. Wields one of the forbidden magics.

Where typical magic users bend and weave the arcane, X twists and forces it to bend to his will — a violent, dominating relationship with magic itself rather than a cooperative one.

**Session 1 (Doomed Future):** Appeared alongside [[Kronos]] during the final battle. The earth elemental [[Kip]] summoned crumbled immediately in his presence.
