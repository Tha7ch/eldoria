## Overview

Manuel is the Lord Protector of [[The Kingsguard]] and the greatest knight in all of [[Brittania]]. Undefeated in single-handed combat. 65 years old, towering at 6'6. He has served the [[House Atreides]] royal family for 50 years and was a grandfather figure to all of the royal children — the constant, immovable presence in their lives. He never had children of his own, and the royal family was as close as he ever came.

## Appearance

- 6'6, imposing physical presence
- 65 years old

## Personality

Efficient and authoritative — snapped Kip's prison bars off rather than wait for a key. Warm beneath the formality; described as not knowing how to raise a child but clearly trying. Genuinely mourns that Lucerin's mother was the one who understood his powers best.

## History

The greatest knight in Brittania. Undefeated record in single-handed combat. Notable victories: Manolo Sep Cataal, Goliath Oakenshield, and The Emerald Knight [[Kylo Wisemoon]].

Manuel has been a member of the Kingsguard since he was **15 years old** — 50 years of service. He joined near the end of [[Lucerin Atreides|King Lucerin]]'s grandfather's reign. During that time he formed a close bond with [[Leo Atreides]], Lucerin's father, despite being approximately ten years older than him. Their relationship was genuinely close — more than simply knight and prince.

When Leo fathered [[Damian Grimm]] as an illegitimate son, he entrusted the boy to Manuel to raise. Damian had a deeply difficult upbringing in the castle — hated and mistreated by many around him. Manuel did his best to shield Damian from that cruelty, though he has long wondered if it was enough.

## Relationships

- [[Lumiere Ameris]] — trained her; called her "a prodigy in anything"

## Story

**Session 1 (Campaign 2):** Present at the [[King's Crown]] meeting with [[Tirian Dawnmere]]. When the argument between the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]], the Grand Arbiter, and [[Oldric Ameris]] escalated, Manuel walked Tirian out: *"It might be best if you leave before the yelling starts."* Confirmed as the most recent addition to King's Crown.

**Session 10:** After [[Damian Grimm]] learned of [[Kieran Arden|Kieran]]'s death and walked away, told the group "he doesn't take loss very well." Mentioned it would be interesting to hear [[Aella Galefri]]'s family's reaction to her reappearance. At the [[King's Crown]] meeting, was thanked by [[Tirian Dawnmere]] alongside [[Oldric Ameris]] for running the gala.

**Session 9:** Escorted the party to the King's Crown chamber. Was recently inducted into the council in place of someone unknown — he dislikes the assignment ("I hate that they made me a part of this group. It doesn't fit me. I should be by the king."). Attempted to defuse tension between the High Septon and the Warmaster. Shut down the High Septon's dismissal of Kylo ("easy, he's one of mine"). Gave Jean a small hug after the others left. Reminisced warmly with Aella about Leo and Oldric's youth — their trip to the edge of Elsweyr, Oldric reaching the Cervan Kingdom in later years. Gave Aella, Cyris, and Kip context on Aeron Velmont's family. Took the party into the crypts to brief them privately on the upcoming gala and the People's Liberation Front. Told Aella about the Dawnmere Slaughter (Tirian was away at an engagement party while his family was killed). Revealed the gala will be attended by all ten of the strongest people in Eldoria. Found Damian asleep on the floor between Elio and Iris's graves, picked him up like a sleeping child, and said "Come on, son. It's time to go."

**Session 8:** When [[Hannibal]] appeared to Solstice's group during the escape, pushed past Solstice and Ludwig to face him directly. Hannibal told him "you're not supposed to be here" and seemed genuinely unsettled by Manuel's presence — suggesting he wasn't expected to be alive or present. After Hannibal disappeared, Manuel killed the Warden in five sword strokes: slashed the Warden's Achilles tendon, then stabbed him in the head. Dragged the Warden's body away afterward. Back at the castle, led the Kingsguard in formally praising Solstice for saving the king; warned her privately that whatever is in her bag "won't leave these walls." Trauma dumped to Solstice about Damian — said Damian "thinks with his heart" and "tears his body apart," and that he worries he won't be fit for the Kingsguard much longer. In the throne room later: received Cyris and Kip; heard that Kieran is dead and that the party was essentially left as cannon fodder. Does not yet know the extent of Damian's injuries — [[Kylo Wisemoon]] is keeping it from him. Has a telepathic bond with the Kingsguard; could not reach Damian or Kylo through it after the battle.

**Session 5:** Came to the party to get the full story on Damian's interrogation. Opened up about his worry for Damian — admitted he raised Damian, never had his own children, and fears his mistakes shaped Damian's cruelty. Revealed Damian has blood magic. Also praised each Kingsguard member he trained: Lucius (understood everything), Lumiere (prodigy in anything), Kylo (best of both — magic and sorcery), Jean (great on her own accord).

**Session 2:** Led the Kingsguard to the prison to release the party. Towered over Kip comically. Snapped the prison bars off bare-handed. Delivered the party to Lucerin. Explained the royal family's death and Lucerin's situation. Paid the party 250 gold each for granting Lucerin an audience. Admitted "none of us know how to raise a child."
