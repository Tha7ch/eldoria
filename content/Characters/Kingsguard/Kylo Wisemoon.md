## Overview

Kylo Wisemoon is a Royal Protector of the Royal Family in [[The Kingsguard]]. Once a promising mage at [[Strixhaven]], fate gave him a magical blade known as Dawnstar. [[Manuel]] defeated him in combat and then took him as his only squire before inducting him into the Kingsguard. He recognized [[Cyris Voss]] from his Strixhaven reputation.

## Appearance

- 30 years old, 6'0

## Personality

Perceptive and direct. Called Lucerin an "anomaly" and explained his powers matter-of-factly to Cyris. Has Strixhaven contacts who told him about Cyris.

## History

Former Strixhaven mage with great potential. Acquired the magical blade Dawnstar under unclear circumstances. Was defeated by [[Manuel]] in single combat — one of Manuel's notable victories. Was then taken on as Manuel's only squire and inducted into the Kingsguard.

## Relationships

- [[Manuel]] — mentor; defeated him in combat and became his only pupil
- [[Cyris Voss]] — recognized him from Strixhaven associates; called him "somebody"
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — serves as his Royal Protector

## Appearance

- Large scar running from hairline to mouth over his left eye; the eye is glazed over
- Used to hide it with illusion spells while at Strixhaven; grew better at concealing it over time

## Story

**Session 7:** Attempted to cast the Wish spell to break Hannibal's cube — Hannibal silenced it. The attempt cost him enormously: his right arm is now nearly skeletal. He will not be the same for half a year. Pulled Aella and Cyris back from investigating the Abyssal residue. Delivered the campaign's largest lore drop: the origin of the Celestial Realm and the Abyss, the creation of the demon generals, and the revelation that one demon general is the source of Cyris's power and another is directly responsible for Damian. Sent a private mental message to Cyris about the restricted library and Hannibal's multiverse knowledge. Offered to bring Kip's sister to safety.

**Session 5:** Found Aella sitting outside the guild hall, distressed after Damian's interrogation. Sat with her without pushing. Shared his own trauma — his father died on a reckless quest, his mother took it out on him. Recognized Damian's anger because he saw the same look in himself. Helped Aella mount her mana crystal onto her spear using his magic. A genuine bond formed between them.

**Session 2:** Used high-level teleportation magic (blue sigils) to bring the party to the throne room. Explained Lucerin's anomalous nature to Cyris. Made clear he knew who Cyris was and considered him significant.
