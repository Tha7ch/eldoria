## Overview

Kylo Wisemoon is a Royal Protector of the Royal Family in [[The Kingsguard]] — the actual **5th Kingsguard member**, a position [[Lumiere Ameris]] was originally supposed to fill before choosing to become a general instead. He is the **#3 ranked magical genius** in the world. Once a promising **Lorehold** mage at [[Strixhaven]], fate gave him a magical blade known as Dawnstar. [[Manuel]] defeated him in combat and then took him as his only squire before inducting him into the Kingsguard. He recognized [[Cyris Voss]] from his Strixhaven reputation.

## Appearance

- 30 years old, 6'0

## Personality

Perceptive and direct. Called Lucerin an "anomaly" and explained his powers matter-of-factly to Cyris. Has Strixhaven contacts who told him about Cyris.

## History

Former Strixhaven mage with great potential. Acquired the magical blade Dawnstar under unclear circumstances. Was defeated by [[Manuel]] in single combat — one of Manuel's notable victories. Was then taken on as Manuel's only squire and inducted into the Kingsguard.

## Relationships

- [[Manuel]] — mentor; defeated him in combat and became his only pupil
- [[Cyris Voss]] — recognized him from Strixhaven associates; called him "somebody"
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — serves as his Royal Protector

## Appearance

- Large scar running from hairline to mouth over his left eye; the eye is glazed over
- Used to hide it with illusion spells while at Strixhaven; grew better at concealing it over time

## Story

**Session 10:** Brought [[Cyris Voss]] to the forbidden area of [[Strixhaven]]'s library to research [[Hannibal]]'s magic. Asked Cyris about his magic type — Cyris deflected, saying "we'll say water." Kylo didn't press: "I wouldn't get far asking you for the other." [[Karsus Morningstar]] confirmed the threat is a forbidden magic user who can nullify spells while leaving the caster with the aftershock — referencing the damage Kylo sustained from the silenced Wish spell.

**Session 9:** Escorted the party to the King's Crown chamber alongside Jean and Lucius. Purposely stayed out of the way during the meeting — Cyris noticed. Tried to step forward and speak ("If I may —") and was immediately cut off by the High Septon ("You may not. Go back to where you were, you glorified bodyguard."). Manuel defended him and the Septon allowed it with heavy sarcasm. Kylo then vouched for the party's actions during the parade, noting Solstice's idea to use the bag of holding. Afterward dismissed back to the king.

**Session 8:** Searched the city with the group for Damian — following the path of destruction. Called weakly for him. After Damian was found mangled in a crater, Kylo directed the group: Damian has to pull through on his own; Healing Word from Aella confirmed it only burns him (blood magic). Critically: told the group to keep Damian's condition from Manuel — "He'll lose his mind." Guided Kip to walk behind Seros so he didn't have to see the body. Led the group back to the castle, answered Cyris's pointed question about the lack of evacuation planning ("our oath is toward protecting the king"). At the crossroads, split the group — sent Cyris and Kip to the throne room, led Seros, Damian, and Kieran's body to the infirmary.

**Session 7:** Attempted to cast the Wish spell to break Hannibal's cube — Hannibal silenced it. The attempt cost him enormously: his right arm is now nearly skeletal. He will not be the same for half a year. Pulled Aella and Cyris back from investigating the Abyssal residue. Delivered the campaign's largest lore drop: the origin of the Celestial Realm and the Abyss, the creation of the demon generals, and the revelation that one demon general is the source of Cyris's power and another is directly responsible for Damian. Sent a private mental message to Cyris about the restricted library and Hannibal's multiverse knowledge. Offered to bring Kip's sister to safety.

**Session 5:** Found Aella sitting outside the guild hall, distressed after Damian's interrogation. Sat with her without pushing. Shared his own trauma — his father died on a reckless quest, his mother took it out on him. Recognized Damian's anger because he saw the same look in himself. Helped Aella mount her mana crystal onto her spear using his magic. A genuine bond formed between them.

**Session 2:** Used high-level teleportation magic (blue sigils) to bring the party to the throne room. Explained Lucerin's anomalous nature to Cyris. Made clear he knew who Cyris was and considered him significant.
