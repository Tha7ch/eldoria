## Overview

Jeane Howelle is the Queen's Guard in [[The Kingsguard]] — the first female member and the first Queen's Guard in the organization's history. She made her name at the Bicentennial Winter Solstice Knight's Tournament by defeating The Lion, Alastair Westmoore, in brutal single combat.

## Appearance

- 6'1, 27 years old
- Long white hair, worn loose and flowing down her back *(corrected from "high ponytail" — see [[Image Generation Guide]] reference image)*
- Cold, sharp eyes — pale and focused
- Full silver-white plate armor, worn and battle-scarred, form-fitted and practical
- Long white cape flowing from her shoulders
- Carries a longsword — elegant, clean, silver hilt
- Chain detail at the collar/neck
- Imposing and precise — the kind of person who controls every room she walks into without raising her voice

## Personality

Calm and confident. Doesn't waste words. Kicked Solstice's cell door down without drama. "Only the ones who deserve it." Respects anyone who proves their worth, but her default assumption is that commoners are weaker and less worthy — a deeply ingrained noble bias. She will not respect someone until they demonstrate strength. For example, she did not respect [[Kaisel Irvenest]] until he became a Chosen and completed several difficult missions — then she began to begrudgingly acknowledge his strength. This attitude extends to how she treats the party. She is particularly intimidated by her grandfather [[Archibald Howell|the High Septon]].

## History

Three years before Campaign 2, [[House Howelle]] hosted a gala announcing Jeane's engagement to [[Lanius]] — a surprise to most of the city, who hadn't expected the household's quiet ward to be the news of the evening. The gala took place the same night [[House Dawnmere]] was massacred. *(unconfirmed whether the engagement ultimately proceeded — see [[Tirian - One Night]] and Mysteries below)*

Made her mark at the Bicentennial Winter Solstice Knight's Tournament. Impaled The Lion, Alastair Westmoore, with her sword Oathkeeper. Shortly after was summoned to the Royal Court and assigned as first female Kingsguard member and first Queen's Guard.

## Relationships

- [[House Howelle]] — her noble house
- [[The Kingsguard]] — serves as Queen's Guard
- [[Lanius]] — grew up together; he was a ward of [[House Howelle]] and they were close. Engaged to him as of the gala the night of [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]]. She despises the [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]] and everything it stands for — but her love for Lanius himself has never wavered. She pointed [[Aella Galefri]] toward the PLFB to find him, which she couldn't officially do.
- [[Damian Grimm]] — despises him
- [[Lucas Lyons]] — can't stand him, "especially after Lanius leaving" (per [[Ludwig St. James|Ludwig]])
- [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]] — her grandmother [[Olenna Howelle]]'s husband, but not her blood relative — Olenna had Jeane's mother with a different man before marrying him. Jeane hates him.

## Story

**Session 1 (Campaign 2):** Present at the [[King's Crown]] meeting when the [[Archibald Howell|High Septon]] pressured [[Tirian Dawnmere]] to hunt the Liberation Front. Was meek throughout the Septon's speech — visibly clenching her fist but saying nothing. There is clearly some unspoken history between her and the Septon. Tirian nodded to her on his way out.

**Session 9:** Escorted the party to the King's Crown chamber alongside Lucius and Kylo. Looked almost like a kid in front of the council — Solstice noticed she appeared meek. Received a small hug from Manuel after the council members left. Went completely still when Manuel recounted the details of the Dawnmere Slaughter in the crypts.

**Session 8:** Accompanied [[Manuel]] and [[Lucius Raymor]] as part of the group escorting the king during the parade attack. Reassured the group that Damian and Kylo could handle anything. After Lucerin was secured, shot Manuel a look and offered to go check on the others — specifically calling Damian "your darling son." Went to check on Damian in the infirmary.

**Session 2:** Released [[Solstice Shuriv]] from her cell by kicking the door down. Brief, effective exchange — earned Solstice's respect with minimal words.
