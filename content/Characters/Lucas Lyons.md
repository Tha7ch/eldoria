## Overview

Lucas Lyons, known as **The Lionheart**, is the Chosen of [[Helios]], God of the Sun, and one of the ten strongest people in all of [[Eldoria]]. He is the most prominent member of [[House Lyons]], a newly formed noble house in the last century. Overbearing but means well — Manuel's assessment.

![[Lucas Lyons (4x5 Portrait).webp|350]] ![[Lucas Lyons (Reference).webp|350]]

## Appearance

- Human male, mid-20s; tall, athletic knight's build
- Golden blonde hair with vivid crimson/red streaks, medium-length and slightly wild
- Red/crimson eyes
- Fair skin
- Ornate silver full plate armor with gold filigree detailing throughout
- Thick white fur at the shoulder pauldrons
- White flowing cape with red lining
- Red leather gauntlets; a dark cross motif on one gauntlet
- Carries himself with the easy confidence of someone who knows how good he is

## Personality

Overbearing, brash, and loud — but genuinely warm beneath it. Calls Manuel "old geezer" as a term of affection. Knelt without hesitation when corrected about dismissing the party. Announced himself as "chivalrous and arrogant" without irony. Star-struck Aeron when he arrived. Comfortable treating powerful people casually — comfortable enough to call Damian "the bastard" and then immediately apologize when he remembered Manuel's feelings.

## Relationships

- [[House Lyons]] — his noble house
- [[Helios]] — his god; he is Helios's Chosen
- [[Manuel]] — calls him "old geezer"; clearly familiar; Manuel introduced him
- [[Aeron Velmont]] — knows him; threw his arm around Aeron immediately; teased him about spending time in the woods
- [[Lumiere Ameris]] — calls him "the brute"; asked Manuel if he'd arrived yet; traveled with him and witnessed demonic artifacts together
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — tension; asked about Kaisel's sister [[Elizabeth Irvenest]] and got a glass thrown at his head for it
- [[Ludwig St. James]] — fellow Chosen; Ludwig said he deserved the glass
- [[Jeane Howelle]] — she "can't stand" him (per Lucius)

## Story

**Session 1 (Campaign 2):** Attended a round-table meeting of the [[Chosen]]. Reported seeing demonic possessions and artifacts carrying demonic power from roughly 3,000 years ago while traveling with [[Lumiere Ameris]]. Asked [[Kaisel Irvenest]] about his sister [[Elizabeth Irvenest]] — got a glass thrown at his head for it. [[Ludwig St. James]] said he deserved it; Lucas apologized. Has an unnamed sister, age 19. Left alongside Lumiere after the meeting.

**Session 9:** Appeared in the rose garden, walking up between Jean and Lucius. Called the party "nobodies" before being corrected, then knelt and pledged House Lyons to their service. Asked about Lumiere's arrival. Set out toward the crypts but did not follow the group inside.
