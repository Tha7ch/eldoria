## Overview

Veyr is an information broker who operates out of a private bathhouse at the far end of [[Black Lantern Row]] in [[Caer Brittanis]]. Her name carries enough weight that even hardened criminals speak it carefully. She is the only known person to have independently investigated [[Aella Galefri]]'s disappearance to any meaningful depth — spending months exhausting every external lead before reaching a conclusion she found deeply uncomfortable.

## Appearance

- Dark red hair in a layered wolf-cut — soft, face-framing pieces falling in loose waves to just above the shoulders; tousled but deliberate, more sultry than rough
- Bright green eyes, sharp and perpetually assessing
- Freckles across her cheeks and nose
- A diagonal scar running across her forehead and through one eyebrow
- A second scar across the bridge of her nose

## Personality

Composed, sharp, and in control. Most people who walk into the bathhouse make her guards nervous; Veyr just watches to see what they'll do. She reads people quickly — too quickly for most people's comfort — and doesn't bother hiding that she's doing it.

She has a dry wit and a light teasing manner when she's comfortable, but the sharpness never fully leaves her eyes. She is not cruel. She simply understands exactly what the world is and has no interest in pretending otherwise.

Beneath the control, she is genuinely attached to her family in ways she doesn't perform for anyone. Her sister [[Mira]] was one of them. When Kaisel returned with the journal, she didn't say much — but her hands were shaking.

## History

Built her information-brokering network in [[Black Lantern Row]] over an indeterminate period. Her operation is substantial enough that she has contacts across [[Caer Brittanis]]'s entire criminal underground — traffickers, gangs, merchants, drunk nobles at parties, criminals in alleys. She has men she trusts, women she trusts more, and enough leverage over enough people to buy almost any secret in the city.

When [[Aella Galefri]] disappeared, Veyr investigated entirely on her own — not because she was hired but because a disappearance that clean, happening in front of every noble family, was a problem she couldn't leave unsolved. She spent months on it. Travel records. Guard routes. Servant gossip. Smuggling paths. Old tunnels. Hidden roads. Criminal ledgers. Every lead turned to dust.

Her conclusion: there was no external trail because there was no external actor. Whoever orchestrated Aella's disappearance was already inside [[House Galefri]].

She was still sitting with that answer when [[Kaisel Irvenest]] walked through her door looking for the same girl.

## Relationships

- [[Mira]] — younger sister; deceased; possessed and killed by a demon while Veyr's people could find no trace of her; Kaisel brought back her journal
- [[Kaisel Irvenest]] — came to her looking for Aella; she shared her findings and asked him to check on Mira; he is one of the few people she speaks to honestly
- [[Lanius]] — the only member of the [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]] she's willingly worked with; he personally approached her for information, she charged him an absurd price, and he paid without complaint
- [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]] — sees them as dangerous idealists, not heroes — another group chasing power, whose actions cause as much suffering as the systems they fight

## Story

**Kaisel's age-17 backstory:** [[Kaisel Irvenest]] found his way to Veyr through [[Kane Orso]] after demolishing the The Crooked Boar. Veyr had already heard about the Crooked Boar before he arrived — five days is a long time in her line of work. She shared everything she'd found on Aella's disappearance, concluded that someone inside [[House Galefri]] was responsible, and asked Kaisel to check on [[Mira]] in exchange for the information. Kaisel returned a few weeks later with Mira's journal and the news that she was gone. Veyr held the journal against her chest and didn't speak for a while. Then she said Mira could rest now.

**On the [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]:** Veyr views the PLFB as dangerous idealists whose actions often cause as much suffering as the people they oppose — where others see heroes or revolutionaries, she sees another group chasing power. The only time she willingly worked with them was when [[Lanius]], their leader, personally approached her for information. She charged him an absurd amount of money; he paid it without argument. Afterward, she made her position clear: *"I don't care about your revolution. I don't care about your cause. If you want information, bring money. If you don't have money, leave."*
