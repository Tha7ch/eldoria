## Overview

Lanius is the chosen of [[Veyrana]] (Goddess of Storms) and [[Indra]] (God of the Sky). He has two inherent magics — **Storm** and **Sky** — making him one of only two known people with dual inherent types (the other being [[Cyris Voss]]). He is the **first dual caster mage in over 3,000 years**, having mastered both. He fights like a brawler who uses wind to enhance his punches. More a force of nature than a magical genius — he does not have a domain, and doesn't need one. He operates mostly outside the institutional [[Church of the Storm]], with no formal connection to it. He is associated with [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]], and his location is known only through them. [[Jeane Howelle]] pointed Aella toward him, though she noted she couldn't officially do so. [[Kylo Wisemoon]] does not like him.

- Short spiky black hair — wild and energetic, sticking up in multiple directions
- Slightly pointed ears
- Sharp green-gold eyes — calm and quietly dangerous, the look of someone with nothing to prove
- Gold drop earring on one ear
- Shirtless; powerfully muscular and broad-shouldered — a brawler's body
- Extensive black tribal tattoos covering his entire chest, abs, shoulders, neck, and right arm — circular swirling patterns mixed with sharp angular marks

- Gold ornate armor/mechanical pieces at the right hip
- Black draped cloth wrapped loosely around the waist and hips, flowing to one side
- Expression: composed, unhurried, looking slightly upward — the settled confidence of someone who has already won whatever fight you're imagining

## Personality

Brawler-type. Despite founding a revolutionary movement, Lanius does not want to kill all the nobles. He lived on both sides of the divide and knows the system is the enemy, not every individual who benefits from it. His ideology is one of systemic change, not extermination. This puts him at odds with [[Roland Royce]], whose approach is far more brutal and who would burn the whole structure down if he could.

## History

Lanius grew up in **Lowtown** — the slum district of [[Caer Brittanis]]. He was adopted from there by [[House Howelle]] as a child, becoming a ward of the noble house. He saw a girl his age with unforgettable white hair near his slum home around the time of his adoption — her identity is unknown.

He was raised alongside [[Jeane Howelle]], and the two grew close. He had been quietly known within the household as a Chosen of the gods, though House Howelle never made a public show of it — to most of the city he was simply "the ward." He was considered for [[The Kingsguard]] at one point but ultimately passed over.

The night [[House Dawnmere]] was massacred, House Howelle hosted a gala announcing Lanius and Jeane's engagement — a surprise to most of the city, who hadn't expected the quiet ward to be the news of the evening. At that gala, Lanius spoke briefly with [[Tirian Dawnmere]] about House Dawnmere's commoner relief efforts (grain shipments to the river districts, sold below cost). Lanius was struck by how directly Tirian's actions matched the words "it is the duty of the strong to protect the weak" — a phrase he'd heard all his life from House Howelle without ever seeing it actually practiced. *(unconfirmed whether the engagement to Jeane ultimately proceeded — see [[Tirian - One Night]] and Mysteries below)*

After his adoption, he was raised among nobility. When he returned to Lowtown as an adult, the shock of seeing it clearly — without the nostalgia, and with the eyes of someone who had been on both sides of the divide — broke something in him. He walked the streets in the rain and felt, with total clarity, that the people he had been taught to see as weak weren't weak at all. The system that elevated some and abandoned others was corrupt, and he had unknowingly benefitted from it. Standing in the rain, he tore the sigil of [[House Howelle]] from his tunic and made a silent vow to himself. That anger became the foundation of [[The People's Liberation Front of Brittania]]. *(see [[Lanius - The Duty of the Strong]] for the full account)*

Around the time he was founding the Liberation Front, [[Veyrana]] gave him the name **Stormbringer**.

Lanius is the **first person in over 3,000 years to reach the level of a First Generation Mage** — meaning he is a dual caster with exceptional mana levels and spell strength far beyond modern standards — the ancient mages who were the first humans to ever unlock and utilize arcane power. To reach this state is to tap into the same raw magical potential those original mages wielded at the dawn of human magic. In a fight, Lanius alongside [[Roland Royce]] could take on the former top 10 strongest — and Lanius alone is SO much stronger than the old top 10 that it would be like fighting #2 and #4 simultaneously.
