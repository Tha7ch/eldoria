## Overview

Margit Thorne is the head maid of [[House Dawnmere]], running the household staff and keeping the estate presentable on a fraction of its former budget. She's the one person in the house who treats [[Tirian Dawnmere]] like a person rather than a legacy — and makes sure he actually eats and sleeps.

## Appearance

- Voluptuous, curvy build
- Dark brown hair, swept up into a loose bun with a few curling strands escaping around her face
- Warm brown eyes — kind, but quick to narrow when she's unimpressed
- Round, soft face with laugh lines
- Dark blue dress with a cream-colored apron tied at the waist, sleeves usually rolled up to the elbow
- Calloused hands, always busy with something — often planted on her hip or raised mid-point when making a point
- Carries herself with the brisk energy of someone who has run a household for decades

## Personality

Brisk, warm, and a little exasperated in the specific way of someone who has fed and clothed a household for over twenty years. Doesn't bother with the formality Osric maintains — she'll scold Tirian for skipping meals, comment on the state of his coat, and ask pointed questions about where he's been "at that hour." Genuinely loves him, in a rough, unsentimental, very maternal way.

## History

Margit came up through the House Dawnmere household staff as a girl, starting as a scullery maid under the previous head maid before working her way up over decades. She survived [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] by chance — it was her one night off in months, spent visiting her sister in the city. She returned the next morning to find the estate sealed off and half her found-family dead.

She stayed on afterward, helping [[Tirian Dawnmere]] hold together what was left of the household and rebuild it.

## Relationships

- [[House Dawnmere]] — has worked in the household for decades
- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — the one person in the house who treats him like a person rather than a lord. He lets his guard down around her more than almost anyone — partly because she'd see through a performance anyway. Makes sure he takes care of himself.
- [[Marielle Penn]] — oversees her as head maid
