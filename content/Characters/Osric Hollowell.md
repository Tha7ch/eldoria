## Overview

Osric Hollowell is the steward of [[House Dawnmere]] — the man who actually runs the estate day to day. He has served the house since before [[Sorin Dawnmere]] was its head, and is one of the last people alive who remembers the family from before [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]].

## Appearance

- Tall and thin, slightly stooped with age but still upright in bearing
- White/silver hair, neatly combed back
- Pale blue-grey eyes — observant, missing nothing
- Lined face, clean-shaven
- Dark, immaculately pressed steward's coat/suit with royal purple lapels and gold trim, white shirt and dark tie underneath
- House Dawnmere crest embroidered on the breast pocket, with "Dawnmere" stitched beneath it
- Carries an old pocket watch — a small, constant habit of checking it
- Carries himself with the quiet precision of someone who has spent a lifetime making things run smoothly without being noticed

## Personality

Formal and soft-spoken, almost unflappable — the kind of man who could announce a disaster in the same even tone he'd use to announce dinner. Beneath the composure, he is deeply sentimental. He keeps small mementos of the family he lost — nothing displayed, just kept.

## History

Osric taught a young [[Sorin Dawnmere]] to ride a horse and has served House Dawnmere in one capacity or another for decades. On the night of [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]], he was away from the estate handling house business in [[Caer Brittanis]] — a stroke of chance that spared his life and has left him carrying quiet survivor's guilt ever since.

He returned the next morning to find the estate sealed off and the family he'd served for most of his life gone. He has remained at House Dawnmere ever since, devoting himself to keeping it running and to [[Tirian Dawnmere]], the family's sole survivor.

## Relationships

- [[House Dawnmere]] — has served the house for decades; lifelong devotion
- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — the closest thing to a father figure Tirian has left; one of the only people alive who remembers him as a child. "Young master" slips out sometimes. Doesn't pry into Tirian's nighttime absences — the same way he never asked what Sorin did on his own quiet nights out.
