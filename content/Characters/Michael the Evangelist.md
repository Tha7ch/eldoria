## Overview

Michael the Evangelist is an oath-breaker paladin and warlock who worships [[Erebus]], God of Pain, Agony, and Murder. He is the primary antagonist of Session 1 (the doomed future), ultimately unraveled out of existence by [[Luciferus]]. His present-timeline status and role are unknown. Gio's character *(unconfirmed — player character or NPC?)*

## Appearance

- Short dark crimson/red spiky hair
- Glowing red eyes — sinister, burning with fanatical intensity
- Pale skin, imposing physical build
- Black and deep crimson ornate armor with jagged, spiked details — corrupted and demonic in aesthetic
- Long dark crimson/black cape flowing dramatically behind him
- Wields a massive flaming lance/spear that burns at both ends with hellfire
- Fire surrounds him constantly — he stands in it, moves through it, it moves with him
- The armor looks like it was made from something that should not exist
- His presence fills a room the way a fire fills a room — consuming

## Personality

Fanatical and theatrical. Calls himself a "messenger" spreading the word of the gospel. Genuinely believes in his mission. Showed no remorse for innocent deaths. Claims no one in life is truly innocent.

## History

*(largely unknown in present timeline)* In the doomed future, he was captured, tried, and executed by Lucerin — his head was removed. He survived *(unconfirmed — or returned to life)* and sought revenge against Lucerin, eventually fighting the party at Armageddon. Was finally "unraveled" out of existence by [[Luciferus]].

## Relationships

- [[Erebus]] — his god, whom he serves
- [[Luciferus]] — called "All heil [[Erebus]]" before Luciferus unraveled him — relationship unclear
- [[Lucerin Atreides]] — executed him; Michael called him an "imposter" and later sought revenge

## Story

**Session 1 (Doomed Future):** Chained and bloodied before Lucerin's throne, one arm a stump. Called Lucerin an imposter. Executed — taken away, then his head removed by Kieran. Lucerin kicked it away. Later appeared alive at the Armageddon battlefield seeking revenge. Fought the party. Could cast fireball ("Burn"). Was cut down by Kieran. Finally unraveled by Luciferus.
