## Overview

Marielle Penn is a young maid in the [[House Dawnmere]] household, hired after [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] as part of [[Tirian Dawnmere]]'s quiet effort to rebuild the house with people who genuinely need the work. To her, working for House Dawnmere — and for Tirian specifically — is the best thing that's ever happened to her family.

## Appearance

- Long, straight brown hair, worn loose with a white ribbon/bow pinned at the side
- Light/fair skin
- Warm brown eyes and an easy, genuine smile
- Slender build, nearly fully grown
- Maid's uniform: black long-sleeved dress with a lace collar and lace cuffs, layered under a white frilled pinafore apron
- Eager posture — always half a step too quick, like she's trying to prove she belongs

## Personality

Eager and a little clumsy, trying hard to prove herself. What started as starstruck hero-worship of Tirian — handsome, kind, famous, and genuinely nice to her on her first day in a way none of her previous employers (if any) ever were — has settled into loyal devotion. She'd walk into fire for House Dawnmere, and especially for him.

## History

Marielle comes from a poor family in [[Caer Brittanis]]. She was hired into the House Dawnmere household after the massacre, as part of Tirian's effort to rebuild the staff with people the house's reputation for being beloved by commoners could actually help. The job is a genuine lifeline for her family.

## Relationships

- [[House Dawnmere]] — recently hired, part of the rebuilt household staff
- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — idolizes him; he barely thinks about her most days, but to her he's the best thing that's ever happened to her family
- [[Margit Thorne]] — works under her as head maid
