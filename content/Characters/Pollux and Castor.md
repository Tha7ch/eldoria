## Overview

Pollux and Castor are two separate knights who serve as [[Archibald Howell|The High Septon]]'s primary guards. One is the **sun knight** and the other is the **moon knight** — together they are known as "the sun and moon knight." They stay close to him at public events and are considered a significant obstacle for anyone trying to get near the Septon undetected.
