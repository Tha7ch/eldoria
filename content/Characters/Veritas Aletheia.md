## Overview

Veritas Aletheia is the Grand Arbiter — a member of [[King's Crown]], the ruling council of [[Brittania]]. He is a wielder of **Judgement magic**, one of [[The Forbidden Magics]]. His power manifests as a scale that appears before him; what it weighs or measures is unclear. He appears disinterested and detached, and carries himself with the absolute certainty of someone who has already rendered his verdict.

## Appearance

- 42 years old
- Short silver-grey hair, lightly textured, neatly kept
- Thin wire-frame glasses
- Fair/light skin
- Calm, perpetually focused expression — not warm, not openly hostile; simply evaluating
- Wears an ornate white scholarly coat/robe covered in star and cross motifs with gold chain details throughout
- Often surrounded by books, scrolls, astronomical instruments, and the arcane scale he manifests
- Carries an air of absolute detachment — as though nothing he observes fully surprises him

## Personality

Dismissive and condescending. Called Kip "goblin" without hesitation. Rolled his eyes at the High Septon's grandstanding. Giggled when the meeting was adjourned — seemingly delighted by the awkwardness of it. His manner suggests detachment from political performance while still wielding real power. Notably, after Solstice growled at him on the way out, he simply looked down at her — no fear, no irritation. Just assessment.

## Powers

- **Judgement magic** — one of [[The Forbidden Magics]]; manifests as a scale that appears before him
- The scale appeared unprompted when he looked at the party in Session 9 — it did not move in either direction

## Story

**Session 9:** Present at the King's Crown meeting with the party. Identified Aella as "the Galefri girl." Called Kip "goblin" and was rebuffed by Aella. A scale manifested in front of him when he looked at the group — it didn't move in either direction. Noticed Solstice's grip on her bag and pressed her on its contents. After the meeting, adjourned it himself with a giggle and walked out — Solstice growled at him as he passed.
