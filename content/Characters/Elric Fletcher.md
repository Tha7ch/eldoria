## Overview

Elric Fletcher is the Archmaester — a member of [[King's Crown]], the ruling council of [[Brittania]]. He appears nervous and is notably warmer than his council peers, showing genuine concern for the party's wellbeing.

![[Elric Fletcher (4x5 Portrait).webp|350]] ![[Elric Fletcher (Reference).webp|350]]

## Appearance

- 50 years old
- Very long flowing silver-grey hair, slightly wavy, worn loose to the shoulders
- Heavy-lidded dark brown eyes, furrowed brow — carries a slightly stern expression despite his warm manner
- Short white beard at the chin
- Medium olive skin
- Wears a rich dark brown academic cloak with elaborate gold embroidery trim; an ornate blue gemstone brooch clasps it at the chest; white embroidered garment underneath

## Personality

The most outwardly compassionate member of the King's Crown seen so far. Checked on the party's injuries unprompted. The only council member to directly express sympathy for Kieran's death. Invited [[Cyris Voss]] to come visit him — a quiet, genuine offer. His nervousness during the meeting may reflect discomfort with the company or awareness of the council's darker dynamics.

## Relationships

- [[King's Crown]] — member; Archmaester
- [[Cyris Voss]] — expressed personal interest in him; invited him to visit

## Story

**Session 9:** Present at the King's Crown meeting. Checked whether the party had lingering wounds or trauma. Offered condolences for Kieran's death — the only King's Crown member to do so. As the meeting broke up, quietly invited Cyris to come see him.
