## Overview

Lucerin Atreides is the current king of [[Brittania]], despite being a child. He inherited the throne after a plot killed his entire family — he survived only because he had snuck out to watch a magic duel at [[Strixhaven]]. He possesses an anomalous primordial power: foresight that lets him see past, present, and future. He dreamed of the party before meeting them, and trusts them above everyone else.

## Appearance

- Short, slightly tousled brown hair with natural lighter highlights — messy in a deliberate way
- Fair skin
- Blue-grey eyes, heavy-lidded and thoughtful — the eyes of someone who sees more than they say
- Blue gemstone drop earring
- Elaborate white high-collared robes with intricate gold embroidery and celestial/star motifs
- Dark navy cape/mantle layered over the robes, heavily embroidered in gold with ornate patterns
- Constellation details woven into his clothing — fitting for someone whose power is tied to seeing across time
- Small for his age; the clothing carries more weight than he does, but he wears it correctly

## Personality

Carries himself with quiet, unexpected authority for a child. Polite and formal — insists on titles even when others decline them. Has a loneliness to him that occasionally surfaces. Used to be a "jolly boy" before his family's death; has held onto enough of that to keep going. Becomes briefly whimsical with Kip. Deeply burdened by his responsibility.

## History

Fourth in line for the Brittanian throne. Was gifted with foresight from a young age — his mother [[Myrcella Atreides]] was best at nurturing these powers. The entire royal family was killed in a political plot approximately one month before the campaign begins, while Lucerin was secretly away at Strixhaven watching a magic duel. His uncle [[Oldric Ameris]] — Leo's brother, born Oldric Atreides before marrying into [[House Ameris]] — was War Master of [[King's Crown]] and held a claim to the throne as Leo's brother, but stepped aside and gave it up, passing it to Lucerin. [[King's Crown]], the ruling council, is now grooming him toward their agenda. His illegitimate brother [[Damian Grimm]] serves in the [[The Kingsguard]] partly to protect him.

The [[House Atreides]] line is descended from King [[Arthur Atreides]] of the Ancient Kingdom of [[Camelot]], chosen by the Celestial Realm to lead humanity. Abnormal magic types running in the family across generations are believed to be a mark of this divine selection — Lucerin's primordial foresight is the latest expression of this.

**His siblings (all deceased):** [[Godwyn Atreides]] (First Prince, 25), [[Gideon Atreides]] (Second Prince, 23), [[Catherine Atreides]] (First Princess, ~16), [[Violet Atreides]] (Second Princess, ~8), [[Elio Atreides]] (Fourth Prince, 5, twins), [[Iris Atreides]] (Third Princess, 5, twins).

## Relationships

- [[Damian Grimm]] — illegitimate brother; loved him; Damian serves in the Kingsguard in part to protect him
- [[Oldric Ameris]] — uncle; the former Warmaster who stepped aside so Lucerin could take the throne
- [[Lumiere Ameris]] — cousin; heir to the throne if Lucerin dies
- [[Manuel]] — Lord Protector; most trusted guardian
- [[The Kingsguard]] — his five protectors
- [[King's Crown]] — the council grooming him; he does not fully trust them

## Story

**Session 2:** Met the party after the Kingsguard freed them from prison on his behalf. Showed them a vision of their future selves in a ruined throne room. Asked for their help leading Brittania.

**Session 3:** Appeared at the guild hall. Broke from formality to directly accuse [[King's Crown]] of sabotaging him and possibly being responsible for his family's deaths. Named [[House St. James]], [[House Ameris]], and [[House Howelle]] as the trustworthy houses. Confirmed his awareness of political danger — "They won't say it, so I will." Demonstrated his foresight by showing them a vision of their older selves in a ruined future throne room. Briefly healed [[Kieran Arden]]'s face marking using primordial magic. Knelt to the party when asking for their help. Asked them to aid him in leading Brittania to a better tomorrow.
