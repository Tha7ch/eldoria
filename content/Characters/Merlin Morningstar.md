## Overview

Merlin Morningstar is a 2nd-year student at [[Strixhaven]] and member of [[House Morningstar]], a noble family of powerful mages. Known as **The Prodigy of Potential**, he constantly pushes his use of the arcane to new heights no mage has ever reached before.

## Appearance

- Dark skin, sharp blue eyes
- Short, tousled black hair
- Wears a wizard hat — starry navy/purple fabric with gold trim and a large blue gem at the band, dangling star and crystal charms (more modestly sized than the classic oversized wizard hat)
- White cloak with gold star/diamond embroidery, fastened at each shoulder with round gold clasps set with blue gems, worn over dark navy/purple robes with gold star motifs
- Layered gold chain necklaces with a star-shaped pendant

- [[Cyris Voss]] — friend; Merlin showed him [[Strixhaven]]'s forbidden section, which Karsus overlooks and is fine with
