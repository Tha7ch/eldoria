## Overview

Merlin Morningstar is a 2nd-year **Prismari** student at [[Strixhaven]] and member of [[House Morningstar]], a noble family of powerful mages. Known as **The Prodigy of Potential**, he is arguably the strongest and most gifted mage alive — the #1 ranked magical genius. He constantly pushes his use of the arcane to new heights no mage has ever reached before.

**Inherent magic — Time:** Merlin's inherent magic is chronomancy — one of [[The Forbidden Magics]]. This is a closely guarded secret, known only to [[Cyris Voss]] and [[Karsus Morningstar]]. He is more naturally talented at time magic than Cyris, but Cyris is more creative and revolutionary — when Cyris invented a chronomancy spell, it stunned Merlin and drove him to be better. His and Cyris's time magic reacts extremely when they are near each other — Merlin's goes haywire around Cyris. He initially sought Cyris out partly because of this resonance, hiding the real reason. Their advisor at [[Strixhaven]] was the previous student of the previous chronomancer *(name unknown)*.

## Appearance

- Dark skin, sharp blue eyes
- Short, tousled black hair
- Wears a wizard hat — starry navy/purple fabric with gold trim and a large blue gem at the band, dangling star and crystal charms (more modestly sized than the classic oversized wizard hat)
- White cloak with gold star/diamond embroidery, fastened at each shoulder with round gold clasps set with blue gems, worn over dark navy/purple robes with gold star motifs
- Layered gold chain necklaces with a star-shaped pendant

## Personality

Most people treat Merlin with deference because of his family name — asskissing is constant, making genuine friendships rare. His bond with [[Cyris Voss]] is one of the few exceptions: Cyris never put him on a pedestal, and the friendship is real.

## Relationships

- [[House Morningstar]] — his house
- [[Karsus Morningstar]] — father; Headmaster of [[Strixhaven]], where Merlin studies; Karsus encouraged Merlin to befriend [[Cyris Voss]], and the two became genuine friends
- [[Cyris Voss]] — friend and fellow time magic wielder; Merlin showed him [[Strixhaven]]'s forbidden section, which Karsus overlooks and is fine with. Their time magic resonates violently when they are near each other. Cyris drives Merlin to be better through sheer creativity
- [[The Forbidden Magics]] — wields Time magic (chronomancy), one of the forbidden magics; not a Demon General
- [[Kronos]] — the Demon General of Time; Merlin's demonic counterpart in the mortal↔demon parallel
- [[Strixhaven]] — student
