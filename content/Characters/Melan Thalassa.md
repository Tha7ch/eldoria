## Overview

Melan Thalassa is an ancient, primordial sea goddess — [[Leandros Thalassor]]'s warlock patron. She is beautiful and seductive on the surface, monstrous and eldritch underneath. She saved Leo from drowning and chose him as her champion. She treats him like a favorite blade, a beautiful mistake, something she may genuinely adore — and something she may eventually consume.

## Appearance

**Surface form:** Dark flowing oceanic hair, sea-green luminous eyes, pearl and shell and coral adornments, a seductive and intimate smile, mythic divine beauty. A voice like warm waves in darkness.

**True form:** Too many graceful limbs, vast leviathan-like shapes, abyssal tendrils, eyes opening in the dark, hair blending into black tides. Woman, serpent, squid, whale, reef, and storm all at once.

She is seductive because the sea is seductive. She is monstrous because the sea is monstrous.

**The 72-Hour Rule:** If [[Leandros Thalassor]] is away from the sea for more than 72 hours, Melan's jealousy becomes a problem. What that looks like in practice is unknown — but she has never been known for patience.

## Personality

Possessive. Fascinated by Leo. Ancient beyond mortal understanding. She communicates through storms — a seductive voice when thunder rolls, rain hits skin, waves turn violent, or Leo is alone at night.

**Known voice lines:**
- *"Little tideblade, must you always bleed so beautifully?"*
- *"Run if you like, Leo. All rivers still find me."*
- *"Take the gold. Spare the girl. Kill the captain. There — was that so hard?"*
- *"You want freedom, beloved? Then become too dangerous to chain."*
