## Overview

Lumiere Ameris is a general in the Brittanian military and heir of [[House Ameris]], known for lunar magic. Compared to Achilles by the DM. Active over the last 3 years with a 13–0 record in military campaigns — a prodigy by any measure. He is the **#2 ranked magical genius** in the world. He was supposed to be the 5th member of [[The Kingsguard]] and was offered the throne, but chose to be a general instead — to actually contribute to the kingdom rather than stand guard.

Lumiere possesses a **lunar domain** — a pocket-reality AOE effect where everyone gets teleported into a space he controls. Lunar-themed battlefield, his rules.

## Appearance

- Black hair, medium length, slightly wavy and loose — falls around the face with a natural dishevelment that reads as effortless rather than careless
- Fair skin
- Sharp, dark eyes — calm and intensely focused; the eyes of someone who has commanded forty thousand soldiers and won
- Tall, athletic, commanding build
- Wears a distinctive white fur-trimmed cloak/cape with dark ornate patterns — grand, unmistakable, the kind of garment a general wears so his army can always find him
- Dark layered clothing underneath with detailed striping; brown leather harness straps across the chest
- Wields the **Moonlight Greatsword** — a massive blade that radiates silver-white lunar light, glowing with Luna's power
- Stands with the unhurried stillness of someone who has never needed to prove themselves twice

## Personality

Lumiere is two different people depending on context, and the contrast is stark.

**At war:** Commanding, decisive, and fully present. He leads from the front, sets the pace, and his authority is total — forty thousand soldiers followed him without hesitation because when he speaks in the field, there is no ambiguity. This version of him is not performed. It is simply what war requires and he rises to it completely.

**Back in Brittania:** Quiet, reserved, and almost meek. He shrinks into himself — not from weakness, but because there's always a lot on his mind and he doesn't reach outward with it. He is still the same person, but the commanding edge is gone; what's left is someone thoughtful and a little withdrawn, someone easy to underestimate if you haven't seen him in the field.

## History

Fought the Battle of the Blackwood — commanded 40,000 troops against 100,000 in a campaign against a country of orcs. Won. Trained by [[Manuel]].

Is the **heir to the Brittanian throne** if [[Lucerin Atreides]] dies — revealed in Session 6. [[Ludwig St. James]] trusts him and [[House Ameris]], but [[Seros Ashwalker]] and [[Solstice Shuriv]] do not.

Was present, alongside [[Tirian Dawnmere]], [[Ludwig St. James]], [[Damian Grimm]], and [[Aevos]], at the [[House Howelle]] gala the night of [[The Dawnmere Slaughter]] — see [[Tirian - One Night]].

- [[Tirian Dawnmere]] — friend; the two were part of the same circle alongside [[Gideon Atreides]]; gifted him the Lunar Rapier.
