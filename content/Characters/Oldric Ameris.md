## Overview

Oldric Ameris is the War Master of [[King's Crown]] and the High General of the Brittanian Army. His position on King's Crown makes him one of the six most powerful figures in Brittania's political structure. His control of the Brittanian Army — a third of which is made up of [[House Ameris]] and its subordinate families — gives him enormous military leverage.

Born **Oldric Atreides**, he is the younger brother of [[Leo Atreides]], uncle of [[Lucerin Atreides]], and father of [[Lumiere Ameris]]. He married into [[House Ameris]] and gave up the Atreides surname. As Warmaster, he stepped aside and surrendered his own claim to the throne after the [[Assassination of the Royal Family]], allowing his nephew Lucerin to become king.

## History

Born into [[House Atreides]] as the younger brother of [[Leo Atreides]]. At some point he married into [[House Ameris]], taking their name and giving up his birthright as an Atreides — a union that produced his son, [[Lumiere Ameris]]. He rose to become War Master of [[King's Crown]] and High General of the Brittanian Army.

Following the [[Assassination of the Royal Family]], which killed his brother Leo and Leo's entire family except for [[Lucerin Atreides]], Oldric — as Leo's brother and thus next in line — held a claim to the Brittanian throne. He chose to step aside and surrender that claim, allowing Lucerin to be crowned king instead. *(unconfirmed — why did he step aside so willingly?)*
