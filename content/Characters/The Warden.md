## Overview

The Warden is [[Hannibal]]'s enforcer. Can grow to eight feet tall in combat. Wields a flail. Was trapped alongside [[Kieran Arden]] in Hannibal's purple cube during the parade attack — nearly killed Kieran before Hannibal called him off. Disappeared with Hannibal.

## Relationships

- [[Hannibal]] — his commander; acts on Hannibal's orders
