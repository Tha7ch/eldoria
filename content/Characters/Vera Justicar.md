## Overview

Vera is the High Priestess of the [[Church of Love]] and leader of [[House Justicar]], a half-elven noble house. She is one of the few mortals who routinely converses directly with a goddess — her faith in [[Mara]] is described as unshakeable and has never wavered.

## Relationships

- [[Church of Love]] — High Priestess; leads it
- [[House Justicar]] — head of house
- [[Mara]] — her goddess; she routinely converses with her directly
- [[Vero Justicar]] — her son; travels spreading the word of Mara
- [[Ludwig St. James]] — connection present; both are tied to Mara's sphere
- [[Aella Galefri]] — Ludwig suggested Aella visit Vera; Vera knows something about Aella's family
