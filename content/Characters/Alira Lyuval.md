## Overview

Alira Lyuval is a former Thieves Guild prodigy who walked away on her own terms and became an adventurer. She uses her guild-trained skills — balance, blade work, lockpicking, stealth — but for her own chosen purposes: recovering stolen artifacts, dismantling corrupt officials, exposing criminal rings. She fights with joy.

## Appearance

- Lean, athletic, agile build
- Fair complexion with a warm, rosy glow
- Bright genuine smile, clear gray-green eyes
- Just past shoulder-length hair with bold alternating black and gold streaks, slightly tousled
- Fitted charcoal-black leather rogue jacket with ivory bodice underneath, white-gold filigree trim
- Red sash, leather utility belt with pouches, gold medallion charm at hip
- High leather boots with gold accent detailing
- Built for mobility

## Personality

Easy, unforced confidence. Rarely tense even in danger. Treats challenges like puzzles. Light teasing humor, refuses to let heavy moods linger. Sharp and perceptive beneath the lightness — always reading the room. Values independence deeply; resists control or expectation. Chooses optimism deliberately. Fights with joy, not bitterness.

## History

Raised inside The Thieves Guild from her earliest memories — not as an orphan pitied, but as an investment. Excelled at every trial: balance drills, lockpicking, silent movement, blade work. The Guild masters called her their brightest spark. As she grew, she recognized she was being shaped into an asset, not a person. Began sneaking out at night just to breathe — climbing rooftops, watching sunrises. One evening after a flawless job, she simply walked out the side entrance and didn't come back. Lived quietly under false names for years (courier, caravan guard, roadside performer), then chose adventuring deliberately — using guild skills on her own terms.

She keeps the Guild medallion at her belt not as loyalty, but as proof of distance traveled.
