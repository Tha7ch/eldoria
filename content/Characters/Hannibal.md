## Overview

Hannibal is the primary antagonist introduced in Session 7. He is not an assassin — he wants to "usher in a new era." He possesses power that is **not man-made** and appears to have Abyssal origin or connection. He can freeze the surrounding area with cold, nullify spells with a gesture, travel through realities by cutting through the fabric of space, and trap targets in translucent purple cubes that repel all attacks — even Kylo's Wish spell. He has knowledge of **alternate timelines and universes**, referring to "this universe" and "you're actually alive in this one" when speaking to Seros and Kip. He knows [[Kip]]'s sister's name.

## Appearance

- Makes the surrounding area cold simply by being present

## Personality

Theatrical and contemptuous. Views the party as a "secondary, more menial task than the king." Dismissive of religion, heroism, and the idea that the party can stop him. Not cruel for its own sake — methodical. Said he was "over the limit" when he chose to release Kieran rather than kill him outright.

## Powers

- **Reality cutting** — travels by cutting through the fabric of reality with what appears to be a knife
- **Translucent purple cube** — traps targets; repels all attacks including blood spears and Aella's Witch Bolt; psychic backlash to investigators; not a man-made spell; leaves Abyssal residue (screaming)
- **Cold aura** — makes the surrounding area cold; Kip took frost damage
- **Spell nullification** — silences spells with a "shh" gesture; snuffed Kip's spell and Kylo's Wish spell
- **Multiverse awareness** — knows about alternate timelines and versions of people; says things like "in this universe" and "you're actually alive in this one"
- **Commands the Warden** — a powerful enforcer who can grow to 8 feet tall

## History

Unknown. He is not affiliated with the known assassins — he arrived separately, killed one of the assassins himself (pointed a thumb down, they dropped dead), and made clear he has his own agenda beyond the parade attack.

- [[The Warden]] — his enforcer
- [[Kip]] — knows his sister's name (Nixi); references alternate versions of Kip across timelines
